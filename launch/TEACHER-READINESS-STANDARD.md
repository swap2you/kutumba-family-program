# Teacher Readiness Standard — Cycle 1

**Goal:** A lesson-ready teacher can teach this week's objective without inventing doctrine.

## Lesson-ready checklist

A teacher is ready only when they can:

1. State this week's **objective** in one sentence.
2. Explain the **key source** at the depth required for their band.
3. Name one **misconception / boundary** for the week.
4. Distinguish **scripture** vs **analogy**.
5. Distinguish **quotation** vs **paraphrase**.
6. Identify **no-speculation zones**.
7. Safely run the week's **activity**.
8. Answer common questions **or defer appropriately**.
9. Apply **no-shame** correction rules.
10. Know the **parent handoff** for reunification.

## Approved deferral line

> "I don't want to guess. I will verify that from Śrīla Prabhupāda's books / our source packet and come back to you."

## What this is not

- Not a credential or initiation claim  
- Not permission to invent philosophy  
- Not a substitute for local temple authority  

## Pre-week rhythm

Use `TEACHER-PRE-WEEK-CHECKLIST.md` plus each week's `teacher/PRE-WEEK-CHECKLIST.md`.

## Authority boundaries

| May | Must not |
|---|---|
| Teach from prepared packet | Invent verses or purports |
| Use approved analogies with limits | Claim science proves ātman/karma/God |
| Defer hard questions | Force children to chant or confess |
| Redirect safely | Publicly shame or rank children |

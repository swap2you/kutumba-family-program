## C1-W1 — ŚB 1.2.18

- **Devanāgarī:** नष्टप्रायेष्वभद्रेषु नित्यं भागवतसेवया । भगवत्युत्तमश्लोके भक्तिर्भवति नैष्ठिकी ॥ १८ ॥
- **IAST:** naṣṭa-prāyeṣv abhadreṣu nityaṁ bhāgavata-sevayā / bhagavaty uttama-śloke bhaktir bhavati naiṣṭhikī
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** When we regularly hear and serve the Bhāgavata (book and devotee association), troubles in the heart are cleared and steady devotion to the Lord becomes established.
- **URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/18/
- **Keywords:** hearing, service, steady-devotion
- **Teaches:** Protected weekly hearing plus association steadies family devotion.
- **Scope boundary:** Does not teach body/soul ontology (W2–W3).
- **Rights status:** Sanskrit verse text from VedaBase display; KUTUMBA teaching meaning original; no full purport; no BBT English translation copied.

## C1-W2 — BG 2.13

- **Devanāgarī:** देहिनोऽस्मिन् यथा देहे कौमारं यौवनं जरा । तथा देहान्तरप्राप्तिर्धीरस्तत्र न मुह्यति ॥ १३ ॥
- **IAST:** dehino ’smin yathā dehe kaumāraṁ yauvanaṁ jarā / tathā dehāntara-prāptir dhīras tatra na muhyati
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Just as the embodied self passes through childhood, youth, and old age in one body, the sober person is not bewildered when the self passes to another body.
- **URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/13/
- **Keywords:** body-changes, sober, continues
- **Teaches:** Body changes; conscious self continues.
- **Scope boundary:** Does not fully unfold jīva ontology (W3).
- **Rights status:** Sanskrit verse text from VedaBase display; KUTUMBA teaching meaning original; no full purport.

## C1-W3 — BG 2.20

- **Devanāgarī:** न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः । अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥ २० ॥
- **IAST:** na jāyate mriyate vā kadācin nāyaṁ bhūtvā bhavitā vā na bhūyaḥ / ajo nityaḥ śāśvato ’yaṁ purāṇo na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** The soul is never born and never dies; unborn, eternal, and everlasting — not destroyed when the body is destroyed.
- **URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/20/
- **Keywords:** eternal, unborn, not-destroyed
- **Teaches:** The jīva is eternal, conscious, individual — not the temporary body and not God Himself.
- **Scope boundary:** Does not teach human-form rarity (W4) or temporary-world shelter (W5).
- **Rights status:** Sanskrit verse text from VedaBase display; KUTUMBA teaching meaning original; no full purport.

## C1-W4 — ŚB 11.9.29

- **Devanāgarī:** लब्ध्वा सुदुर्लभमिदं बहुसम्भवान्ते मानुष्यमर्थदमनित्यमपीह धीर: । तूर्णं यतेत न पतेदनुमृत्यु यावन्नि:श्रेयसाय विषय: खलु सर्वत: स्यात् ॥ २९ ॥
- **IAST:** labdhvā su-durlabham idaṁ bahu-sambhavānte mānuṣyam artha-dam anityam apīha dhīraḥ / tūrṇaṁ yateta na pated anu-mṛtyu yāvan niḥśreyasāya viṣayaḥ khalu sarvataḥ syāt
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** After many births one obtains the rare human form — temporary yet able to give the highest purpose. A sober person should quickly endeavor for the ultimate good while this body lasts.
- **URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/11/9/29/
- **Keywords:** rare, human-form, endeavor
- **Teaches:** Human life is a rare opportunity for self-realization — protect inquiry and practice time.
- **Scope boundary:** No fear-based death pressure on children; no species contempt.
- **Rights status:** Sanskrit verse text from VedaBase display; KUTUMBA teaching meaning original; no full purport.

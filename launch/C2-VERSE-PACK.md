## C2-W1 — BG 3.9

- **Devanāgarī:** यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः । तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर ॥ ९ ॥
- **IAST:** yajñārthāt karmaṇo ’nyatra loko ’yaṁ karma-bandhanaḥ / tad-arthaṁ karma kaunteya mukta-saṅgaḥ samācara
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Work done for the satisfaction of the Lord frees us from bondage; otherwise action binds. Therefore act with detachment for His purpose.
- **URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/9/
- **Keywords:** karma, sacrifice, bondage
- **Teaches:** How action binds — and how offering transforms work.
- **Scope boundary:** Does not teach fatalism that removes responsibility (W2).
- **Rights status:** Sanskrit verse text from VedaBase display; KUTUMBA teaching meaning original.

- **Scope boundary:** Does not teach the soul has no agency.

## C2-W3 — BG 2.22

- **Devanāgarī:** वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि । तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णान्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही ॥ २२ ॥
- **IAST:** vāsāṁsi jīrṇāni yathā vihāya navāni gṛhṇāti naro ’parāṇi / tathā śarīrāṇi vihāya jīrṇāny anyāni saṁyāti navāni dehī
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** As a person puts on new garments, giving up old ones, the embodied self similarly accepts new bodies, giving up old ones — taught calmly, without fear tactics for children.
- **URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/22/
- **Keywords:** reincarnation, garments, calm
- **Teaches:** Birth and death are changes of dress for the eternal self.
- **Scope boundary:** Age-sensitive; no graphic death content; no scare pedagogy.
- **Rights status:** Sanskrit verse text from VedaBase display; KUTUMBA teaching meaning original.

## C2-W4 — BG 14.5

- **Devanāgarī:** सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणा: प्रकृतिसम्भवा: । निबध्नन्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम् ॥ ५ ॥
- **IAST:** sattvaṁ rajas tama iti guṇāḥ prakṛti-sambhavāḥ / nibadhnanti mahā-bāho dehe dehinam avyayam
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Goodness, passion, and ignorance — born of material nature — bind the eternal embodied self within the body.
- **URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/14/5/
- **Keywords:** modes, sattva, rajas, tamas
- **Teaches:** Recognize the three modes shaping mood, speech, and choices.
- **Scope boundary:** Does not teach mode-labeling as a tool to shame family members.
- **Rights status:** Sanskrit verse text from VedaBase display; KUTUMBA teaching meaning original.

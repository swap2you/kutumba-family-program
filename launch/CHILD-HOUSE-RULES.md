# Child House Rules — KUTUMBA Cycle 1

Parents remain onsite. Facilitators guide the session. Host property rules also apply.

## Shared rules (all ages)

1. Keep your body safe — feet on the floor; hands gentle.
2. Use respectful speech — no teasing, yelling, or mean labels.
3. Follow the facilitator safety cue immediately (pause / freeze / come here).
4. No jumping on furniture.
5. No indoor throwing.
6. No writing on walls, books, or furniture that are not activity materials.
7. Ask before entering private rooms.
8. Respect belongings — ask before touching what is not yours.
9. Devices away unless the lesson specifically needs them.
10. Snack only in the allowed area; water anytime with permission.
11. Help clean up before leaving.

## Correction ladder (no public shame)

1. Calm reminder  
2. Redirect / offer a choice  
3. Brief quiet reset in a visible space  
4. Parent involvement  
5. Sit out a specific activity  
6. Private parent/program conversation for repeated patterns  

**Never:** public shame, yelling, sarcasm, corporal punishment, forced chanting, or comparing children.

## Younger (K–2) emphasis

- Soft voices indoors  
- Stay with the group  
- Hands to yourself  
- Ask before leaving the room  

## Older (Grades 4–5) emphasis

- Model respectful speech for younger children  
- Devices fully away unless assigned  
- Help clean-up leadership  
- Honest questions welcome; speculation wait for source check

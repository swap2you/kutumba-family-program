# KUTUMBA Family Covenant

**Scope:** Cycle 1 founding cohort — voluntary program commitment  
**Not:** a legal contract, employment agreement, or temple membership instrument  
**Privacy:** Do not commit completed signed copies or real family names to Git

## Purpose

This covenant helps four founding families and facilitators share clear expectations for Saturday 2:00–4:00 PM KUTUMBA sessions so that hearing, practice, and mutual respect can grow safely.

## What KUTUMBA is / is not

| Is | Is not |
|---|---|
| Family sādhana and formation community | Social club or drop-off childcare |
| Protected weekly hearing + home practice | Substitute for the temple |
| Parent-onsite shared learning | Initiation pathway or certification program |
| Respectful spiritual friendship | Gossip, ranking, or public sādhana scoring |

## Family commitments

By joining Cycle 1, each founding family agrees to:

1. **Protect Saturday 2:00–4:00 PM** as a real calendar commitment.
2. **Aim for whole-family participation** (parents onsite with children).
3. **Communicate absence or lateness** before the session when possible.
4. **Arrive ready to begin** rather than expect a full restart for late arrivals.
5. **Attempt the minimum home practice** each week (usually 5–15 minutes).
6. **Respect host property** and common house rules during the program.
7. **Follow child house rules** and support facilitator redirection.
8. **Avoid gossip, comparison, and public humiliation**.
9. **Give respectful private feedback** rather than side conversations.
10. **Retain primary responsibility** for their own children.
11. **Keep private information private** (no public child ranking; no photography/recording by default without agreement).
12. **Pause or leave respectfully** if the program is not a fit.

## Attendance philosophy

Attendance is not a measure of spiritual worth. Unavoidable absence with communication is normal. Repeated patterns of lateness, absence, or non-participation lead to a private support conversation — not a public leaderboard.

## Facilitator correction authority

During KUTUMBA time, facilitators may calmly redirect behavior for safety, respect, and learning. Parents remain primary caregivers and support redirection. Host property and safety rules also apply.

## Feedback route (private)

Use the template questions: What helped? What confused you? What should we adjust? Any safety/comfort concern? Are you able to commit to the next cycle?

## Pause / exit

A family may pause or withdraw with dignity. No public explanation is required. Remaining families continue without commentary.

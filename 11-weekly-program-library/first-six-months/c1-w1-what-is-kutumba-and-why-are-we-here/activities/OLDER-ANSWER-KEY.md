# C1-W1 Older Answer Key (Teacher Only)

**Week:** What Is KUTUMBA and Why Are We Here?  
**Primary:** ŚB 1.2.18 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/18/  
**Do not distribute to students during first attempt. Not a ranking tool.**

---

## Section A — expected directions

1. **Includes:** Regular hearing of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam and service connected to the Bhāgavata — including book and devotee association (accept close paraphrases).
2. **Circle:** regularly, hear-serve (or hear/serve), steady devotion. **Cross out:** laboratory, ranking.
3. **Two halves:** Protected weekly hearing **and** home practice (path for family growth).
4. **Not “Saturday enough”:** The verse stresses *nityaṁ* / regular Bhāgavata service; event tourism without home practice is incomplete.
5. **Not “alone always holier”:** Association/service with devotees is part of the Bhāgavata-sevā frame; awkwardness is not sin.
6. **If-then cue:** Any concrete 5–15 minute plan (e.g., After dinner dishes, we say the memory line once).

## Section B — scenario directions

### Saturday-only
- Mistaken: “Attendance equals growth.”
- Response: Thank them for showing up; invite one tiny weekday cue.
- Action: Write if-then plan after dinner.
- Do not say: “You’re not serious”; public ranking.

### Home-only avoiders
- Mistaken: “Alone is always safer/holier.”
- Response: Honor home sincerity; one low-pressure association micro-step.
- Action: Arrive early once / sit near a friend / stay for snack only.
- Do not say: “You’re proud/antisocial.”

### Ranking hallway
- Mistaken: “Ranking children produces devotion.”
- Response: Compare only to your own family’s last week, privately.
- Action: Each child names one personal minimum — no scoreboard.
- Do not say: “Look how advanced that child is.”

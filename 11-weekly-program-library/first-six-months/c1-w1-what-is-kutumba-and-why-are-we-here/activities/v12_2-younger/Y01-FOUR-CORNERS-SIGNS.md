# C1-W1 · Y01 · Four Corners Signs

**Print:** US Letter · portrait · one sign per page (or cut on half-page marks)  
**Use:** Tape one sign in each corner of the room for the Four Corners game.  
**Type note:** Enlarge to 18–22 pt for wall reading; K–2 may need teacher read-aloud.

---

## PRINT TIP

Print pages 1–4. Cut on dashed lines only if using half-page wall cards. Keep full pages for floor corners.

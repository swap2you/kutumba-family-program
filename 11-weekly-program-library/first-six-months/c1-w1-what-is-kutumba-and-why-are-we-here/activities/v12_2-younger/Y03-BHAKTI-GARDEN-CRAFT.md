# C1-W1 · Y03 · Bhakti Garden Craft

**Print:** US Letter · one page per child · crayons and stickers welcome  
**Type note:** Large print · big coloring areas for K–2

---

## Flower to color

Draw or color a large flower with **four petals** and a big center circle.

| Petal | Label | Child-friendly meaning |
|-------|-------|------------------------|
| Top | **HEAR** | We listen to Kṛṣṇa-kathā. |
| Right | **CHANT** | We say Kṛṣṇa's names. |
| Bottom | **SERVE** | We help with love. |
| Left | **RESPECT** | We use kind words and care. |

**Center circle (write or glue):**

> Our family helps one another remember Kṛṣṇa.

**Bottom line:**

> This week our family will grow: _______________________________

☐ We will try this at home (child or parent may check).

Compact sketch (optional — children may ignore and draw freely):

```
              [ HEAR ]
         ________________
        /                \
 [CHANT]   OUR FAMILY     [SERVE]
        \  helps one     /
         \ another      /
          \ remember   /
           \ Kṛṣṇa.  /
            \______/
            [RESPECT]
```

## Child directions (teacher reads)

1. Color the center first.
2. Color each petal a different soft color.
3. With a grown-up, fill the blank: one small way your family will grow this week.
4. Take the garden sheet home. Keep it near your dinner place or family reading place.

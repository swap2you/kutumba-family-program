# C1-W1 · Y02 · Four Corners Scenario Cards

**Print:** US Letter · cut on thick dashed lines · 12 cards  
**Type note:** Large print for reading aloud to K–2  
**Game:** Teacher reads a card; children walk to HEAR / CHANT / SERVE / RESPECT corner.

## Teacher answer key (do not give to children)

| Card | Scenario | Corner |
|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | The family listens to a Kṛṣṇa story. | **HEAR** |
| 2 | We sing Hare Kṛṣṇa together. | **CHANT** |
| 3 | You help put the activity supplies away. | **SERVE** |
| 4 | You ask before touching a friend's toy. | **RESPECT** |
| 5 | You sit quietly while another child answers. | **RESPECT** |
| 6 | You help bring scripture to the family reading place. | **SERVE** |
| 7 | You repeat the memory line with the teacher. | **HEAR** |
| 8 | You join the mahā-mantra softly. | **CHANT** |
| 9 | You help a younger child find crayons. | **SERVE** |
| 10 | You keep feet on the floor in the host home. | **RESPECT** |
| 11 | You listen when a parent reads one verse. | **HEAR** |
| 12 | Your family chants three mahā-mantras at home. | **CHANT** |

**Teacher note:** If a child chooses a reasonable second corner, affirm the thinking, then gently guide to the best match. No ranking. No shame.

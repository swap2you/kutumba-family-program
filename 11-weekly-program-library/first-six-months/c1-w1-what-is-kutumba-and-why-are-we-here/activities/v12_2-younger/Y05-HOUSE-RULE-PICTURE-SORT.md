## Teacher answer key (do not give to children)

| Card | Scenario | Sort | Kind teacher line |
|------|----------|------|-------------------|
| A | Feet on the floor during the story | **SAFE / KIND** | "Safe body — thank you." |
| B | Jumping on the couch in the host home | **NEEDS A RESET** | "Furniture rests. Feet visit the floor." |
| C | Soft voice when asking for help | **SAFE / KIND** | "Kind words help our garden." |
| D | Snatching crayons | **NEEDS A RESET** | "Kind hands. Ask, then wait." |
| E | Freezing on the teacher cue | **SAFE / KIND** | "Freeze bodies — great listening." |
| F | Entering a private room without asking | **NEEDS A RESET** | "Ask first. Private rooms stay private." |
| G | Helping put toys away | **SAFE / KIND** | "Serving with love — thank you." |
| H | Calling a child a mean name | **NEEDS A RESET** | "Mean labels stop. Try a kind word." |

**Never:** public shame, yelling, sarcasm, ranking children, or forcing chanting as punishment.

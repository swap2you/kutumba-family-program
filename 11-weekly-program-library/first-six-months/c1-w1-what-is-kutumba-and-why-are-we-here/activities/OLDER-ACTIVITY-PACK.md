## Section A — Text observation (ŚB 1.2.18)

**IAST:** naṣṭa-prāyeṣv abhadreṣu nityaṁ bhāgavata-sevayā / bhagavaty uttama-śloke bhaktir bhavati naiṣṭhikī

**KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** When we regularly hear and serve the Bhāgavata (book and devotee association), troubles in the heart are cleared and steady devotion to the Lord becomes established.

*(Labeled KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not labeled as BBT translation.)*

**Source:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/18/

1. In your own words, what does “regularly hear and serve the Bhāgavata” include?
2. Circle three key words in the teaching meaning: regularly / hear-serve / steady devotion / laboratory / ranking. (Cross out the two that do **not** belong.)
3. Name the two halves of the week’s conclusion (hearing + _______________).
4. Why is “Coming on Saturday is enough” **not** the meaning of this verse?
5. Why is “Alone is always holier than association” incomplete for this verse?
6. Write one if-then home cue (5–15 minutes) for your family this week.

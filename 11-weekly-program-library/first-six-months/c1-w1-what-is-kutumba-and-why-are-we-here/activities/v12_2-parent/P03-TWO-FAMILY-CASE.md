## Facilitator balance notes (presenter only · do not shame)

- **A** has association and weekly rhythm but lacks home watering of the garden.  
- **B** has private practice but risks isolation from devotee association and service.  
- Repair is **rebalancing** — protect Saturday *and* a tiny home practice; keep temple/association as friendship and service, not optional “extra.”  
- Bridge to ŚB 1.2.18: regular hearing and service of the Bhāgavata (book and association) supports steady devotion.

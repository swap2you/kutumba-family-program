### Example (may read aloud · families write their own)

> After dinner on Tuesday and Thursday, we will sit for five minutes, offer one prayer, chant three mahā-mantras, and each say one appreciation. On a hard day, minimum version is one prayer and one mahā-mantra. Reminder: card by the dinner dishes.

### Week 1 home-practice standard (program)

- one prayer  
- three mahā-mantras  
- one appreciation  
- about five minutes  
- at least once before next Saturday (standard: twice)

Keep private. Minimum version counts as success.

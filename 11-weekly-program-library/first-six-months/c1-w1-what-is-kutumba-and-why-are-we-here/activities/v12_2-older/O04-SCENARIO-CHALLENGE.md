## Teacher answer key (expected directions · not ranking scores)

### Scenario 1
- **Good:** Protecting Saturday rhythm; showing up with the group.  
- **Missing:** Home watering of hearing — tiny regular practice.  
- **Next:** Add a 5-minute if-then cue (for example after dinner dishes).  
- **Principle:** Regular Bhāgavata hearing/service (ŚB 1.2.18); Saturday + home practice; KUTUMBA is not attendance-only.

### Scenario 2
- **Good:** Real home practice and sincerity.  
- **Missing:** Devotee association, temple service, and friendship that supports steady devotion.  
- **Next:** Rejoin association in a small, doable way; keep home practice.  
- **Principle:** KUTUMBA supports temple life; association matters; not a replacement temple and not isolation-as-holiness.

### Scenario 3
- **Good:** Noticing a need for care of the host home — but dignity still comes first.  
- **Missing:** Respectful correction; no public shame.  
- **Next:** Calm private reset; apologize if needed; model kind repair; continue learning.  
- **Principle:** Respectful family/host-home correction; child house rules without humiliation.

### Scenario 4
- **Good:** Honest question; teacher humility.  
- **Missing:** A verified source answer in that moment.  
- **Next:** Say “I will check the source and return”; write the question; do not invent doctrine.  
- **Principle:** Source-based learning / study readiness; KUTUMBA is not a place to invent philosophy.

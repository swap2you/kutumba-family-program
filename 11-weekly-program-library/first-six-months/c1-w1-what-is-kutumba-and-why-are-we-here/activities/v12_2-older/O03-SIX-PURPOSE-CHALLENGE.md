## Teacher answer key

| Example | Matches purpose |
|---------|-----------------|
| E1 After dinner we chant three mahā-mantras together. | **1 Home chanting / hearing** |
| E2 We help set up chairs for Saturday kīrtana at the temple. | **2 Temple service / association** |
| E3 A parent corrects a child calmly in a quiet space. | **3 Respectful family correction** |
| E4 Our family joins Janmāṣṭamī or another festival with service. | **4 Festival participation** |
| E5 Before class we open the verse link and check the source. | **5 Study readiness / source discipline** |
| E6 Siblings share clean-up jobs without arguing. | **6 Family cooperation / service** |
| E7 A parent reads one short Bhāgavata passage at home. | **1 Home chanting / hearing** |
| E8 We greet devotees and stay for association. | **2 Temple service / association** |
| E9 When someone speaks harshly, we pause and restart with respect. | **3 Respectful family correction** |
| E10 We help with prasādam distribution or festival decorating. | **4 Festival participation** |
| E11 We write notes from the lesson instead of guessing doctrine. | **5 Study readiness / source discipline** |
| E12 Everyone carries one bag and thanks the host. | **6 Family cooperation / service** |

**Extension:** Each student writes one “our family example” for the purpose they need most.

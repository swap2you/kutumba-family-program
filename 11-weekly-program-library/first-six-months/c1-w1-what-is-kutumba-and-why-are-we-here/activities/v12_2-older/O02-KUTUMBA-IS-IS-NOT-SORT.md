## Teacher answer key

### KUTUMBA IS
1. Family formation — parents and children learning together.  
2. Supports temple life and devotee association.  
3. Parents participate and remain onsite on Saturday.  
4. Small home practice that waters what we hear.  
5. Respectful correction — calm, private when needed.  
6. Source-based learning from śāstra and approved guides.

### KUTUMBA IS NOT
1. Drop-off babysitting while parents leave the program.  
2. A replacement for temple worship and community.  
3. Competition to see who is “more spiritual.”  
4. A public sādhana leaderboard.  
5. Initiation or temple certification.  
6. A place to invent philosophy without sources.

**Discussion line:** Why does “supports temple life” matter for ŚB 1.2.18’s call to regular Bhāgavata service and association?

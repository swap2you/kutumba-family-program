### Compass directions (copy onto poster if redrawing)

| Direction | Word | One sentence |
|-----------|------|--------------|
| North | **HEAR** | We receive Kṛṣṇa-kathā regularly. |
| West | **PRACTICE** | We water hearing with a tiny home practice. |
| East | **SERVE** | We help with love at home and in service. |
| South | **ASSOCIATE** | We grow with devotees — not alone. |

**Center:** How should our family grow?

**Verse bridge (teacher may say):** ŚB 1.2.18 points to regular hearing and service of the Bhāgavata — book and association — so devotion can become steady.

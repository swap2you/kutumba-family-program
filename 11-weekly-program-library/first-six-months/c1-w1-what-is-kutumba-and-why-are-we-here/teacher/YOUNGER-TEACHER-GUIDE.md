# C1-W1 Younger Teacher Guide (K–2)

**Week:** What Is KUTUMBA and Why Are We Here?  
**Primary:** ŚB 1.2.18 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/18/  
**Session length:** 40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)  
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10  
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

---

## Objective

Children experience that **regular hearing** and a tiny **home practice** belong together. They plant a “Hear → Practice” seed card and take home one watering cue for the week.

## Exact memory phrase

> Regular hearing and service to Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam steadies our family's devotion.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** When we regularly hear and serve the Bhāgavata (book and devotee association), troubles in the heart are cleared and steady devotion to the Lord becomes established.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** When our family hears about Kṛṣṇa again and again, and does one kind practice at home, our hearts can grow steady — like a garden that is watered every week.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**  
  - “Coming on Saturday is enough; we can skip home.”  
  - “Only big kids / only temple people hear.”
- **Do not:** invent sage dialogue; use fear; rank who “hears better”; claim science proves devotion.

## Materials

- Small clear cup or pot with a paper “seed” labeled **Hear → Practice** (demo object)
- Card stock for garden watering cards (one per child)
- Blue crayon / marker for “water drops”
- Soft toss object (backup game)
- Printed `visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` if available (US Letter)
- Crayons; tape; name stickers
- Calm-corner chair visible to room

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; garden demo cup at center. Keep aisles clear for Freeze-and-Remember walking.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome by name; feet on floor; hands in lap.
2. Show the seed cup: “Hear” on one side of the seed, “Practice” on the other.
3. Echo memory phrase twice, clap rhythm: *Regular hearing / and service / steadies our devotion.*
4. Ask: “Who heard a Kṛṣṇa story this week?” Accept nods; no forced stories.

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Long ago, wise people gathered in a peaceful place to hear about the Lord and ask how to live well. They did not come only once and forget. They came ready to listen carefully.  
> We are not those forest sages — but we can borrow their *habit*: set a protected time, hear together, and take one small practice home.  
> Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam teaches that when we regularly hear and serve — the book and the friendship of devotees — the heart can become steadier in devotion to the Lord.  
> A garden grows when someone waters it again and again. One splash and then forgetting is not enough. Saturday hearing *and* a tiny home practice belong together.

**Story boundaries:** Paraphrase from ŚB 1.1–1.2 assembly pattern and ŚB 1.2.18 themes only. No invented quotes. No fear. No claiming our room equals Naimiṣāraṇya.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. What do we do on Saturday that helps our hearts? (**Hear** together)
2. What tiny thing can we do at home so the garden keeps growing? (**Practice**)
3. Who can we serve with kindness this week? (name one person)

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Freeze and Water (steps)

1. Children walk gently in a circle (or march in place).
2. Teacher says **Freeze!** — everyone stops.
3. Teacher says the first half: “Regular hearing and service…” — children finish: “…steadies our family's devotion.”
4. Teacher says **Water!** — children pretend to pour two gentle drops (tap one finger twice) toward the center cup.
5. Play three rounds. On round three, after Freeze, show two picture cards: (A) only Saturday, then empty week; (B) Saturday + tiny home practice. Children point to B.
6. End seated; echo memory phrase once.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Garden watering card (steps)

1. Give each child a folded card-stock “garden card.”
2. Outside: draw a small plant or sprout (model once).
3. Inside, teacher or helper writes: **Regular hearing and service to Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam steadies our family's devotion.** (Or sticker the short line if literacy is limited: *Hear + Practice steadies us.*)
4. Draw five blank water-drop circles on the back.
5. Practice once: child “waters” drop 1 by tracing blue while whispering the memory phrase.
6. Tell them: each home practice this week, color one drop.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet (`visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg`). If short on time, skip coloring and keep the garden card as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind words** — “Garden voices are soft.”
- **Toy rests** — “Toy takes a nap on the shelf.”
- **Quiet reset** — one minute in calm corner holding the seed cup picture; rejoin on next Freeze.

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Crayons in bin; cards stacked; demo cup away.
2. Each child holds garden watering card.
3. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:  
   > Memory phrase: “Regular hearing and service to Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam steadies our family's devotion.” Home cue: After dinner dishes, say the phrase once and color one water drop.
4. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Running | “Freeze bodies — garden walk.” | Freeze, then walk |
| Snatching | “Kind hands water the garden.” | Hands to lap |
| Loud shout | “Soft garden voice.” | Soft repeat |
| Shutdown | “Quiet reset is okay.” | Calm corner 60 sec |

## Backup low-prep (if craft fails or energy crashes)

1. Sit in a circle with soft toss object.
2. Child catches, says one word: “hear,” “practice,” “kind,” or “Kṛṣṇa,” then tosses to next.
3. End with three echoes of the memory phrase.
4. Optional: pass the seed cup; each child “waters” it once with a finger tap.

## What success looks like

- Child can point to Hear and Practice on the seed.
- Child can echo (or finish) the memory phrase.
- Child can name one home watering moment.

## Boundaries

- Snack/water only happens in main schedule (3:30), not as a track bribe.
- No weekly meal language.
- No fabricated approval stickers implying temple certification.
- Privacy: no forced confession of “we never practice at home.”

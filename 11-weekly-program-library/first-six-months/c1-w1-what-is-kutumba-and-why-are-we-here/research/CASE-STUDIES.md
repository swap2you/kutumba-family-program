# C1-W1 Case Studies

### Constructed case 1 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** A family never misses Saturday 2–4, but weekdays have zero hearing, memory line, or gratitude act.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Attendance alone equals spiritual growth.
- **Relevant principle / source:** ŚB 1.2.18 — nityaṁ bhāgavata-sevayā: regularity of hearing/service, not event tourism. — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/18/
- **Compassionate response:** Thank them for showing up. Affirm that Saturday is precious. Then invite one tiny home cue (same chair, same five minutes).
- **Family action:** Write a 5-minute if-then plan: After dinner dishes, we say the memory line once.
- **What not to say:** You're not serious. Other families are more advanced. Coming here is worthless without perfection.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

### Constructed case 2 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** A family chants at home but withdraws from association because community feels awkward.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Alone is always safer and more spiritual than community.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Hearing in association (Bhāgavata and devotees) is part of the verse's seva frame; friendship supports steadiness. — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/18/
- **Compassionate response:** Honor their home sincerity. Name one low-pressure shared moment (arrive early, sit near a friend, stay for snack only).
- **Family action:** Choose one association micro-step this month — not a full social overhaul.
- **What not to say:** You're proud / antisocial. Real devotees never feel awkward.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

### Constructed case 3 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** A parent compares children's seriousness in front of other families.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Ranking children produces devotion.
- **Relevant principle / source:** KUTUMBA charter: no ranking; growth is personal and private where needed. — Program charter / privacy practice (pedagogy)
- **Compassionate response:** Redirect privately: compare only to your own family's last week, never to another child.
- **Family action:** Each child names one personal minimum practice — no public scoreboard.
- **What not to say:** Look how advanced that child is. Shame your kid into chanting.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

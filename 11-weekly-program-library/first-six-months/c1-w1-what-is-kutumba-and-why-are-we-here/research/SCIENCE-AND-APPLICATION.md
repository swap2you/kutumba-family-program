# C1-W1 Science and Application

## Empirical aids for habit design (not doctrinal proof)

Use these only as **habit-design helpers**. They do not prove śāstra, devotion, or Bhāgavata efficacy.

### Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement (2006)

- **Full title:** Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement: A Meta-analysis of Effects and Processes
- **Authors:** Gollwitzer, P. M., & Sheeran, P.
- **Venue:** *Advances in Experimental Social Psychology*, Vol. 38, pp. 69–119
- **DOI (working):** https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1
- **Also:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0065260106380021
- **Finding used:** If-then plans increase follow-through on intended actions (medium-to-large effect across many tests).
- **Limitation:** Does not prove devotion or heart-cleansing; helps design saṅkalpa cues only.
- **Application:** "After dinner dishes, we say the memory line."
- **Takeaway:** Pair a tiny home practice with a clear *when/where* cue so the family does not rely on vague good intentions.
- **What's the catch:** Plans help *starting and sticking*; they do not create spiritual authority, purify the heart, or replace śāstra/sādhu/guru.

### Family Routines and Rituals (2002)

- **Full title:** A Review of 50 Years of Research on Naturally Occurring Family Routines and Rituals: Cause for Celebration?
- **Authors:** Fiese, B. H., Tomcho, T. J., Douglas, M., Josephs, K., Poltrock, S., & Baker, T.
- **Venue:** *Journal of Family Psychology*, 2002, Vol. 16, No. 4, pp. 381–390
- **DOI (working):** https://doi.org/10.1037/0893-3200.16.4.381
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12561283/
- **Finding used:** Predictable family routines/rituals associate with child adjustment, belonging, and healthier family functioning in the reviewed literature.
- **Limitation:** Does not prove spiritual efficacy of Bhāgavata hearing.
- **Application:** Protected Saturday + tiny home ritual language.
- **Takeaway:** A stable weekly gathering plus a small repeated home cue can support belonging and follow-through as *family design*, not as proof of doctrine.
- **What's the catch:** This is a qualitative research review with method limits (mixed measures, convenience samples). Association ≠ causation, and secular “ritual” ≠ Kṛṣṇa-centered sādhana. Keep śāstra as the authority; use the study only to defend *why a protected rhythm is wise*.

> **Citation note:** An earlier draft used DOI `10.1111/1467-8624.t01-1-00525` (Child Development pattern). That ID does **not** belong to this Fiese review. The correct 2002 paper is the *Journal of Family Psychology* entry above.

# C3-W3 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** Guru, Sādhu and Śāstra: How We Receive Spiritual Knowledge
**Primary:** BG 4.34 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/34/
**Memory phrase:** Ask, serve, verify.

---

## Objective

Children practice asking kindly, helping as service, and “checking the book,” and echo Ask, serve, verify.

## Core activity — Three-Door Walk

**Time:** 5–8 minutes

1. Three mats: Ask, Serve, Verify.
2. Walk each; do a gesture (hand raise; helping hands; open-book hands).
3. Two rounds; sit; phrase.

## Story boundary

No naming living gurus for recruitment. No fear stories. No invented quotes.

## Craft — Three-door reminder card (step-by-step)

1. Fold card with three doors drawn.
2. Color; write phrase.
3. Back: draw a book check.

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` on US Letter if present. If missing, children color their craft only.

## Backup (low prep)

Toss and say Ask or Verify.

## Materials

- Three door cards
- Picture “book check”
- Question puppets
- Crayons
- Soft toss
- Calm corner
- Phrase stickers

## Parent note (say at handoff)

Home practice: Write one sincere spiritual question and locate one relevant Śrīla Prabhupāda source.. Snack/water only at the Saturday program; no weekly meal. We do not rank children.

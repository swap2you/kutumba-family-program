## Objective

Students observe BG 4.34, practice source verification, and state KUTUMBA’s non-initiation boundary clearly.

## Exact memory phrase

> Approach spiritual truth with humility, sincere inquiry and service; realized teachers can impart knowledge.

**Short echo:** Ask, serve, verify.

## Teacher background

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Learn the truth by approaching a realized teacher with humility, sincere inquiry, and service — such seers of truth can impart knowledge. (Label as KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not as BBT translation.)
- **Supporting frames (one sentence each):** Learner humility; guru-sādhu-śāstra verification principle; program boundary.
- **Block:** Personality cult / gullibility.; Claiming KUTUMBA confers initiation authority.
- **Scope:** No recruitment; no declaring who is pure devotee; no dīkṣā roleplay.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

Online rule with no source — what do we do first?

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation

Underline praṇipātena / paripraśnena / sevayā / tattva-darśinaḥ. Discuss each.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A of the older activity pack. Teacher circulates; use `OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md` for expected directions (not for grading ranks).

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

A Initiation confusion B Viral rule C Personality cult

Share one insight per group (60 seconds).

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Cult or Inquire

C vs I claims; explain with BG 4.34.

### 33–37 min — Diagram / map task

Three-door diagram with verification arrow to śāstra.

### 37–40 min — Project sentence + handoff

Question + source card to parent.

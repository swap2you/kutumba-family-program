## Objective

Children practice asking kindly, helping as service, and “checking the book,” and echo Ask, serve, verify.

## Exact memory phrase

> Ask, serve, verify.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** Learn the truth by approaching a realized teacher with humility, sincere inquiry, and service — such seers of truth can impart knowledge.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** We learn about Kṛṣṇa by asking politely, helping, and checking trusted books. KUTUMBA helpers teach; they do not give initiation vows.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**
  - “Believe every video.”
  - “Our class helper is my initiating guru.”
- **Do not:** invent deity dialogue; force confession; rank children; use scare tactics; claim temple certification stickers.

## Materials

- Three door cards
- Picture “book check”
- Question puppets
- Crayons
- Soft toss
- Calm corner
- Phrase stickers

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; calm corner with one chair for quiet reset. Keep aisles clear for movement.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome.
2. Echo: *Ask, / serve, / verify.*
3. Show three doors.
4. Say: “Helpers teach; big vows are different.”

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Kṛṣṇa teaches us to learn by bowing our pride, asking sincere questions, and serving.
> Wise teachers who see the truth can give knowledge.
> If a video says something scary, we pause and check a trusted book.
> Our Saturday helpers teach families. They do not give initiation.

**Story boundaries:** No naming living gurus for recruitment. No fear stories. No invented quotes.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. What are the three words? (Ask, serve, verify)
2. Do we believe every video? (No)
3. Does class give initiation? (No)

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Three-Door Walk (steps)

1. Three mats: Ask, Serve, Verify.
2. Walk each; do a gesture (hand raise; helping hands; open-book hands).
3. Two rounds; sit; phrase.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Three-door reminder card (steps)

1. Fold card with three doors drawn.
2. Color; write phrase.
3. Back: draw a book check.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet if available. If short on time, keep the craft as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind hands** — “Hands help; toys rest.”
- **Loud shout** — “Soft voice, please.”
- **Quiet reset** — “Calm corner for sixty seconds.”

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Materials away; craft in hand.
2. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:
   > Memory phrase: “Ask, serve, verify..” Home cue: Write one sincere spiritual question and locate one relevant Śrīla Prabhupāda source.
3. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

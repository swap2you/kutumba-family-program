## Core explanation (facilitator must say)

Spiritual knowledge is received through humility, sincere inquiry, and service to those who see the truth (BG 4.34). Families practice forming honest questions and verifying with Śrīla Prabhupāda’s books on VedaBase. KUTUMBA supports learning; it does not claim initiation authority. Personality cult and gullibility are blocked alongside cynical refusal to ask.

**Conclusion line:** Ask, serve, verify — and do not invent KUTUMBA initiation powers.

**Common errors to block:**
1. Personality cult / gullibility.
2. Claiming KUTUMBA confers initiation authority.

## Analogies / examples with limits (teach inline)

### 1) Three doors
- **Say:** Ask, serve, and verify are three doors into knowledge. Skipping ask becomes silence or pride; skipping serve becomes extractive curiosity; skipping verify becomes gullibility.
- **Limit:** Pedagogy; not a verse quote.
- **Younger:** Three door cards to walk through.
- **Older/adult:** Which door was skipped in a sample mistake?
### 2) Map check with three witnesses
- **Say:** Guru, sādhu, and śāstra function like three witnesses that keep a traveler from following a charming stranger off a cliff.
- **Limit:** Do not pretend KUTUMBA facilitators are automatically those witnesses.
- **Younger:** Check the book picture before believing a rumor.
- **Older/adult:** Write a claim and a verification step.
### 3) School guide ≠ graduation authority
- **Say:** A helpful class guide can teach diligently without issuing diplomas. KUTUMBA guides learning without claiming dīkṣā authority.
- **Limit:** Keep tone respectful to real guru-tattva; do not mock aspirants.
- **Younger:** Teacher helps us learn; big vows are different.
- **Older/adult:** Boundary sentence drill.

## Three constructed household cases (week-specific)

### Constructed case 1 — Facilitator as “my guru now”
- **Situation:** A fictional newcomer tells others the Saturday facilitator has “given initiation vibes” and should control their vows.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** KUTUMBA teaching role = dīkṣā authority.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 4.34 + explicit program boundary.
- **Compassionate response:** Thank eagerness; clarify roles; point to śāstra study and proper temple/guru pathways outside KUTUMBA’s claim scope.
- **Family action:** Write boundary sentence on family card.
- **What not to say:** Mocking the newcomer. Pretending facilitators initiate.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — “helpers teach; big vows are different.” Older — rewrite claim.
### Constructed case 2 — Viral spiritual rule
- **Situation:** A parent forwards a dramatic online rule with no śāstra citation and demands family compliance tonight.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Gullibility = faith.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Ask, serve, verify; locate Prabhupāda source.
- **Compassionate response:** Pause; form a question; search VedaBase path; defer fear tactics.
- **Family action:** Home minimum: question + one source URL/ref.
- **What not to say:** Public shaming for having shared the clip.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — check the book picture. Older — verification worksheet.
### Constructed case 3 — Personality cult around a charming speaker
- **Situation:** A teen treats a charismatic speaker as beyond question and insults anyone who asks for a verse.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Charisma replaces śāstra.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 4.34 inquiry; verification culture.
- **Compassionate response:** Affirm respect; restore permission to ask; require a source step.
- **Family action:** Practice one sincere question without snark.
- **What not to say:** Destroying respect for all teachers. Ranking devotees.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — kind ask practice. Older — cult vs inquire sort.

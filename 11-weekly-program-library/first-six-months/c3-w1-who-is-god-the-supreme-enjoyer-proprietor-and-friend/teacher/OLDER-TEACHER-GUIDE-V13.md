## Objective

Students observe BG 5.29, explain the three peace-giving features, apply stewardship vs neglect, and write one duty remembrance.

## Exact memory phrase

**Short echo:** Kṛṣṇa owns, enjoys, befriends.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

1. Welcome.
2. Hook: “Can knowing who God is change a fight about credit?”
3. Essential question aloud.

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation

1. Read IAST together.
2. Underline bhoktāram / maheśvaram / suhṛdam / śāntim.
3. Observation: What result follows knowing?
4. One-sentence bridges to Īśo 1 and BG 9.26.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A of the older activity pack. Teacher circulates; use `OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md` for expected directions (not for grading ranks).

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

**A Credit fight** **B Grab** **C Careless slogan** — mistaken, response, what not to say.

Share one insight per group (60 seconds).

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Owner or Steward

Claims sorted O (ultimate-owner error) vs S (stewardship true to 5.29). Three rounds.

### 33–37 min — Diagram / map task

Peace triangle: label corners; write one conflict reframed.

### 37–40 min — Project sentence + handoff

Duty + three-line remembrance to parent at 3:10.

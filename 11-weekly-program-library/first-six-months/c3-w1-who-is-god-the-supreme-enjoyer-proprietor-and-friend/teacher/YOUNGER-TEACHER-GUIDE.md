# C3-W1 Younger Teacher Guide (K–2)

**Week:** Who Is God? The Supreme Enjoyer, Proprietor and Friend
**Primary:** BG 5.29 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/5/29/
**Session length:** 40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

---

## Objective

Children learn Kṛṣṇa owns, enjoys, and befriends; practice shared care of a basket; offer a simple drawing/flower in offering mood.

## Exact memory phrase

> Kṛṣṇa owns, enjoys, befriends.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** Knowing Kṛṣṇa as the supreme enjoyer of sacrifice and austerity, the Lord of all worlds, and the friend of all beings, one attains peace.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** Kṛṣṇa owns everything. He enjoys loving gifts. He is everyone’s friend. Knowing this brings peace.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**
  - “It is only mine forever.”
  - “If Kṛṣṇa owns it I can be careless.”
- **Do not:** invent deity dialogue; force confession; rank children; use scare tactics; claim temple certification stickers.

## Materials

- Shared basket
- Three picture cards
- Paper flowers / crayons
- Soft puppet
- Memory phrase stickers
- Soft toss object
- Calm-corner chair

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; calm corner with one chair for quiet reset. Keep aisles clear for movement.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome; feet on floor.
2. Echo: *Kṛṣṇa owns, / enjoys, / befriends.*
3. Show three pictures once.
4. Practice: “We care because Kṛṣṇa owns.”

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Sometimes we say “mine!” and feel hot inside.
> Kṛṣṇa teaches that He is the big Owner, the One who enjoys our loving gifts, and the Friend of every living being.
> When we remember that, we can share and care — and feel more peaceful.
> We do not throw things away carelessly. Because Kṛṣṇa owns them, we take care.

**Story boundaries:** Paraphrase BG 5.29 themes only. No invented deity quotes. No ritual inventions.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. Who owns everything? (Kṛṣṇa)
2. Is Kṛṣṇa our friend? (Yes)
3. If He owns it, do we care more or less? (More)

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Owns–Enjoys–Friend Walk (steps)

1. Three mats labeled with pictures.
2. Children walk gently Owns → Enjoys → Friend while saying the phrase.
3. At Friend mat, hand-to-heart.
4. Return one basket item to the center (stewardship).
5. Two rounds; sit; echo phrase.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Peace triangle card (steps)

1. Triangle card with three corners pre-marked.
2. Child colors corners; helper writes phrase at center.
3. Back: draw one thing to care for at home.
4. Practice pointing to each corner.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet if available. If short on time, keep the craft as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind hands** — “Hands help; toys rest.”
- **Loud shout** — “Soft voice, please.”
- **Quiet reset** — “Calm corner for sixty seconds.”

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Materials away; craft in hand.
2. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:
   > Memory phrase: “Kṛṣṇa owns, enjoys, befriends..” Home cue: Before one daily duty, remember: Kṛṣṇa owns, Kṛṣṇa enjoys, Kṛṣṇa is friend.
3. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Running | Walking feet. | Walk to mat |
| Snatching | Kind hands. | Hands to lap |
| Interrupt shout | Soft voice. | Whisper retry |
| Shutdown | Quiet reset is okay. | Calm corner 60 sec |

## Backup low-prep (if craft fails or energy crashes)

Soft toss: catch and say “Kṛṣṇa owns” or “Kṛṣṇa is friend.”

## What success looks like

- Echoes phrase
- Returns one item to basket
- Says we care because Kṛṣṇa owns

## Boundaries

- No weekly meal language.
- No fabricated approval stickers implying temple certification.
- Privacy: no forced confession.

# C2-W6 Younger Teacher Guide (K–2) — 40-minute executable lesson

**Track window:** approx. 2:30–3:10 (40 minutes). Parents onsite elsewhere; reunite at 3:10.

## Objective

Children can say and show: **We can choose a helpful tool when the morning feels rushed** — and remember that Kṛṣṇa helps our family practice kindness.

## Exact memory phrase

> Knowledge should lead to thoughtful, responsible choice under Kṛṣṇa’s guidance.

*(Age-adapted echo if needed: “We choose a helpful tool with Kṛṣṇa.”)*

## Teacher background (2 minutes, before children arrive)

- Primary center: BG 18.63 — deliberate, then choose; responsibility remains (https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/63/).
- Tonight is **integration review** — no new major doctrine; retrieve Cycle 2 tools gently.
- Block: ranking children; shaming “slow” kids; mode-labeling (“you’re lazy / tamasic”); fatalism (“nothing can change”); victim-blaming.
- Puppet story = fictional rushed morning. No gossip about real families.
- Snack later with whole group only; water okay if needed.

## Minute-by-minute lesson

| Minutes | Block | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Welcome + seated circle | Soft voice; “kind words only”; show five tool cards face-down |
| 3–8 | Memory phrase echo | You say half; they finish; two joyful rounds max |
| 8–16 | Puppet story | Rushed morning puppet script below; ask one wonder question |
| 16–23 | Movement game | Traffic-Light Pause (rules below) |
| 23–33 | Hands-on + craft | Tool-card choice + take-home card |
| 33–37 | Coloring | `visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` |

## Age-appropriate story script (5–8 min) — Puppet rushed morning

Puppet Lāla is late. Socks are missing. A screen is still on. The puppet wants to blame “the clock” and “everybody else.”

Teacher (gentle): “Let’s look for a helpful tool.”

Show tool cards one at a time (simple pictures, few words):

1. **Pause light** — stop, breathe, choose.  
2. **Basket** — devices rest in a basket.  
3. **Snack ready** — water/fruit prepared earlier.  
4. **Kind words** — “How can I help?” instead of blame.  
5. **Remember Kṛṣṇa** — soft name or short prayer before leaving.

Children help the puppet pick **one** tool. The morning becomes a little kinder. Nobody wins a prize for being “best.” We practice together.

Rules: no invented deity dialogue; no frightening detail; no ranking; no calling anyone “bad mode.” Stop at the moral: when life feels rushed, we choose a helpful tool and remember Kṛṣṇa.

## Three wonder questions

1. Which tool helped the puppet most?
2. Whom can we help with kind words this week?
3. When could our family use one tool before we leave the house?

## Movement game (5–7 min) — Traffic-Light Pause — C2-W6

1. Children walk gently in a circle.
2. You say **Red** — freeze (pause).
3. You say **Yellow** — hand on heart (think of a kind choice).
4. You say **Green** — take one calm step and finish the memory phrase.
5. Two rounds only; then sit. Redirect: “Feet on the floor. Kind words only. Soft bodies.”

## Hands-on object lesson (8–10 min) — Tool cards

**Object:** Five simple tool cards (pause / basket / snack / kind words / remember Kṛṣṇa).  
Steps:
1. Teacher names each card once.
2. Retell one rushed moment from the puppet story.
3. Each child chooses **one** card to “take home in the heart” (and may take a paper copy).
4. Secure ending: “One tool is enough. We do not rank tools or children.”

Never: score who picked the “best” card. Never: “You’re always the rushed kid.”

## Craft (8–10 min)

**Tool-card take-home:** Fold a card. Outside: draw a traffic light or basket. Inside: write/sticker the memory phrase (or short echo). Optional: glue a small colored circle (red/yellow/green).

## Coloring / line-art

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg`. Title the page with C2-W6. If unclear, children draw a calm morning helper tool — no scary faces, no blame posters.

## Sanskrit exposure (optional, 1 min)

Softly invite hearing one friendly word: **Kṛṣṇa** or **Hare** — never forced volume. Optional recognition that grown-ups are reviewing a verse about choosing carefully (BG 18.63) — no long Sanskrit drill.

## Behavior redirects

- “Feet on the floor.”
- “Kind words only.”
- “That toy rests for now.”
- “We help; we do not blame.”
- Quiet reset in visible space if needed (parents onsite).

## Backup low-prep (if craft or cards fail)

Sit in a circle; pass a soft scarf; each child names one kind helping word; end with memory phrase echo twice.

## Extension

Helper role: hand out crayons / collect papers / hold the “pause light” card for the group.

## Materials

Printed line art; crayons; memory cards; five tool cards; soft scarf; card stock; tape; red/yellow/green circles optional; puppet or sock puppet.

## Cleanup / handoff

3 minutes cleanup. At door: tell parent the memory phrase and: “Please continue **one** Cycle 2 tool during the off week — even once counts.”

## Home-practice explanation to parents

“Please say the memory phrase once and reuse the child’s chosen tool card (pause, basket, snack ready, kind words, or remember Kṛṣṇa) at least once this off week. No ranking. No mode labels on children.”

# C2-W6 Older Teacher Guide (Grades 4–5) — full track lesson

**Track window:** approx. 2:30–3:10 (40 minutes).

## Objective

Students **facilitate** a household case with the **five-lens method**, synthesize Cycle 2 (action, choice, continuity, modes, shelter), and reject ranking, fatalism, mode-labeling, and victim-blaming.

## Essential question

Can we synthesize choice and modes?

## Text-observation task (10 min)

Open https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/63/. Students write:

1. Who is speaking / what is the teaching setting (Kṛṣṇa to Arjuna — knowledge given; choice remains)?
2. One phrase that signals deliberation before action.
3. One sentence paraphrase in their own words (not a long dump).
4. One thing tonight does **not** do (e.g., does not add new major doctrine; does not teach fatalism; does not rank families).

Then quick retrieval map (write letters only):

- BG 3.9 → action / offering  
- BG 18.63 → choice / responsibility (center)  
- BG 2.22 → continuity / calm speech  
- BG 14.5 → modes as conditions  
- BG 7.14 → shelter beyond māyā loops  

Target synthesis sentence: Choice, consequence, and the modes must form one coherent family practice — synthesis, not new doctrine overload.

## Diagram task

On board or `visuals/V12/` materials, label the five lenses:

1. Action / likely reaction  
2. Controlled / influenced / not controlled  
3. Mode-like conditions (not people labels)  
4. False promise / attention loop  
5. Responsible action + devotional shelter  

**Limit box:** “No ranking · No fatalism · No mode-labeling people · No victim-blaming”

Draw one arrow showing tonight’s conclusion: choice and modes synthesized into one practice.

## Scenario cards — five-lens facilitation

Use the three constructed cases inline in `teacher/MAIN-FACILITATOR-GUIDE-V12.md`:

1. Friday lateness (curriculum case) — prefer this if time is short  
2. Mode-labeling a child  
3. Ranking fear on share night  

In pairs: one student facilitates; one records. Require at least **four lenses** + one environmental redesign + one continuing Cycle 2 method. Coach language that refuses blame-as-identity.

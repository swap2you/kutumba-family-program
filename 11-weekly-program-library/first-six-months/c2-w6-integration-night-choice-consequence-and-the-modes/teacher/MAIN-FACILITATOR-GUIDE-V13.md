## Core explanation (facilitator must say)

Cycle 2 taught action and sacrifice (BG 3.9), responsible choice after hearing (BG 18.63), the soul’s changing of bodies (BG 2.22), bondage by the three modes (BG 14.5), and crossing māyā by surrender (BG 7.14). Integration Night weaves these into one coherent family practice: analyze without blame, choose without fatalism, and pair boundaries with shelter. This is synthesis — not ranking families and not opening a new doctrine track.

**Conclusion line:** Knowledge should lead to thoughtful, responsible choice under Kṛṣṇa's guidance — choose with all five lenses.

**Common errors to block:**
1. Ranking families on “who applied Cycle 2 better.”
2. New doctrine overload / fatalism that denies responsible choice.

## Analogies / examples with limits (teach inline)

### 1) Five camera angles on one scene
- **Say:** One family conflict is like one scene filmed by five cameras. Action, choice, modes, false promise, and shelter each show part of the truth. Using only one camera creates a distorted movie — blame, despair, or fake toughness.
- **Limit:** Pedagogy only — not a verse quotation. Do not film or expose real family fights.
- **Younger:** Five picture cards for one puppet problem.
- **Older/adult:** Name which lens was missing in a sample sentence.
### 2) Toolbox, not trophy shelf
- **Say:** Cycle 2 methods are tools for the off week, not trophies for display. Families pick one tool to keep using — before–during–after, choice pause, bedtime remembrance, clarity reset, or boundary plus shelter.
- **Limit:** Do not shame families who pick the smallest tool.
- **Younger:** Choose one tool card to take home.
- **Older/adult:** Write why your tool fits your real schedule.
### 3) Rudder after the map
- **Say:** Hearing is the map (knowledge). BG 18.63 is the rudder: deliberate, then steer. Modes are wind and current. Shelter is the harbor light. A map without a rudder is decoration; a rudder without a map is thrashing.
- **Limit:** Analogy is pedagogy; return to the five verses.
- **Younger:** Steer left to helping choice; steer right only after pause.
- **Older/adult:** Where do we have map without rudder at home?

## Three constructed household cases (week-specific)

### Constructed case 1 — Friday rush and fatalism
- **Situation:** A fictional family arrives late every Friday. One adult says traffic is destiny; another scrolls while children melt down; kīrtana starts already irritated.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** “Nothing can change — it’s karma,” or ranking the “spiritual” spouse against the “messy” one.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 18.63 deliberation/choice; BG 3.9 action; BG 14.5 modes; BG 7.14 shelter.
- **Compassionate response:** Name all five lenses kindly. Reject fatalism. Pick one environmental change (earlier snack prep; phones in a basket) plus one shelter cue.
- **Family action:** Write one continuing Cycle 2 method for the off week and one measurable environment change.
- **What not to say:** “Your family failed Cycle 2.” Public ranking. Diagnosing past lives.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — puppet rush + traffic-light pause. Older — fill five-lens sheet.
### Constructed case 2 — Mode insult as spirituality
- **Situation:** After a conflict, a parent calls a child “tamasic” as a permanent identity and claims this is Gītā wisdom.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Mode words are fixed labels that excuse contempt.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 14.5 describes binding modes; Cycle 2 blocked permanent insults; BG 18.63 invites better choice.
- **Compassionate response:** Correct firmly: modes describe conditioning weather, not a child’s permanent worth. Repair; choose a clarity-reset tool.
- **Family action:** Family correction card: “Modes ≠ insults.” Practice one respectful redo.
- **What not to say:** Repeating the insult “as teaching.” Comparing children.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — mode-weather matching without naming kids. Older — rewrite insult into weather + choice.
### Constructed case 3 — Boundary without shelter
- **Situation:** A family sets a strict device rule after C2-W5 but refuses japa, verse card, or devotee check-in — “we’ll force it.” Relapse brings shame spirals.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Willpower alone completes Cycle 2; shelter is optional.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 7.14 surrender/shelter; five-lens method requires shelter lens.
- **Compassionate response:** Affirm the boundary; add a tiny shelter. Shame is not a lens.
- **Family action:** Pair the continuing method with one shelter cue written on the fridge.
- **What not to say:** “Fake devotees need force.” Ranking screen-time publicly.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — boundary card + heart-shelter card. Older — write false promise → boundary → shelter.

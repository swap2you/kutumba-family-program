## Objective

Students map five Cycle 2 verses to five lenses, analyze a fictional case without fatalism or ranking, and select one continuing method plus one environmental change.

## Exact memory phrase

> Knowledge should lead to thoughtful, responsible choice under Kṛṣṇa's guidance.

**Short echo:** Choose with all five lenses.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

1. Welcome; feet on floor.
2. Hook: “If someone says ‘it’s karma, nothing can change,’ which verse pushes back — and how?”
3. Essential question: Can we synthesize choice and modes?

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation

1. Display review_iast_line; read once.
2. Students match each BG reference to a lens.
3. Underline vimṛśya / yathecchasi / tathā kuru on BG 18.63 strip.
4. Paraphrase only; no invented dialogue.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A of the older activity pack. Teacher circulates; use `OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md` for expected directions (not for grading ranks).

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

**Card A — Friday rush/fatalism**
**Card B — Mode insult**
**Card C — Boundary without shelter**
For each: missing lens, corrected response, what not to say.

Share one insight per group (60 seconds).

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Lens or Leak

1. Teacher reads a claim.
2. Teams show **L** (uses a true lens) or **K** (leak: fatalism/rank/insult/willpower-only).
3. Three rounds; explain one L with a verse.
4. No mocking.

### 33–37 min — Diagram / map task

Complete five-lens diagram for one fictional case. Circle the shelter cue. Write short echo.

### 37–40 min — Project sentence + handoff

Section E: method + environment change. Hand to parent at 3:10. No ranking.

# C2-W6 Younger Teacher Guide (K–2)

**Week:** Integration Night: Choice, Consequence and the Modes
**Primary:** Cycle 2 review (BG 3.9 · 18.63 · 2.22 · 14.5 · 7.14) — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/63/
**Session length:** 40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

---

## Objective

Children practice a choice pause on a puppet rush problem, meet five simple lens pictures, and take home one tool card with the memory phrase.

## Exact memory phrase

> Choose with all five lenses.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** Choice, consequence, and the modes must form one coherent family practice — synthesis, not new doctrine overload.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** When things feel rushed, we pause, remember Kṛṣṇa, and choose a helping next step. We use all five picture lenses.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**
  - “Nothing can ever change.”
  - “We rank who is best.”
- **Do not:** invent deity dialogue; force confession; rank children; use scare tactics; claim temple certification stickers.

## Materials

- Puppet
- Five lens picture cards
- Traffic-light pause cards
- Tool cards
- Crayons
- Soft toss object
- Calm-corner chair

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; calm corner with one chair for quiet reset. Keep aisles clear for movement.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome by name; feet on floor.
2. Echo three times: *Choose / with / all five lenses.*
3. Show traffic-light: red pause, yellow remember, green help.
4. Say once: “We do not rank friends.”

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Sometimes a family feels rushed and grumpy before singing for Kṛṣṇa.
> Kṛṣṇa teaches us to think carefully and then choose a good next step.
> We can look with five helper pictures: what we did, what we can choose, what mood-weather we feel, what trick the mind tells, and how we take shelter of Kṛṣṇa.
> We do not say “nothing can change.” We pause and choose with Kṛṣṇa’s help.

**Story boundaries:** Paraphrase review themes only. No invented deity quotes. No scary karma talk. No ranking children.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. What do we do at the red light? (Pause)
2. Who helps us choose? (Kṛṣṇa)
3. Do we rank friends? (No)

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Traffic-Light Five Lenses (steps)

1. Spread in open space.
2. Red card — freeze (pause).
3. Yellow — hand on heart + memory phrase.
4. Green — walk to helping mat.
5. Teacher flashes one lens card; children point to it.
6. Three rounds; end seated with phrase echo.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Five-lens tool wheel (steps)

1. Give a paper circle divided into five slices (pre-drawn).
2. Child colors each slice lightly.
3. Helper writes memory phrase in the center: **Choose with all five lenses.**
4. Attach one tool-card tab the child picks for home.
5. Practice pointing to each slice once.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet if available. If short on time, keep the craft as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind hands** — “Hands help; toys rest.”
- **Loud shout** — “Soft voice, please.”
- **Quiet reset** — “Calm corner for sixty seconds.”

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Materials away; craft in hand.
2. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:
   > Memory phrase: “Choose with all five lenses..” Home cue: Continue one selected Cycle 2 method during the off week.
3. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Running | Walking feet. | Walk to mat |
| Snatching | Kind hands. | Hands to lap |
| Interrupt shout | Soft voice. | Whisper retry |
| Shutdown | Quiet reset is okay. | Calm corner 60 sec |

## Backup low-prep (if craft fails or energy crashes)

1. Soft toss circle.
2. Catch, say “Pause” or “Choose with Kṛṣṇa,” toss on.
3. End with three memory-phrase echoes.

## What success looks like

- Echoes memory phrase
- Can pause at red card
- Says we do not rank friends

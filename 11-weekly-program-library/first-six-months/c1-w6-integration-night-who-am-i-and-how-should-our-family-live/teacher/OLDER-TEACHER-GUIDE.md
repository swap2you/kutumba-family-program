# C1-W6 Older Teacher Guide (Grades 4–5)

**Week:** Integration Night — Who Am I and How Should Our Family Live?  
**Primary:** Cycle 1 review chain — ŚB 1.2.18 · BG 2.13 · BG 2.20 · ŚB 11.9.29 · BG 8.15  
**Session length:** ~40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)  
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10  
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

## Objective

Students retrieve the Cycle 1 chain without ranking, match each week to its locked primary (W5 = **BG 8.15**), build a coherent identity–purpose–practice map, choose an invitational share format, and answer: **Can our family explain and apply what we learned?**

## Exact memory phrase

> We remember who we are and how our family chooses to live.

**Short echo:** One chain — hear, identity, soul, chance, shelter.

## Teacher background

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Identity, purpose, and practice must form one coherent family life — reviewing the Cycle 1 chain with W5 as BG 8.15.
- **Locked review set:**
  1. ŚB 1.2.18 — hearing and service steady devotion
  2. BG 2.13 — body changes; self continues
  3. BG 2.20 — eternal soul (not God Himself)
  4. ŚB 11.9.29 — rare human opportunity
  5. BG 8.15 — lasting shelter (locked W5 primary)
- **Block:** Competition/ranking; loudest = wisest; speed to Cycle 2 while confused; wrong Mṛgāri sources (must be CC Madhya 24 if mentioned).
- **Rubric lenses (never placement):** understanding, application, teamwork, source accuracy.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

1. Welcome; feet on floor. Say aloud: “Retrieval is mercy, not a quiz show.”
2. Hook: “Is drawing-only or one sentence enough for success tonight?” Affirm yes.
3. Essential question: Can our family explain and apply what we learned?

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation (review chain)

1. Display five references in order; students copy the chain.
2. Confirm W5 locked primary is **BG 8.15** (cross out BG 5.22 / 9.27 if offered as “the” W5 primary).
3. Observation prompts (write short answers):
   - Which verse steadies hearing + home practice?
   - Which separates body change from continuing self?
   - Which blocks “I am God”?
   - Which teaches rare opportunity without fear?
   - Which names lasting shelter in the Lord?
4. One-sentence synthesis: identity + purpose + practice = coherent family life.
5. Paraphrase only; no new Cycle 2 ontology.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A:
1. Fill each week’s locked primary.
2. Confirm W5 = BG 8.15.
3. Name one blocked misconception per week.
4. Write one integrated family living sentence.

Circulate with answer key for directions, not ranks. Missed weeks = refill kindly, not public “behind.”

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

**Card A — Family fears presenting (thinks contest).** Mistaken: Presentation equals ranking.  
**Card B — One child dominates every share.** Mistaken: Loudest equals wisest.  
**Card C — Parents want Cycle 2 despite W2–W3 confusion.** Mistaken: Speed equals progress.

Each group writes mistaken conclusion / principle / compassionate response / better action / what not to say. Share 60 seconds.

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Chain Match Relay

1. Teams quietly match five themes to five locked primaries.
2. Include two distractor verses to reject.
3. Bonus: if Mṛgāri appears on a card, mark **M24** or **WRONG**.
4. One student explains the necklace in ~20 seconds — no scores announced aloud.

### 33–37 min — Diagram task

Fill:

`Hearing → Body/Self clarity → Soul nature → Rare opportunity → Lasting shelter → Family living practice`

Circle **Family living practice**; write short echo inside. Private family details stay private.

### 37–40 min — Project contribution + reflection + handoff

1. Project: “Our family can explain Cycle 1 by _______________ and will apply _______________ this week.”
2. Choose share format: one sentence / drawing-only / short team roles.
3. Reflection: Where might ranking or speed pretend to be progress?
4. Parent handoff: invitational share; extend C1 privately if unclear; no public “behind.”

## Extension (if group finishes early)

Build a four-lens checklist (understanding / application / teamwork / source accuracy) for their own share — still no placement scores or hallway comparisons.

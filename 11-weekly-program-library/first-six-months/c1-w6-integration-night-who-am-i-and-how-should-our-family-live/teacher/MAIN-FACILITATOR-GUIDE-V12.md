## 10–20 minute speaking script (deliverable prose)

> Facilitation note: Speak naturally. Pause for the 20-second pair share where indicated. Do not replace this with "see research."

Tonight is integration night — not a new ontology dump and not a contest.

Essential question: Can our family explain and apply what we learned?

Primary frame: Cycle 1 review chain, with W5 locked as **BG 8.15**.
Review links: ŚB 1.2.18 · BG 2.13 · BG 2.20 · ŚB 11.9.29 · BG 8.15
Teaching meaning: "Identity, purpose, and practice must form one coherent family life — reviewing the Cycle 1 chain with W5 as BG 8.15."

Conclusion: Identity, purpose, and practice must form one coherent family life.

If your family missed a week, you are still welcome. Retrieval is mercy, not a quiz show. We will refill gaps without announcing who is “behind.” The goal is one coherent family life: hearing, identity clarity, soul nature, protected human opportunity, and lasting shelter in the Lord.

We will walk five stations or five beads:

1) Hearing steadies devotion (ŚB 1.2.18).
2) Body changes; self continues (BG 2.13).
3) Eternal individual soul — not God Himself (BG 2.20 / BG 15.7).
4) Human life is rare opportunity — mercy, not fear; Mṛgāri from Madhya 24 (ŚB 11.9.29).
5) Lasting shelter in the Lord (BG 8.15).

Analogies: necklace, recipe card, station map — all refuse ranking and exam culture.

Shares tonight are invitational. Drawing-only is success. One sentence is success. Rubric lenses are understanding, application, teamwork, and source accuracy — never placement.

If a family is still confused on identity weeks, recommend extending C1 review privately. Speed is not progress.

Supporting review set:
- ŚB 1.2.18 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/18/ — W1 — hearing and service steady devotion.
- BG 2.13 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/13/ — W2 — body changes; self continues.
- BG 2.20 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/20/ — W3 — eternal soul.

- BG 8.15 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/15/ — W5 — lasting shelter (locked as W5 primary for review).

Memory: We remember who we are and how our family chooses to live.

I am going to teach the analogies slowly, with limits in the same breath, because an analogy without a limit becomes a new false doctrine.

First: **Necklace of beads**. Weeks link as one life, not isolated events. Limit: No ranking beads; every family's strand is private.

Second: **Family recipe card**. Ingredients must combine: hearing, identity, opportunity, shelter, practice. Limit: Not culinary perfectionism.

Third: **Station map**. Retrieval practice strengthens memory of the chain. Limit: Not exam culture or scoring families.

Hold those as teaching tools, not as replacements for Cycle 1 review chain.

Now two sourced examples, paraphrased only.

Example A — Cycle 1 chain review (W1–W5 primaries). Integration retrieves prior examples — Naimiṣāraṇya pattern, garment/stages, jīva nature, Mṛgāri mercy (Madhya 24), lasting shelter — without new unproven stories. Link: https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/15/

Example B — W5 locked as BG 8.15 (BG 8.15). When reviewing temporary vs lasting, keep BG 8.15 as the W5 primary (not BG 5.22 as primary). Link: https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/15/

Next I will walk all three household cases out loud so you can copy the pattern at home. Please listen for seven fields every time: situation, mistaken conclusion, principle, source, compassionate response, action, and what not to say.

Case 1.
Situation: A family fears presenting because they think it is a contest.
Mistaken conclusion: Presentation equals ranking.
Principle and source: Non-competitive rubric: understanding, application, teamwork, source accuracy — no ranking. (Program project rubric / charter)
Compassionate response: Offer drawing-only or one-sentence options; celebrate presence.
Family action: Choose share format tonight that feels safe.
What not to say: Score families aloud. Compare presentations.

Case 2.
Situation: One child dominates every share.
Mistaken conclusion: Loudest equals wisest.
Principle and source: Team roles — greeting / drawing / sentence. (Pedagogy)
Compassionate response: Assign roles; invite quieter voices first.
Family action: Rotate who speaks the reunification sentence.
What not to say: Only the talented child may speak.

Case 3.
Situation: Parents want Cycle 2 immediately despite confusion on W2–W3.
Mistaken conclusion: Speed equals progress.
Principle and source: Review-before-C2; extend C1 if unclear. (Cycle architecture)
Compassionate response: Private recommendation to extend review; no public labeling of 'behind.'
Family action: Pick one unclear week for a home re-teach.
What not to say: You're slow. Other families finished.

I will now ask the room three discovery questions. You may answer in a whisper to a partner:
- Which week bead feels strongest in your family?
- Which week still feels foggy?
- What format of share feels safe tonight?

Understanding check — please be ready to say in one sentence: Identity, purpose, and practice must form one coherent family life.. Name the primary: Cycle 1 review chain. Name what we are not teaching tonight: ranking, speculation, and the misconception “Competition or ranking of families.”.

Application — before you leave the shared opening, decide silently: time + place for home practice; who starts if others are tired; and the minimum version if the week is hard. Five minutes counts. Memory line plus one kind action counts.

Let me demonstrate a minimum home practice out loud so nobody leaves confused. Tonight after dinner, or at the same chair each evening, someone says the memory line once: “We remember who we are and how our family chooses to live..” Then one family member paraphrases Cycle 1 review chain in a single sentence. Then one gratitude or service act — thanking someone, offering water, putting shoes away as kindness, or a short prayer. If the house is chaotic, keep only the memory line and one kind action. That still honors tonight’s conclusion: Identity, purpose, and practice must form one coherent family life.

When we reunite at 3:10, I will not score families. I will ask for one sentence: “This week we will…” Drawings count. Whispered parent sentences count. Loud brilliance is not holier than quiet sincerity.

Likely questions you may hear from adults — rehearse these answers with me:

If someone asks, “Do we have to present?” — answer: A share is invited; drawing-only and short formats are honored. No forced performance.

If someone asks, “What is W5's primary in the review chain?” — answer: BG 8.15.

If someone asks, “Will families be ranked?” — answer: No. Ranking is a misconception to block.

If someone asks, “Can we start Cycle 2 next week no matter what?” — answer: Only if the family can explain and apply the chain; otherwise extend C1 review.

If someone asks, “What if we missed a week?” — answer: Retrieve what you can; no shame; use stations to refill gaps.

A short word on boundaries. We do not invent quotations. We do not dump full purports into Git or into the room. We do not diagnose families medically or spiritually in public. We do not guarantee initiation, advancement, or certification. We do not claim human, temple, CPO, or publication approval. Science may help habits; it does not prove ātman or replace śāstra.

Operational covenant aloud: Saturday 2:00–4:00; snack and water only; parents onsite; private struggles stay private; no false approval labels.

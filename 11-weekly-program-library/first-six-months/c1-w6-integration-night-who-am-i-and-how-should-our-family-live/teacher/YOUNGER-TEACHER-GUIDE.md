# C1-W6 Younger Teacher Guide (K–2)

**Week:** Integration Night — Who Am I and How Should Our Family Live?  
**Primary:** Cycle 1 review chain — ŚB 1.2.18 · BG 2.13 · BG 2.20 · ŚB 11.9.29 · BG 8.15  
**Session length:** 40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)  
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10  
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

## Objective

Children **retrieve** Cycle 1 as one necklace of beads — hearing, body/self, soul who serves, rare chance, lasting lamp — and take home a five-bead family card. Drawing-only shares count as success; no ranking.

## Exact memory phrase

> We remember who we are and how our family chooses to live.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** Identity, purpose, and practice must form one coherent family life — reviewing the Cycle 1 chain with W5 locked as BG 8.15.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** Five beads on one string: Hear+Practice · Body changes/I continue · Spark who serves · Rare chance · Thanks + Kṛṣṇa’s lamp. Missing a week is okay — we refill kindly, not like a contest.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**  
  - “Tonight is a contest / ranking.”  
  - “Only loud kids win.”  
  - “We must rush to Cycle 2 even if confused.”
- **Do not:** invent new ontology; score families; force verbal shares; cite Mṛgāri from wrong books if W4 bead appears (CC Madhya 24 only).

## Materials

- Five large paper beads or circles labeled: Hear · Body/Self · Soul · Chance · Lamp
- Yarn or string for necklace demo
- Card stock for five-bead take-home cards
- Crayons; soft toss object; line-art if available
- “Drawing share” option cards (success without speaking)

## Room setup

Five bead stations in a gentle path; circle for opening; aisles clear for Bead Walk.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome; feet on floor. “Tonight we remember — not a contest.”
2. Show empty string, then add beads one by one as you name weeks lightly.
3. Echo memory phrase three times: *We remember who we are / and how our family / chooses to live.*
4. Ask: “Is drawing your bead okay?” Yes — celebrate nods.

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> For five weeks our families learned pieces that belong on one string.  
> First bead: Regular hearing and a tiny home practice steady our devotion.  
> Second bead: My body changes; I continue as the conscious self — kind words about bodies.  
> Third bead: I am an eternal soul — a spark who serves Kṛṣṇa, not the whole sun — and I still care for my body.  
> Fourth bead: Human life is a rare chance to ask who I am and serve — chance words, not scare words.  
> Fifth bead: Temporary joys with gratitude; lasting fulfillment in Kṛṣṇa — the lamp.  
> Tonight we put the beads together. If your family missed a bead, we smile and refill. Loud is not wiser. Drawing is success. We remember who we are and how our family chooses to live.

**Story boundaries:** Review only locked primaries above. No new metaphysics. No ranking. W5 bead = BG 8.15. Mṛgāri only if needed = Madhya 24.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. Are we having a contest tonight? (**No**)
2. Name one bead you remember. (any week)
3. What is one way our family can live this week? (hear / kind words / serve / Must / thanks)

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Bead Walk (steps)

1. Place five bead stations in a path.
2. Children walk slowly station to station.
3. At each station, teacher gives a one-word cue; children echo the matching short line:
   - Hear → “Hear + Practice”
   - Body → “I continue”
   - Soul → “Spark who serves”
   - Chance → “Rare chance”
   - Lamp → “Thanks + lamp”
4. After station five, freeze and say full memory phrase.
5. Two full walks max. Optional: children carry a paper bead and drop it in a “family bowl” (no scores).
6. End seated.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Five-bead family card (steps)

1. Give horizontal card stock with five circles pre-drawn (or children draw five).
2. Color each bead a different color; helper labels 1–5 with week icons if literacy is limited.
3. Inside or below, write memory phrase (helper may write).
4. Child draws one family living choice in a sixth “live” box (dinner phrase, kind words, thanks).
5. Practice pointing beads 1→5 while whispering cues.
6. Offer drawing-only reunification option: show card instead of speaking.

Full steps in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md`.

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

Line-art if time; else keep bead card. Drawing-only is complete success.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

- **Contest talk** — “No ranking — beads for every family.”
- **Dominating share** — “Friend’s turn; drawing is success.”
- **Running** — “Bead walk feet.”
- **Quiet reset** — calm corner with one bead; rejoin on phrase.

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

> Memory: “We remember who we are and how our family chooses to live.” Home cue: point to one bead nightly and do one living choice. Drawing-only share is success. Do not rush confused families publicly.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Ranking talk | “No contest.” | Soft rewrite |
| Dominating | “Friend’s turn.” | Pass turn |
| Running | “Bead walk.” | Walk |
| Shutdown | “Drawing is success.” | Show card |

## Backup low-prep

Soft toss: catch → name one bead word (hear / continue / spark / chance / lamp). Three phrase echoes. Skip stations if energy crashes; use five cards on floor instead.

## What success looks like

- Child can point to at least three beads with a cue word.
- Child can echo memory phrase.
- Child feels safe with drawing-only share.

## Boundaries

- Snack/water at 3:30; no weekly meal.
- No family ranking; no “behind” labels.
- Review only — no new Cycle 2 ontology.

# C1-W6 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** Integration Night — Who Am I and How Should Our Family Live?  
**Primary:** Cycle 1 review — ŚB 1.2.18 · BG 2.13 · BG 2.20 · ŚB 11.9.29 · BG 8.15  
**Memory phrase:** We remember who we are and how our family chooses to live.

---

## Objective

Children walk five beads of Cycle 1, echo the memory phrase, and take home a five-bead family card. Drawing-only share is full success — no ranking.

## Core activity — Necklace of Five Beads (5–8 min)

1. Show yarn; add beads one by one with child-friendly cues:
   - Hear + Practice
   - Body changes / I continue
   - Spark who serves
   - Rare chance
   - Thanks + lamp (Kṛṣṇa)
2. Children touch each bead and repeat the cue.
3. Say: “Missing a bead? We refill kindly — not a contest.”
4. Three echoes of memory phrase.

## Story boundary

Review only the five locked primaries. No new ontology. No invented dialogue. No ranking. W5 bead = BG 8.15. If Mṛgāri mentioned: CC Madhya 24 only.

## Movement — Bead Walk

1. Five stations in a path.
2. At each station, echo the cue word/line.
3. After station five, freeze + full memory phrase.
4. Two walks max. Optional: drop a paper bead in family bowl (no scores).

## Craft — Five-bead family card (step-by-step)

1. Card with five circles (pre-drawn or child-drawn).
2. Color each bead differently; optional icons for weeks 1–5.
3. Write memory phrase below (helper may write).
4. Sixth “live” box: draw one family living choice.
5. Practice pointing 1→5 with cues.
6. Reunification option: show card instead of speaking.
7. Take home near dinner table.

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` if present; else color bead card.

## Backup (low prep)

Soft toss: “hear,” “continue,” “spark,” “chance,” “lamp.” Close with phrase. Floor cards instead of stations if needed.

## Materials

Five bead cutouts, yarn, card stock, crayons, soft toss object, drawing-share option cards, line art optional.

## Parent note

Home: point to one bead nightly + one living choice. Drawing-only presentation is success. No family ranking; do not publicly label anyone “behind.” Snack/water only at program.

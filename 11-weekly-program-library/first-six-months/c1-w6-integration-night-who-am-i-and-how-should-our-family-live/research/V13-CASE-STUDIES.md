# C1-W6 Case Studies

### Constructed case 1 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** A family fears presenting because they think it is a contest.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Presentation equals ranking.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Non-competitive rubric: understanding, application, teamwork, source accuracy — no ranking. — Program project rubric / charter
- **Compassionate response:** Offer drawing-only or one-sentence options; celebrate presence.
- **Family action:** Choose share format tonight that feels safe.
- **What not to say:** Score families aloud. Compare presentations.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

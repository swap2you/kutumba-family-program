# C3-W2 Younger Teacher Guide (K–2)

**Week:** Who Is Kṛṣṇa? The Supreme Personality of Godhead
**Primary:** BG 7.7 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/7/7/
**Session length:** 40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

---

## Objective

Children say nothing is above Kṛṣṇa, string pearls on a thread, and hear an authorized short Govardhana shelter picture without invented quotes.

## Exact memory phrase

> Nothing is above Kṛṣṇa.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** There is no truth superior to Kṛṣṇa; everything rests upon Him as pearls are strung on a thread.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme. Everything rests on Him like pearls on a string. We speak kindly.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**
  - “Tease others to prove we are right.”
  - “Make up Kṛṣṇa talking lines.”
- **Do not:** invent deity dialogue; force confession; rank children; use scare tactics; claim temple certification stickers.

## Materials

- Paper pearls/string
- Mountain/shelter props
- Outline picture cards
- Crayons
- Soft toss
- Calm corner
- Phrase stickers

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; calm corner with one chair for quiet reset. Keep aisles clear for movement.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome.
2. Echo: *Nothing / is above / Kṛṣṇa.*
3. Show pearl string.
4. Practice: “We don’t tease.”

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Kṛṣṇa says nothing is higher than Him. All things rest on Him like pearls on a thread.
> When the people of Vraja needed protection, they took shelter of Kṛṣṇa, who held the big hill like a shelter umbrella.
> We do not invent extra talking lines. We remember Kṛṣṇa’s care and say His name with love.

**Story boundaries:** Authorized skeleton only. No invented deity dialogue. No scary storm trauma talk.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. Is anyone above Kṛṣṇa? (No)
2. Do we tease to prove a point? (No)
3. Who do we take shelter of? (Kṛṣṇa)

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Pearl Thread and Mountain Shelter (steps)

1. Children hold paper pearl strings and walk a circle saying the phrase.
2. On signal, gather under “mountain” arms/prop.
3. Whisper one Kṛṣṇa name.
4. Two rounds; sit.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Pearls-on-thread card (steps)

1. Card with drawn thread.
2. Glue 3–5 paper pearls.
3. Helper writes: **Nothing is above Kṛṣṇa.**
4. Back: draw mountain shelter.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet if available. If short on time, keep the craft as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind hands** — “Hands help; toys rest.”
- **Loud shout** — “Soft voice, please.”
- **Quiet reset** — “Calm corner for sixty seconds.”

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Materials away; craft in hand.
2. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:
   > Memory phrase: “Nothing is above Kṛṣṇa..” Home cue: Remember one name or quality of Kṛṣṇa once daily.
3. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Running | Walking feet. | Walk to mat |
| Snatching | Kind hands. | Hands to lap |
| Interrupt shout | Soft voice. | Whisper retry |
| Shutdown | Quiet reset is okay. | Calm corner 60 sec |

## Backup low-prep (if craft fails or energy crashes)

Toss and say a Kṛṣṇa name; end with phrase.

## What success looks like

- Echoes phrase
- Refuses teasing line
- Remembers shelter picture

## Boundaries

- Snack/water only happens in main schedule (3:30), not as a track bribe.
- No weekly meal language.
- No fabricated approval stickers implying temple certification.
- Privacy: no forced confession.

## Core explanation (facilitator must say)

BG 7.7 establishes that nothing is superior to Kṛṣṇa and that all existence rests upon Him. Supporting paraphrases (BG 10.8, ŚB 1.3.28) reinforce source and supremacy without requiring KUTUMBA to invent theology. Families cultivate wonder and affection while refusing sectarian aggression and refusing invented or premature confidential pastime talk.

**Conclusion line:** Nothing is above Kṛṣṇa — speak it with śāstra and reverence.

**Common errors to block:**
1. Sectarian aggression toward persons.
2. Invented confidential pastimes or fabricated dialogue.

## Analogies / examples with limits (teach inline)

### 1) Pearls on a thread
- **Say:** Pearls are beautiful, yet without the thread they scatter. BG 7.7 teaches that all this rests on Kṛṣṇa as pearls rest on a thread.
- **Limit:** Pedagogy from the verse image; do not mock others’ beads/faith objects.
- **Younger:** String paper pearls; say “Kṛṣṇa holds all.”
- **Older/adult:** What depends on Kṛṣṇa in this teaching — and what is not “equal absolute”?
### 2) Map vs preference
- **Say:** A map is not the same as “I like this color.” Supremacy claims in KUTUMBA are map claims from śāstra via the ācārya, not club preference.
- **Limit:** Do not claim KUTUMBA is itself the map.
- **Younger:** We learn from Kṛṣṇa’s book, not from teasing.
- **Older/adult:** Write one śāstra-based sentence vs one preference-only sentence.
### 3) Mountain shelter (Govardhana)
- **Say:** When danger came, the people of Vraja took shelter of Kṛṣṇa’s arrangement under Govardhana — supremacy shown as protection and care, not as a debate trophy.
- **Limit:** Authorized summary only; no invented quotes.
- **Younger:** Hands as mountain; friends underneath — shelter.
- **Older/adult:** List supremacy, protection, pride-correction without dialogue invention.

- **Situation:** A fictional older student mocks a classmate’s family’s worship style to “defend Kṛṣṇa’s supremacy.”
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Aggression proves conviction.

- **Compassionate response:** Correct: state śāstra calmly; apologize for insult; protect dignity.
- **Family action:** Practice one respectful explanation sentence at home.

- **Age adaptation:** Younger — “We don’t tease.” Older — rewrite insult into śāstra sentence.
### Constructed case 2 — Invented pastime for fun
- **Situation:** A parent adds made-up dramatic dialogue to a kathā “to keep kids interested,” including premature confidential tones.

- **Relevant principle / source:** Authorized kathā only; no invented dialogue.

- **What not to say:** Shaming the parent as “demoniac.” Public exposure.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — fixed story spine. Older — mark “authorized vs invented.”

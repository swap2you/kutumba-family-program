# C3-W2 Analogies and Limits

**Week:** Who Is Kṛṣṇa? The Supreme Personality of Godhead
**Rule:** Label every analogy as pedagogy. Never present an analogy as a śāstra quotation.

| Analogy | Teaching value | Source status | Failure point / limit | Younger use | Older/adult use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pearls on a thread | Teaching value | Pedagogy | Pedagogy from the verse image; do not mock others’ beads/faith objects. | String paper pearls; say “Kṛṣṇa holds all.” | What depends on Kṛṣṇa in this teaching — and what is not “equal absolute”? |
| Map vs preference | Teaching value | Pedagogy | Do not claim KUTUMBA is itself the map. | We learn from Kṛṣṇa’s book, not from teasing. | Write one śāstra-based sentence vs one preference-only sentence. |
| Mountain shelter (Govardhana) | Teaching value | Pedagogy | Authorized summary only; no invented quotes. | Hands as mountain; friends underneath — shelter. | List supremacy, protection, pride-correction without dialogue invention. |

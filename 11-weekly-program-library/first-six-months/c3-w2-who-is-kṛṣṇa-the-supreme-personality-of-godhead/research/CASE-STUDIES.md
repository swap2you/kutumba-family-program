# C3-W2 Case Studies

**Week:** Who Is Kṛṣṇa? The Supreme Personality of Godhead
**Primary principle source:** BG 7.7 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/7/7/
**Note:** All cases are fictional / anonymized teaching constructs for the founding cohort. Not reports about real cohort families.

---

### Constructed case 1 — Sectarian insult on the playground (fictional)

- **Situation:** A fictional older student mocks a classmate’s family’s worship style to “defend Kṛṣṇa’s supremacy.”
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Aggression proves conviction.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 7.7 supremacy with reverence; block sectarian aggression.
- **Compassionate response:** Correct: state śāstra calmly; apologize for insult; protect dignity.
- **Family action:** Practice one respectful explanation sentence at home.
- **What not to say:** Anything that humiliates another child or tradition’s members.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — “We don’t tease.” Older — rewrite insult into śāstra sentence.
### Constructed case 2 — Invented pastime for fun (fictional)

- **Situation:** A parent adds made-up dramatic dialogue to a kathā “to keep kids interested,” including premature confidential tones.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Entertainment license beats authorization.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Authorized kathā only; no invented dialogue.
- **Compassionate response:** Return to outline cards; wonder without fabrication.
- **Family action:** Choose one authorized name/quality/pastime line for daily remembrance.
- **What not to say:** Shaming the parent as “demoniac.” Public exposure.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — fixed story spine. Older — mark “authorized vs invented.”
### Constructed case 3 — Preference-only faith (fictional)

- **Situation:** A teen says, “Kṛṣṇa is supreme because our program likes Him,” with no śāstra anchor.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Club preference equals theology.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 7.7 / 10.8 / 1.3.28 as map.
- **Compassionate response:** Affirm affection; add one verse paraphrase as ground.
- **Family action:** Write verse + one quality on a card.
- **What not to say:** “You’re fake.” Rank devotion.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — pearls craft + phrase. Older — triangle notes.

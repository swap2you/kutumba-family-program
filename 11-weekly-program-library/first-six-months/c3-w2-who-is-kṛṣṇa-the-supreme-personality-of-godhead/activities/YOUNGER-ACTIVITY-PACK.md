# C3-W2 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** Who Is Kṛṣṇa? The Supreme Personality of Godhead
**Primary:** BG 7.7 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/7/7/
**Memory phrase:** Nothing is above Kṛṣṇa.

---

## Objective

Children say nothing is above Kṛṣṇa, string pearls on a thread, and hear an authorized short Govardhana shelter picture without invented quotes.

## Core activity — Pearl Thread and Mountain Shelter

**Time:** 5–8 minutes

1. Children hold paper pearl strings and walk a circle saying the phrase.
2. On signal, gather under “mountain” arms/prop.
3. Whisper one Kṛṣṇa name.
4. Two rounds; sit.

## Story boundary

Authorized skeleton only. No invented deity dialogue. No scary storm trauma talk.

## Craft — Pearls-on-thread card (step-by-step)

1. Card with drawn thread.
2. Glue 3–5 paper pearls.
3. Helper writes: **Nothing is above Kṛṣṇa.**
4. Back: draw mountain shelter.

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` on US Letter if present. If missing, children color their craft only.

## Backup (low prep)

Toss and say a Kṛṣṇa name; end with phrase.

## Materials

- Paper pearls/string
- Mountain/shelter props
- Outline picture cards
- Crayons
- Soft toss
- Calm corner
- Phrase stickers

## Parent note (say at handoff)

Home practice: Remember one name or quality of Kṛṣṇa once daily.. Snack/water only at the Saturday program; no weekly meal. We do not rank children.

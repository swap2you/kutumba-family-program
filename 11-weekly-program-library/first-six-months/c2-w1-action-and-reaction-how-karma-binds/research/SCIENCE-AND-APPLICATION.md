# C2-W1 Science and Application

## Decision — Option B

**N/A — no empirical claim is needed for this week's doctrinal conclusion.**

## Reason

Karma-bondage as taught in Bhagavad-gītā 3.9 (https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/9/) is a **śāstra conclusion**, not an empirical psychology claim. The week’s teaching meaning — that work done for the satisfaction of the Lord frees from bondage while otherwise action binds — rests on revealed text and authorized pedagogy, not on laboratory proof.

## What may still be used at practice-design level only

Habit literature (cue → routine → reflection) may support **practice design** for the Before–During–After Service Method at the habit level only — for example, choosing a trigger time for one routine duty. Such literature:

- does **not** prove ātman, karma, or Viṣṇu;
- does **not** replace BG 3.9;
- must not be phrased as “science confirms karma.”

“We practice habits carefully because practice helps families remember. The doctrine itself comes from śāstra — tonight, Bhagavad-gītā 3.9.”

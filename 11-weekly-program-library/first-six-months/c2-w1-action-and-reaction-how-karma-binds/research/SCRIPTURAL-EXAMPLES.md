## Supporting examples

### 1) BG 4.16 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/16/

- **Devanāgarī:** किं कर्म किमकर्मेति कवयोऽप्यत्र मोहिताः । तत्ते कर्म प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥ १६ ॥
- **IAST:** kiṁ karma kim akarmeti kavayo ’py atra mohitāḥ / tat te karma pravakṣyāmi yaj jñātvā mokṣyase ’śubhāt
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Even thoughtful people become confused about what action and what inaction really are; the Lord teaches action so that understanding can free one from misfortune.
- **Teaching use:** Humility about “simple” definitions of work; invite families to learn from śāstra rather than slogans.
- **Limitation:** Do not expand into a full epistemology lesson.

### 2) BG 4.17 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/17/

- **Devanāgarī:** कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः । अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः ॥ १७ ॥
- **IAST:** karmaṇo hy api boddhavyaṁ boddhavyaṁ ca vikarmaṇaḥ / akarmaṇaś ca boddhavyaṁ gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** One should carefully understand action, forbidden action, and inaction — because the path of karma is deep and easy to misunderstand.
- **Teaching use:** Light taxonomy for older students and parents: action / forbidden action / inaction.
- **Limitation:** Not a complete scholarly map of all karma categories.

### 3) BG 4.18 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/18/

- **Devanāgarī:** कर्मण्यकर्म यः पश्येदकर्मणि च कर्म यः । स बुद्धिमान्मनुष्येषु स युक्तः कृत्स्नकर्मकृत् ॥ १८ ॥
- **IAST:** karmaṇy akarma yaḥ paśyed akarmaṇi ca karma yaḥ / sa buddhimān manuṣyeṣu sa yuktaḥ kṛtsna-karma-kṛt
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** One who sees freedom from binding reaction even while working, and sees binding reaction even in apparent idleness, is wise and engaged rightly.
- **Teaching use:** Block “stop all duties to avoid karma”; affirm offered engagement.
- **Limitation:** Keep language age-safe; avoid abstract philosophical overload for K–2.

### 4) BG 3.13 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/13/

- **Devanāgarī:** यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तो मुच्यन्ते सर्वकिल्बिषैः । भुञ्जते ते त्वघं पापा ये पचन्त्यात्मकारणात् ॥ १३ ॥
- **IAST:** yajña-śiṣṭāśinaḥ santo mucyante sarva-kilbiṣaiḥ / bhuñjate te tv aghaṁ pāpā ye pacanty ātma-kāraṇāt
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Those who accept what remains after offering are purified; those who cook only for their own sense pleasure take on sinful reaction.
- **Teaching use:** Kitchen-level illustration of offered vs self-centered action.
- **Limitation:** No shaming of newcomers’ food practice; no perfectionism contest.

## Objective

Children will explain in child language that choices plant seeds, and they will practice planting “service seeds” (kind, careful helping) rather than “hurry-hurt seeds.”

## Story script (5–8 minutes) — read/speak as prose

“Friends, sit soft like little seeds in safe soil. Today we learn: choices are seeds.

When I plant a mango seed, I do not get a mango in one second. I wait. Later, something grows. Our choices are like that. If I help put shoes in a neat line, that is a service seed. If I grab and shout ‘mine!’, that is a hurry-hurt seed. Different seeds grow different gardens.

Long ago there was a king who loved to give gifts. One day a gift got mixed up because he did not check carefully enough. The mix-up caused trouble. We do not make up extra talking for the king. We learn one thing: careful checking is a service seed. Careless hurry can grow a hard problem.

Kṛṣṇa teaches grown-ups in Bhagavad-gītā that work done for the Lord can free us, and other work can bind. For us today, we say it this way: Choices are seeds. I want to plant service seeds.

We will move like seeds. We will make a service-seed card. We will color. And when we see our parents, we will say our phrase together.”

## Three wonder questions

1. What is one service seed you can plant at home tonight?
2. What happens inside a friendship when we plant hurry-hurt seeds?
3. Whom can we help carefully this week without asking for a clap?

## Movement game — Seed Steps (WITH STEPS)

**Name:** Seed Steps  
**Time:** 7–8 minutes  
**Space:** Clear floor circle

1. Teacher places two zones with tape: **Service Garden** (green card) and **Hard Patch** (gray card).
2. Children stand in the middle as “sleeping seeds” (crouch).
3. Teacher holds up one picture card and names it aloud (example: “soft words” or “yank toy”).
4. If it is a service seed, children take three gentle heel-toe steps toward Service Garden, then freeze with hands on heart.
5. If it is a hurry-hurt seed, children take three tiny steps toward Hard Patch, then freeze and show “reset hands” (palms open, quiet).
6. Teacher says: “We can plant a new seed!” Children walk back to center and reset.
7. Play 6–8 cards. End with all children in Service Garden echoing: “Choices are seeds. I want to plant service seeds.”
8. If energy spikes: switch to seated hand-motions only (grow tall / curl small).

---

## Craft — Service Seed Card (WITH STEPS)

**Time:** 8–9 minutes

1. Give each child a half-sheet card with a large empty seed outline.
2. Child colors the seed warm colors (yellow/orange/green).
3. Inside or under the seed, child draws **one** service action (helping set cups, returning a toy, gentle words).
4. Teacher or helper writes the child’s words in one short sentence if the child dictates (“I help with shoes”).
5. Glue or tape the memory phrase strip on the back: “Choices are seeds. I want to plant service seeds.”
6. Place finished cards in the Service Seeds basket until parent handoff.
7. Early finishers color the line-art printable (next section).

## Behavior redirects

- Feet on floor · hands kind · voices soft · toys rest on the shelf.
- If a child boasts or ranks (“I planted more seeds!”): “In KUTUMBA we cheer for everyone’s service seed.”
- If a child says someone “has bad karma”: “We don’t say that here. We plant our next service seed.”
- Quiet reset: sit, three breaths, soft palm tap on knees, rejoin.

---

## Backup (if craft fails or time collapses)

1. Sit in a circle.
2. Pass the soft object.
3. Each child names one kind action or points to a service-seed card.
4. Whole group echoes the memory phrase three times.
5. Color only if minutes remain.

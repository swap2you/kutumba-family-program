## Objective

Students will observe BG 3.9, explain that work aimed only at separate material interest binds while work for Viṣṇu should be done without selfish attachment, and analyze one action using intention–method–consequence.

## Primary text observation — BG 3.9

Open and keep visible: https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/9/

- **Devanāgarī:** यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः । तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर ॥ ९ ॥
- **IAST:** yajñārthāt karmaṇo ’nyatra loko ’yaṁ karma-bandhanaḥ / tad-arthaṁ karma kaunteya mukta-saṅgaḥ samācara
- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Work done for the satisfaction of the Lord frees us from bondage; otherwise action binds. Therefore act with detachment for His purpose.

Students write on observation sheet (5 minutes):

1. Setting / speaker-listener (as known from class: Kṛṣṇa to Arjuna)
2. Key Sanskrit phrase they notice (example: karma-bandhanaḥ)
3. Teaching-meaning paraphrase in their own words (not a long dump)
4. One thing this verse does **not** say (example: “it does not say we should stop all duties” / “it does not say we may blame victims”)

## Worksheet questions (write answers; check with `activities/OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md`)

1. According to BG 3.9’s teaching meaning, what kind of work causes bondage?
2. What kind of work should we perform, and how (attachment posture)?
3. Using BG 4.16–18 support (URLs on worksheet), why is “just stop working” too simple?
4. Give one household example of offered work vs selfish work that look similar from outside.
5. Why must we refuse the sentence: “That family’s hardship proves their past karma”?

## Scenario cards (real prompts — print from activity pack)

Students draw one card and answer: intention / method / likely consequence / one repair.

1. **Angry text:** You send a sharp message in a group chat after a schedule mix-up.
2. **Gift for praise:** You give a donation mainly hoping for public thanks.
3. **Quiet repair:** You fix a sibling’s project and notice yourself waiting for praise.

Teacher circulates; stop victim-blame language immediately.

---

## Puzzle / game — Action-Chain / Seed-Fruit Match (WITH STEPS)

**Materials:** 8 seed cards + 8 fruit/result cards (see `activities/OLDER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md`)

1. Shuffle seed cards and fruit cards into two piles.
2. Students work in pairs.
3. Match each seed (action) to a plausible fruit (result). Include at least two **delayed** results (practice → skill; harsh words → later distrust).
4. For each match, pairs place an arrow card labeled **direct**, **indirect**, or **delayed**.
5. Teacher checks two matches aloud using answer key guidance.
6. Extension: build one **action chain** of three links from a single online post seed (post → screenshot → widened conflict) without shaming any real classmate.

## Diagram task — Intention–Method–Consequence

Students complete a three-box diagram for one scenario:

- **Intention:** Why am I acting?
- **Method:** How am I acting (careful / hasty / honest / showy)?
- **Consequence:** What may grow for me and for others?

Then add a fourth mini-box: **Offered version** — how would before–during–after change this?

Diagram may align with `visuals/V12/concept-diagram.svg` if projected.

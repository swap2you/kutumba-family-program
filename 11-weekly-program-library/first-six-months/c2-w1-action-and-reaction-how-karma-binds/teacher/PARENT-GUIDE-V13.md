## Source layer (use mid-track)

**Primary:** BG 3.9 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/9/

**Devanāgarī:** यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः । तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर ॥ ९ ॥

**IAST:** `yajñārthāt karmaṇo ’nyatra loko ’yaṁ karma-bandhanaḥ / tad-arthaṁ karma kaunteya mukta-saṅgaḥ samācara`

**KUTUMBA teaching meaning (not a BBT translation):**  
Work done for the satisfaction of the Lord frees us from bondage; otherwise action binds. Therefore act with detachment for His purpose.

### Constructed case 1 — Chanting as punishment (fictional)

- **Situation:** A fictional parent says, “Go chant until you learn your lesson,” after a spill, linking mantra to shame.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Nāma is a behavior whip.
- **Relevant principle / source:** CC Antya 20.12 — victory of saṅkīrtana as cleansing mercy, not punishment.
- **Compassionate response:** Separate natural consequences from mantra. Invite optional soft hearing later.
- **Family action:** Family card: “We do not punish with chanting.”
- **What not to say:** Public exposure of the parent. Calling them demoniac.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — soft hearing only. Older — rewrite punishment script.
### Constructed case 2 — Volume contest ranking (fictional)

- **Situation:** Siblings argue who is the “best kīrtana leader”; a quieter child withdraws.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Loudest = most spiritual.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Attentive hearing; block performance ranking.
- **Compassionate response:** Reset to call-and-response with hearing goal; affirm quiet participation.
- **Family action:** Three-day five-minute plan with attention cue.
- **What not to say:** Ranking voices publicly. Forcing solos.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — soft voice practice. Older — hearing vs contest worksheet.
### Constructed case 3 — Generic label drift (fictional)

- **Situation:** A facilitator keeps saying only “holy name” and never “śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam / congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's holy name,” leaving the verse’s specificity vague.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Generic label is enough.
- **Relevant principle / source:** User requirement / verse refrain — name śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam.
- **Compassionate response:** Correct language mid-session kindly; reread victory line.
- **Family action:** Memory phrase practice with full refrain.
- **What not to say:** Humiliating the facilitator in front of kids.
- **Age adaptation:** All ages — echo full phrase.

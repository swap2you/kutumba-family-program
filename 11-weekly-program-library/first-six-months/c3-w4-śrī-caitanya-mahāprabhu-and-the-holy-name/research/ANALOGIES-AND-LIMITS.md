# C3-W4 Analogies and Limits

**Week:** Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and the Holy Name
**Rule:** Label every analogy as pedagogy. Never present an analogy as a śāstra quotation.

| Analogy | Teaching value | Source status | Failure point / limit | Younger use | Older/adult use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mirror of the heart | Teaching value | Pedagogy | Analogy from the verse image; not a medical claim. | Wipe a small mirror prop gently while hearing the mantra. | What does “attention” add beyond noise? |
| Forest fire cooled | Teaching value | Pedagogy | No trauma storytelling for children. | Hands cool-down gesture after soft chant. | Name one stress cue that kīrtana might meet with calm. |
| Choir vs contest | Teaching value | Pedagogy | Do not insult enthusiastic singers; redirect to hearing. | Call-and-response with soft voices. | Write hearing goal vs ranking goal. |

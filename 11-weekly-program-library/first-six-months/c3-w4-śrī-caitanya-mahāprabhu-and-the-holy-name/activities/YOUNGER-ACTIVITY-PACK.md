# C3-W4 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and the Holy Name
**Primary:** CC Antya 20.12 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/20/12/
**Memory phrase:** Paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam.

---

## Objective

Children echo Paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam, practice soft call-and-response, and learn we never punish with chanting.

## Core activity — Soft Echo Circle

**Time:** 5–8 minutes

1. Circle; facilitator sings a short line softly.
2. Children echo softly.
3. Hand-to-ear “I heard it.”
4. Two rounds; sit; full phrase once.

## Story boundary

No invented Lord Caitanya quotes. No fear. No ranking.

## Craft — Heart-mirror card (step-by-step)

1. Card with mirror circle.
2. Color gently; write phrase helper-assisted.
3. Back: draw family singing softly.

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` on US Letter if present. If missing, children color their craft only.

## Backup (low prep)

Toss and whisper “Kṛṣṇa”; end with phrase.

## Materials

- Soft clap guide
- Mirror prop
- Phrase poster
- Crayons
- Soft toss
- Calm corner
- Simple melody cue

## Parent note (say at handoff)

Home practice: Five-minute family kīrtana or attentive mantra listening on three days.. Snack/water only at the Saturday program; no weekly meal. We do not rank children.

## Core explanation (facilitator must say)

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu gave and spread śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam — congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's holy name. CC Antya 20.12 glorifies its transformative gifts. Families practice short, attentive kīrtana without ranking voices and without weaponizing mantra as punishment.

**Conclusion line:** Paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam — attention over performance.

**Common errors to block:**
1. Force chanting as punishment.
2. Performance ranking of voices/kīrtana skill.

## Analogies / examples with limits (teach inline)

### 1) Mirror of the heart
- **Say:** A dusty mirror cannot show a clear face. Śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam is praised as cleansing the heart-mirror — pedagogy for attention and hope, not for shaming “dirty kids.”
- **Limit:** Analogy from the verse image; not a medical claim.
- **Younger:** Wipe a small mirror prop gently while hearing the mantra.
- **Older/adult:** What does “attention” add beyond noise?
### 2) Forest fire cooled
- **Say:** Material stress can feel like a spreading fire. The verse praises saṅkīrtana as cooling that blaze — invite shelter, not graphic fear.
- **Limit:** No trauma storytelling for children.
- **Younger:** Hands cool-down gesture after soft chant.
- **Older/adult:** Name one stress cue that kīrtana might meet with calm.
### 3) Choir vs contest
- **Say:** A choir listens; a contest performs to win. Family kīrtana aims at hearing and responding, not ranking.
- **Limit:** Do not insult enthusiastic singers; redirect to hearing.
- **Younger:** Call-and-response with soft voices.
- **Older/adult:** Write hearing goal vs ranking goal.

## Three constructed household cases (week-specific)

### Constructed case 1 — Chanting as punishment
- **Situation:** A fictional parent says, “Go chant until you learn your lesson,” after a spill, linking mantra to shame.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Nāma is a behavior whip.
- **Relevant principle / source:** CC Antya 20.12 — victory of saṅkīrtana as cleansing mercy, not punishment.
- **Compassionate response:** Separate natural consequences from mantra. Invite optional soft hearing later.
- **Family action:** Family card: “We do not punish with chanting.”
- **What not to say:** Public exposure of the parent. Calling them demoniac.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — soft hearing only. Older — rewrite punishment script.
### Constructed case 2 — Volume contest ranking
- **Situation:** Siblings argue who is the “best kīrtana leader”; a quieter child withdraws.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Loudest = most spiritual.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Attentive hearing; block performance ranking.
- **Compassionate response:** Reset to call-and-response with hearing goal; affirm quiet participation.
- **Family action:** Three-day five-minute plan with attention cue.
- **What not to say:** Ranking voices publicly. Forcing solos.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — soft voice practice. Older — hearing vs contest worksheet.
### Constructed case 3 — Generic label drift
- **Situation:** A facilitator keeps saying only “holy name” and never “śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam / congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's holy name,” leaving the verse’s specificity vague.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Generic label is enough.
- **Relevant principle / source:** User requirement / verse refrain — name śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam.
- **Compassionate response:** Correct language mid-session kindly; reread victory line.
- **Family action:** Memory phrase practice with full refrain.
- **What not to say:** Humiliating the facilitator in front of kids.
- **Age adaptation:** All ages — echo full phrase.

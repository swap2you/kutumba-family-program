## Objective

Children echo Paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam, practice soft call-and-response, and learn we never punish with chanting.

## Exact memory phrase

> Paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** All victory to śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana — which cleanses the mirror of the heart, extinguishes the forest fire of material existence, spreads the moonlight of good fortune, is the life of knowledge, expands the ocean of bliss, and bathes the self in nectar at every step.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** We sing Kṛṣṇa's names together — śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam. We listen kindly. We do not use singing to punish.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**
  - “Chant as punishment.”
  - “Louder kids win.”
- **Do not:** invent deity dialogue; force confession; rank children; use scare tactics; claim temple certification stickers.

## Materials

- Soft clap guide
- Mirror prop
- Phrase poster
- Crayons
- Soft toss
- Calm corner
- Simple melody cue

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; calm corner with one chair for quiet reset. Keep aisles clear for movement.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome.
2. Echo phrase slowly in parts.
3. Soft clap once.
4. Say: “We don’t punish with chanting.”

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu taught families to chant Kṛṣṇa's holy names together — śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam.
> It cleans the heart like wiping a mirror and cools hot worry like water on a fire.
> We listen carefully. We do not yell to win. We never make someone chant because they are in trouble.

**Story boundaries:** No invented Lord Caitanya quotes. No fear. No ranking.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. Do we punish with chanting? (No)
2. Do we listen? (Yes)
3. Whose names? (Kṛṣṇa’s)

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Soft Echo Circle (steps)

1. Circle; facilitator sings a short line softly.
2. Children echo softly.
3. Hand-to-ear “I heard it.”
4. Two rounds; sit; full phrase once.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Heart-mirror card (steps)

1. Card with mirror circle.
2. Color gently; write phrase helper-assisted.
3. Back: draw family singing softly.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet if available. If short on time, keep the craft as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind hands** — “Hands help; toys rest.”
- **Loud shout** — “Soft voice, please.”
- **Quiet reset** — “Calm corner for sixty seconds.”

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Materials away; craft in hand.
2. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:
   > Memory phrase: “Paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam..” Home cue: Five-minute family kīrtana or attentive mantra listening on three days.
3. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Running | Walking feet. | Walk to mat |
| Snatching | Kind hands. | Hands to lap |
| Interrupt shout | Soft voice. | Whisper retry |
| Shutdown | Quiet reset is okay. | Calm corner 60 sec |

- Attempts phrase
- Soft echo once
- Says no punishment chanting

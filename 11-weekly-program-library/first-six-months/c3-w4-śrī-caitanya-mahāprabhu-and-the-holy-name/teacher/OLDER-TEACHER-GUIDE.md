## Objective

Students observe CC Antya 20.12, explain śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam benefits in classroom language, and plan three-day attentive practice without ranking.

## Exact memory phrase

> The congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's holy name — śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam — cleanses the heart and expands spiritual life.

**Short echo:** Paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam.

## Teacher background

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** All victory to śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana — which cleanses the mirror of the heart, extinguishes the forest fire of material existence, spreads the moonlight of good fortune, is the life of knowledge, expands the ocean of bliss, and bathes the self in nectar at every step. (Label as KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not as BBT translation.)
- **Supporting frames (one sentence each):** CC Ādi 17.21 yuga-dharma; ŚB 12.3.51 hope; attention over performance.
- **Block:** Force chanting as punishment.; Performance ranking of voices/kīrtana skill.
- **Scope:** No full aparādha prosecution; no advanced ecstasy claims.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

Punishment chanting vs mercy chanting — which matches the verse?

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation

Read IAST refrain; list benefits in student words; insist on śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam label.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A of the older activity pack. Teacher circulates; use `OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md` for expected directions (not for grading ranks).

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

A Punishment B Volume contest C Generic-only label

Share one insight per group (60 seconds).

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Hearing or Contest

H vs C claims; explain with verse.

### 33–37 min — Diagram / map task

Benefit map around the refrain.

### 37–40 min — Project sentence + handoff

Three-day plan to parent.

# C3-W4 Parent Guide

## Essential question
Why chant the holy name?

## Source
CC Antya 20.12 / Śikṣāṣṭaka 1 context — teaching meaning: Chanting the holy name cleanses the mirror of the heart, extinguishes the forest fire of material existence, and spreads the moonlight of auspiciousness — the life of all knowledge.

## Discussion
One realistic household tension; no compelled confession.

## Application
5–15 minute cue; minimum version counts.

## Privacy

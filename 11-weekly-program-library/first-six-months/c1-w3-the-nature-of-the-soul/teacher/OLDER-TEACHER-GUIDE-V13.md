## Objective

Students observe BG 2.20, map jīva is/is-not (eternal, conscious, individual, minute, related in service — **not** God Himself), complete a sun-ray diagram, practice a science deferral line, and answer: **If I am a soul, how should I live?**

## Exact memory phrase

> I am an eternal soul — conscious, individual, and meant for Kṛṣṇa's service.

**Short echo:** Spark who serves — not the sun.

## Teacher background

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** The soul is never born and never dies; unborn, eternal, and everlasting — not destroyed when the body is destroyed. (KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not BBT translation.)
- **Supporting frames:** BG 15.7 — eternal fragmental parts (qualitative likeness, quantitative difference); BG 2.17 — that which pervades the body is indestructible; ŚB 5.10 Jaḍa Bharata context — bodily labels do not define the soul (gentle paraphrase only).
- **Block:** “All souls are God / we are the Supreme”; “science disproved the soul”; “body contempt is spiritual.”
- **Science stance:** **N/A — no empirical claim is needed for this week's doctrinal conclusion** about ātman. Labs measure bodies/behavior; doctrine rests on śāstra.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

1. Welcome; feet on floor.
2. Hook: “If a child proudly says ‘I am God,’ what gentle correction fits — and what would shame look like?” Collect two answers; hold for verse.
3. Essential question: If I am a soul, how should I live?

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation (BG 2.20)

1. Display verse; read IAST together once; Devanāgarī slowly or authorized audio.
2. Students underline: **never born / never dies / not destroyed when the body is destroyed**.
3. Observation prompts (write short answers):
   - What continues when the body is destroyed?
   - What is **not** claimed (that jīva = the Supreme)?
   - What domain does laboratory science measure?
4. Add bridges:
   - BG 15.7 — fragmental part; spark ≠ sun.
   - Science deferral line aloud once as a class.
5. Paraphrase only; no invented dialogue; Jaḍa Bharata only if gentle and nonviolent.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A:
1. Restate teaching meaning in one sentence.
2. Fill: eternal _______________ parts (fragmental).
3. Mark IS / IS NOT for body, Supreme, conscious individual, service.
4. Write a gentle correction for “I am God.”
5. Write science deferral in own words.
6. Name one body-care act as service readiness.

Circulate with answer key for directions, not ranks.

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

**Card A — Child: ‘I am God.’** Mistaken: Soul equals the Supreme.  
**Card B — Adult: ‘Science disproved the soul.’** Mistaken: Empiricism settled ātman.  
**Card C — Family neglects hygiene ‘because we are soul.’** Mistaken: Body contempt is spiritual.

Each group writes mistaken conclusion / principle / compassionate response / better action / what not to say. Share 60 seconds.

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Soul Map True/False

1. Teacher reads statements (examples below).
2. Teams hold **T** or **F**, then one sentence why.
3. Four to five rounds; respectful energy; no mocking.

Sample statements:
- The soul is never born. (T)
- The jīva is God Himself. (F)
- Labs prove the soul exists. (F)
- Caring for the body can support service. (T)
- Bodily job labels fully define the person. (F)

### 33–37 min — Diagram task

Complete is/is-not table and sun ← ray sketch. Circle **service relationship**. Write short echo inside. Low-risk examples only.

### 37–40 min — Project contribution + reflection + handoff

1. Project: “Because I am a soul, this week I will live by _______________.”
2. Reflection: How do BG 2.20 and BG 15.7 together block both nihilism and “I am God”?
3. Cleanup; reunification sheets ready.
4. Parent handoff: daily phrase + one care act as service readiness; keep science deferral ready.

## Extension (if group finishes early)

# C1-W3 Parent Guide

## Essential question
If I am a soul, how should I live?

## Source
BG 2.20 — teaching meaning: The soul is never born and never dies; unborn, eternal, and everlasting — not destroyed when the body is destroyed.

## Discussion
One realistic household tension; no compelled confession.

## Application
5–15 minute cue; minimum version counts.

## Privacy

# C1-W3 Older Teacher Guide (Grades 4–5)

## Objective

Students explain: **The jīva is eternal, conscious, individual, minute, and related to Kṛṣṇa in service — not God Himself.** using BG 2.20.

## Essential question

If I am a soul, how should I live?

## Text-observation task (10 min)

Open https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/20/.  
Students write:
1. Who is speaking / what is the setting (if known)?  
2. One phrase that means "this continues" or "this is temporary/rare/eternal" depending on week.  
3. One sentence paraphrase in their own words.  
4. One thing this verse does **not** say.

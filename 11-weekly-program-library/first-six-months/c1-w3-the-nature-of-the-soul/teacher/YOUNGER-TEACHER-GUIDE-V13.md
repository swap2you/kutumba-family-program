## Objective

Children experience that they are an **eternal soul** — conscious, individual, and meant for **Kṛṣṇa’s service** — not God Himself. They take home a “spark serves the sun” cue card and one care-as-service act.

## Exact memory phrase

> I am an eternal soul — conscious, individual, and meant for Kṛṣṇa's service.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** The soul is never born and never dies; unborn, eternal, and everlasting — not destroyed when the body is destroyed.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** You are a tiny bright spark meant to love and serve Kṛṣṇa. Sparks are not the whole sun. We still brush teeth and sleep — caring for the body helps us serve.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**  
  - “I am God.”  
  - “Science proved there is no soul.”  
  - “Bodies don’t need care because we are soul.”
- **Do not:** invent dialogue; use violence in Jaḍa Bharata retelling; shame a child who says “I am God”; claim lab proof of ātman.

## Materials

- Yellow “sun” circle and smaller “spark/ray” cutouts (one spark per child)
- Soft house picture or toy house + small figure (resident demo)
- Card stock for spark-service cards
- Crayons; printed line-art if available
- Soft toss object; calm-corner chair

## Room setup

Circle; sun poster at center; spark cutouts ready; aisles clear for Spark-and-Serve movement.

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome; feet on floor.
2. Show sun + one spark: “Same light-nature — spark is not the sun.”
3. Echo memory phrase twice (break into beats): *I am an eternal soul — / conscious, individual — / meant for Kṛṣṇa's service.*
4. Briefly recall W2: body changes; self continues — today we learn what kind of self.

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Last week we learned: bodies change; the conscious self continues. Today we ask: what kind of self am I?  
> Kṛṣṇa teaches that the soul is never born and never dies. When the body is finished, the soul is not destroyed.  
> The living being is like a ray of the sun — sharing light-nature, but not the whole sun. We are tiny parts meant to serve Kṛṣṇa, not to become the Supreme.  
> Long ago, a wise person named Jaḍa Bharata helped a king see that labels about bodies — strong, weak, important jobs — do not define the soul. We tell that gently, without scary details.  
> If someone says “I am God,” we smile and say: “You are a dear spark meant to serve, not the whole sun.” And we still care for our house — our body — so we can serve with energy.

**Story boundaries:** BG 2.20 + BG 15.7 fragmental part; Jaḍa Bharata / ŚB 5.10 gentle nonviolent paraphrase only. No invented quotes. No “science proves soul.”

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. Is the spark the whole sun? (**No**)
2. What is the soul meant for? (**Kṛṣṇa’s service**)
3. Name one way to care for the body so we can serve. (sleep / water / brush)

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Spark and Serve (steps)

1. Children stand in open space holding imaginary sparks (open palms).
2. Teacher calls **Spark!** — children wiggle fingers (alive, conscious).
3. Teacher calls **Not the sun!** — point to sun poster; shake head gently; whisper “I serve.”
4. Teacher calls **Serve!** — take three gentle steps toward a “service mat” (help pile: toy tidy, or kindness mat) and sit.
5. Three rounds. Round three: show cards (A) “I am God” (B) “I am a spark who serves.” Point to B on Serve.
6. End seated; echo memory phrase once.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Spark-service cue card (steps)

1. Fold card stock; stick or draw a yellow spark on the front.
2. Inside, write: **I am an eternal soul — conscious, individual, and meant for Kṛṣṇa's service.** (or short: *Dear spark — I serve Kṛṣṇa.*)
3. On the back: draw one service act (help set plate, soft voice, tidy toys) AND one body-care act.
4. Practice: touch spark → say “not the sun” → say “I serve.”
5. Take-home placement: near toothbrush or dinner plate.

Full steps also in `activities/v13-younger/`.

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

Color line-art if time; else keep spark card.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

- **Feet on floor** — “Spark feet on the mat.”
- **Kind words** — “Serve voice.”
- **I am God boast** — “Smile — dear spark who serves.”
- **Quiet reset** — calm corner with sun picture; rejoin on Serve.

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

> Memory: “I am an eternal soul — conscious, individual, and meant for Kṛṣṇa's service.” Home cue: once daily — say the phrase and do one care act as service readiness. Labs measure bodies; soul teaching comes from śāstra.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| “I am God” | “Dear spark who serves.” | Repeat gently |
| Body neglect talk | “Care helps us serve.” | Name one care act |
| Running | “Spark walk.” | Walk |
| Shutdown | “Quiet reset okay.” | Calm corner 60 sec |

## Backup low-prep

Soft toss: catch → say “spark,” “serve,” or “care.” Three phrase echoes. Optional: place spark cutouts on sun, then take one home.

## What success looks like

- Child can say spark ≠ sun.
- Child can echo (or finish) memory phrase.
- Child can name one service + one care act.

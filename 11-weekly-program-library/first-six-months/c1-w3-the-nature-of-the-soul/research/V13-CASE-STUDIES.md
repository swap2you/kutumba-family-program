# C1-W3 Case Studies

### Constructed case 1 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** A child proudly says, 'I am God.'
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Soul equals the Supreme.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 15.7 — eternal fragmental part; BG 2.20 — eternal individual self. — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/15/7/
- **Compassionate response:** Smile; correct gently: we are tiny parts meant to serve, not the whole.
- **Family action:** Practice the sentence: 'I am a soul who serves Kṛṣṇa.'
- **What not to say:** You're blasphemous. Shame the child publicly.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

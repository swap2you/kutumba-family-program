# C1-W3 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** The Nature of the Soul  
**Primary:** BG 2.20 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/20/  
**Memory phrase:** I am an eternal soul — conscious, individual, and meant for Kṛṣṇa's service.

---

## Objective

Children show spark ≠ sun, echo the memory phrase, and take home a spark-service card with one care act and one service act.

## Core activity — Sun and Spark (5–8 min)

1. Place large yellow sun at center.
2. Give each child a small spark/ray cutout.
3. Say together: “Same light-nature — not the whole sun.”
4. Children place sparks near sun, then hold one spark to heart: “I serve Kṛṣṇa.”
5. Three echoes of memory phrase.

## Story boundary

Paraphrase only from younger teacher guide (BG 2.20; spark/sun; gentle Jaḍa Bharata labels). No invented dialogue. No violence. No “I am God” affirmation. No science-proof claims.

## Movement — Spark and Serve

1. **Spark!** = wiggle fingers.
2. **Not the sun!** = point to sun; whisper “I serve.”
3. **Serve!** = three steps to service mat; sit.
4. Three rounds; round three choose “spark who serves” card over “I am God” card.

## Craft — Spark-service cue card (step-by-step)

1. Fold card stock; draw/stick yellow spark on front.
2. Inside: write memory phrase or short echo *Dear spark — I serve Kṛṣṇa.*
3. Back left: draw one **service** act (help, soft voice, tidy).
4. Back right: draw one **care** act (sleep, water, brush).
5. Practice: spark → “not the sun” → “I serve.”
6. Take home near toothbrush or dinner plate.

## Backup (low prep)

Soft toss: “spark,” “serve,” or “care.” Close with phrase.

## Materials

Sun poster, spark cutouts, card stock, crayons, soft toss object, line art optional.

## Parent note

Home: say phrase once daily + one care act as service readiness. Labs measure bodies/behavior; soul teaching is from śāstra — not lab proof. Snack/water only at program; no ranking.

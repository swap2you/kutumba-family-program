# C1-W3 · Y02 · Spark and Sun Match

**Print:** US Letter · K–2  
**Mechanic:** Classification map (soul is / is-not)  
**Primary bridge:** BG 2.20  
**URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/20/

---

## Teaching purpose

Match spark → sun with the limit: same light-nature, not the whole sun.

## How to run

1. Show sun poster and spark cutouts.
2. Children place sparks near sun.
3. Say: same light-nature — not the whole sun.
4. Whisper: I serve Kṛṣṇa.

## Materials

Sun poster; spark cutouts; soft toss object.

## Age boundary

K–2

## Success

Child can say spark is not the sun.

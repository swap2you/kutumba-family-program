1. Accept faithful paraphrase of never born/never dies; not destroyed when body destroyed.
2. **fragmental** (eternal fragmental parts — BG 15.7).

6. Sleep, hygiene, food, medicine, etc. as service readiness.

## Section B — scenario directions

- Mistaken: Soul = Supreme.
- Principle: BG 15.7 fragmental part; BG 2.20 eternal individual self.
- Response: Smile; gentle spark/sun correction.
- Do not: public shame / “blasphemer” labeling of a child.

### Science dismissal
- Mistaken: Science disproved the soul.
- Principle: Different domains.
- Response: Agree labs measure physical processes; no false lab proof tonight.
- Do not: “Scientists are demons”; “never ask questions.”

### Hygiene neglect
- Mistaken: Body contempt is spiritual.
- Principle: Resident cares for house; care without identity confusion.
- Action: One body-care act as service this week.
- Do not: “Ignore doctors”; “only weak people rest.”

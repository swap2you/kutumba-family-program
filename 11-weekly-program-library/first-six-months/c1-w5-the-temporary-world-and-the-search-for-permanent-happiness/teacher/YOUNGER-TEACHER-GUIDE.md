# C1-W5 Younger Teacher Guide (K–2)

**Week:** The Temporary World and the Search for Permanent Happiness  
**Primary:** BG 8.15 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/15/  
**Session length:** 40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)  
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10  
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

---

## Objective

Children experience that **temporary joys** can be received with **gratitude**, while **lasting fulfillment** is in Kṛṣṇa. They make a sparkler/lamp thank-you card — without shaming toys or hugs.

## Exact memory phrase

> Temporary joys can be used with gratitude; lasting fulfillment is in Kṛṣṇa.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** Having attained the Lord, great souls do not return again to this temporary world of misery; they have reached the highest perfection.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** Toys and parties are like sparklers — bright and short. Kṛṣṇa is like a steady lamp. We say thank you for sparklers; we look for lasting light in Kṛṣṇa. Hugs and fun are not “bad.”
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**  
  - “Toys and family love are worthless.”  
  - “The next buy will finally make me happy forever.”  
  - “Science proves detachment / proves Kṛṣṇa.”
- **Do not:** invent deity dialogue; shame a child’s joy; promise identical Dhruva results; treat BG 5.22 as the locked primary (support only).

## Materials

- Sparkler picture + lamp picture (safe drawings — no real sparklers indoors)
- Small offering-plate prop or paper plate
- Card stock for Thank-You / Lamp cards
- Crayons; soft toss object; line-art if available
- Calm-corner chair

## Room setup

Circle; sparkler vs lamp cards visible; space for Sparkle-and-Still movement.

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome; feet on floor.
2. Show sparkler card then lamp card: “Brief flash / lasting light.”
3. Echo memory phrase twice (two halves): *Temporary joys with gratitude; / lasting fulfillment in Kṛṣṇa.*
4. Ask: “What is one fun thing you enjoyed this week?” Affirm; add “Thank You, Kṛṣṇa” together once.

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Kṛṣṇa teaches that great souls who attain the Lord do not return again to this temporary world of misery — they reach the highest perfection.  
> “Temporary world of misery” does **not** mean birthdays are bad or parents’ hugs are worthless. It means temporary things cannot be our final forever home.  
> A sparkler is fun — then it fades. A lamp keeps shining. We can enjoy a sparkler and still remember the lamp.  
> Sometimes people think the next toy will finally finish wanting. Wanting can keep growing — like drinking salty water in a story picture (not a doctor claim). Better: enjoy, say thank you, and remember Kṛṣṇa.  
> Dhruva’s story shows mixed wishes can turn toward the Lord — we do not promise the same results to every child. Tonight we practice: gratitude for temporary joys; lasting fulfillment in Kṛṣṇa.

**Story boundaries:** BG 8.15 primary; BG 5.22 / 9.27 support only; Dhruva paraphrase without result guarantees. No shame. No invented quotes.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. Are hugs and toys worthless? (**No** — receive with thanks)
2. Can a sparkler be our forever home? (**No**)
3. Whom do we remember for lasting fulfillment? (**Kṛṣṇa**)

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Sparkle and Still (steps)

1. Children stand in open space.
2. Teacher calls **Sparkle!** — gentle finger wiggles for three seconds (temporary joy).
3. Teacher calls **Thank You!** — hands together; whisper “Thank You, Kṛṣṇa.”
4. Teacher calls **Still lamp!** — freeze tall; whisper second half of memory phrase.
5. Three rounds. Round three: show two endings — (A) grab next toy with angry want; (B) enjoy + thanks. Point to B on Still lamp.
6. End seated; full phrase once.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Thank-You / Lamp card (steps)

1. Fold card stock.
2. Outside left: draw a sparkler or favorite toy; label **Thanks**.
3. Outside right: draw a simple lamp; label **Kṛṣṇa**.
4. Inside: write memory phrase (helper may write) or short: *Thanks for sparklers; lamp is Kṛṣṇa.*
5. Back: child draws one temporary joy + one offering/thanks moment (plate, prayer hands).
6. Practice: touch Thanks → Lamp while saying the phrase.

Full steps in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md`.

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

Line-art if time; else keep card.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

- **Toy shame from peer** — “Joy with thanks is okay.”
- **Grabby want** — “Sparkle, then Thank You.”
- **Running** — “Still lamp bodies.”
- **Quiet reset** — calm corner with lamp card.

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

> Memory: “Temporary joys can be used with gratitude; lasting fulfillment is in Kṛṣṇa.” Home cue: before one fun thing, say “Thank You, Kṛṣṇa.” Do not shame toys. Gratitude research ≠ proof of Kṛṣṇa.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Shaming joy | “Thanks is allowed.” | Soft rewrite |
| Grabby spiral | “Sparkle → Thank You.” | Pause + thanks |
| Running | “Still lamp.” | Freeze |
| Shutdown | “Quiet reset okay.” | Calm corner 60 sec |

## Backup low-prep

Soft toss: catch → say “thanks,” “lamp,” or “Kṛṣṇa.” Three phrase echoes. Optional: pass paper plate; each child “offers” a word of thanks.

## What success looks like

- Child can show Thanks vs Lamp.
- Child can echo memory phrase (or halves).
- Child does not treat affection as sinful.

## Boundaries

- Snack/water at 3:30; no weekly meal.
- Primary locked: **BG 8.15** (not BG 5.22).
- No ranking; no forced confession of purchase habits.

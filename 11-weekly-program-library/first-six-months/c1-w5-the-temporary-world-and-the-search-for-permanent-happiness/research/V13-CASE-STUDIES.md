# C1-W5 Case Studies

### Constructed case 1 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** After each weekend, the family chases the next purchase for a happiness spike.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** The next thing will finally satisfy.
- **Relevant principle / source:** BG 8.15 lasting shelter; BG 5.22 temporary contact pleasure. — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/15/
- **Compassionate response:** Pause with gratitude before buying; name what already blesses the home.
- **Family action:** One gratitude sentence before any non-essential purchase this week.
- **What not to say:** You're materialistic garbage. Never enjoy anything.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

### Constructed case 2 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** A parent shames a child's joy in a toy.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Material joy is sinful; affection is worthless.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Affection is not worthless; temporary joys can be received with thanks and offering mood (BG 9.27). — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/9/27/
- **Compassionate response:** Allow lawful joy; add a simple offering/thank-you to Kṛṣṇa.
- **Family action:** Toy joy + one sentence: 'Thank You, Kṛṣṇa.'
- **What not to say:** Throw it away to prove detachment.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

### Constructed case 3 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** Work always defeats prayer time in family conflicts.

- **Relevant principle / source:** Temporary world framing — protect one non-negotiable spiritual cue. — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/15/

- **Family action:** Calendar a non-negotiable 5–10 minute cue.
- **What not to say:** Quit your job tonight or you're not devoted.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

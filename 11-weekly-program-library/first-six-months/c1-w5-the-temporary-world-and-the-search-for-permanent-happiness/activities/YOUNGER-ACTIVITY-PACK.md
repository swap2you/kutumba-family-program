# C1-W5 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** The Temporary World and the Search for Permanent Happiness  
**Primary:** BG 8.15 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/15/  
**Memory phrase:** Temporary joys can be used with gratitude; lasting fulfillment is in Kṛṣṇa.

---

## Objective

Children enjoy temporary joys with thanks, point to lasting fulfillment in Kṛṣṇa, and take home a Thank-You / Lamp card — without shaming toys or hugs.

## Core activity — Sparkler vs Lamp (5–8 min)

1. Show sparkler picture: “Bright — then done.”
2. Show lamp picture: “Steady light.”
3. Children enjoy a clap-sparkle for three seconds, then freeze for lamp.
4. Say together: “Thank You, Kṛṣṇa” after naming one fun thing.
5. Three echoes of memory phrase (may split into two halves).

## Story boundary

Paraphrase BG 8.15 from younger teacher guide; supports BG 5.22 / 9.27 only as helpers. No invented dialogue. No shame. No promising Dhruva-identical results. No real sparklers indoors.

## Movement — Sparkle and Still

1. **Sparkle!** = finger wiggles (3 sec).
2. **Thank You!** = hands together; whisper thanks.
3. **Still lamp!** = freeze; whisper lasting-fulfillment half.
4. Three rounds; choose enjoy+thanks card over angry-want card.

## Craft — Thank-You / Lamp card (step-by-step)

1. Fold card stock.
2. Left: draw sparkler or favorite toy; label **Thanks**.
3. Right: draw lamp; label **Kṛṣṇa**.
4. Inside: memory phrase or *Thanks for sparklers; lamp is Kṛṣṇa.*
5. Back: one temporary joy + one thanks/offering moment.
6. Practice Thanks → Lamp with the phrase.
7. Take home near toy shelf or snack plate.

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` if present; else color card.

## Backup (low prep)

Soft toss: “thanks,” “lamp,” “Kṛṣṇa.” Close with phrase. Optional: pass paper plate for one thank-you word each.

## Materials

Sparkler/lamp pictures, card stock, crayons, paper plate prop, soft toss object, line art optional.

## Parent note

Home: before one fun thing, say “Thank You, Kṛṣṇa.” Do not shame toys or affection. Locked primary is BG 8.15. Gratitude research may support habits — not proof of Kṛṣṇa. Snack/water only at program; no ranking.

# C1-W5 Older Answer Key (Teacher Only)

**Week:** The Temporary World and the Search for Permanent Happiness  
**Primary:** BG 8.15 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/15/  
**Do not distribute to students during first attempt. Not a ranking tool.**

---

## Section A — expected directions

1. Faithful paraphrase: attaining the Lord; no return to temporary misery; highest perfection.
2. **Locked primary:** BG 8.15 (BG 5.22 and 9.27 are supports only).
3. Examples: toys, birthdays, lawful fun, family hugs — received with thanks.
4. Affection/lawful joy are not worthless; temporary things simply cannot be final shelter.
5. Any sincere pause: name blessing / thank Kṛṣṇa / ask if needed before buying.
6. Research may support well-being habits (gratitude); cannot prove Kṛṣṇa or BG 8.15 metaphysics.

## Section B — scenario directions

### Purchase chase
- Mistaken: Next thing finally satisfies.
- Action: Gratitude pause before non-essential purchase.
- Do not: “Materialistic garbage”; “never enjoy.”

### Shame toy joy
- Mistaken: Material joy sinful; affection worthless.
- Action: Allow lawful joy + “Thank You, Kṛṣṇa.”
- Do not: Force throw-away to prove detachment.

### Work defeats prayer
- Mistaken: Career alone is permanent security.
- Action: Protect one non-negotiable spiritual cue; honor livelihood.
- Do not: “Quit your job tonight.”

## Section C — diagram

Coherent Temporary → Gratitude → Lasting chain; short echo in lasting cell.

## Section D

**G** = thanks, offering mood, lasting shelter. **S** = seize forever claim or shame joy.

## Section E–F

Sincere cue + unashamed temporary joy. Reflection hits both extremes.

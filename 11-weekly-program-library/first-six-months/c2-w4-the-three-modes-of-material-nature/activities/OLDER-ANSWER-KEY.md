## Primary

- **Reference:** BG 14.5  
- **URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/14/5/  
- **Secure paraphrase target (KUTUMBA teaching meaning):** Goodness, passion, and ignorance — born of material nature — bind the eternal embodied self within the body.

## Conclusion students should reach

Recognize the three modes as changing influences that shape mood, speech, and choices; describe conditions, not people; use clarity to support hearing; refuse pride and food policing.

## Short-answer targets

1. **Teaching meaning:** Must match the secure paraphrase above (minor wording OK).  
2. **Goodness binds:** Attachment to happiness/knowledge; pride possible (BG 14.6 sense).  
3. **BG 14.26:** Unfailing devotion transcends modes—redirect from superiority to bhakti.  
4. **Reset:** Any concrete remove/add/move earlier/simplify that serves chanting/reading.  
5. **Reunification:** Mentions changing influences + no person-labeling + hearing/practice.

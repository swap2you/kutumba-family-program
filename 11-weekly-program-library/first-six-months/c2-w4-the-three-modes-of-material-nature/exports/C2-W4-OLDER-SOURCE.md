# C2-W4 Older Teacher Guide (Grades 4–5)

**Track length:** ~40 minutes (Saturday 2:35–3:10)  
**Essential question:** Which mode is shaping this moment?  
**Primary:** BG 14.5 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/14/5/  
**Conclusion students should state:** Modes are changing influences that bind the embodied self; we describe conditions, not people; clarity serves bhakti, not pride.

---

## Objective

Students will (1) observe BG 14.5 on VedaBase and paraphrase the KUTUMBA teaching meaning, (2) sort scenario cards into sattva/rajas/tamas *tendencies* without labeling persons, (3) complete a diagram and worksheet, and (4) write one reunification sentence plus a project contribution idea.

## Teacher background

- Teaching meaning: Goodness, passion, and ignorance — born of material nature — bind the eternal embodied self within the body.
- Supports: BG 14.6–14.9 (tendencies + goodness trap), 14.17 (fruits), 14.26 (bhakti transcends), 17.8–10 only as non-policing food-tendency footnote if asked.
- Misconception to interrupt immediately: using modes to shame siblings/classmates/other families.

## Materials

- Devices or printed screenshot of https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/14/5/ (teacher-controlled)
- `activities/OLDER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` printables / mode-sort cards
- `activities/OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md` (teacher only)

## Minute-by-minute

### 0–5 — Hook

Show three evening captions (calm ordered / multi-screen rush / neglected dull). Ask: “Same family—what changed?” Collect condition words only. Post essential question.

### 5–12 — Primary text observation

Students open or view BG 14.5 and complete four lines in notebooks:

1. **Setting:** Kṛṣṇa teaching Arjuna in the Gītā (Chapter 14).
2. **Key Sanskrit/IAST phrase they notice** (e.g., *guṇāḥ prakṛti-sambhavāḥ* or *nibadhnanti*).
3. **Paraphrase (KUTUMBA teaching meaning):** Goodness, passion, and ignorance — born of material nature — bind the eternal embodied self within the body.
4. **What it does *not* say:** That we should nickname people as modes.

Teacher checks paraphrases against answer key; corrects invented verse numbers.

### 12–18 — Worksheet questions (from activity pack)

Students answer:

- One tendency of each mode (from BG 14.6–9 / 14.17 sense).
- Why goodness can still bind (BG 14.6).
- How BG 14.26 redirects pride.

### 18–26 — Scenario cards + mode_sort puzzle

In pairs, sort six short scenarios into three columns: clarifying / restless-craving / dull-covering.  
**Rule card on table:** “Sort the *situation*, not the person.”  
Teacher circulates; flips any “Rahul is tamas” talk back to condition language.  
One group shares a sort aloud; class may challenge kindly with evidence from tendencies.

### 26–32 — Diagram task

On the concept diagram, label:

- Three modes
- “Bind the embodied self”
- Arrow to “bhakti transcends (BG 14.26)”
- Red “X” on “label people”

### 32–36 — Project contribution + reflection

- Write one evening redesign: remove / add / move earlier / simplify.
- Reflection sentence: “An analogy helps me picture modes, but scripture is the authority because…”
- Extension (fast finishers): How would food-tendency talk stay kind under BG 17.8–10 without policing allergies/budgets?

### 36–40 — Reunification sentence + cleanup

Each student drafts one sentence to share upstairs, e.g.:  
“Modes change; we reset the room so we can hear, without calling people names.”  
Stack materials; teacher keeps answer key private.

## Answer key pointer

Use `activities/OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md` for paraphrase targets and reject lists. Do not distribute the key to students.

## Behavior / speech ethics

- Interrupt mode-labeling of peers in one calm sentence.
- No confession pressure about home mess.
- Privacy: no ranking families’ “scores.”

## Backup if tech fails

Teacher reads IAST aloud from verse card; students write paraphrase from dictation; card sort continues offline.

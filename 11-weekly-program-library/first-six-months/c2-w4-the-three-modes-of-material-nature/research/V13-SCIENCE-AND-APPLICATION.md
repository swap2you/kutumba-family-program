**Doctrine first:** The three modes (*guṇas*) as ontological features of material nature are taught by śāstra (BG 14.5ff). Empirical research does **not** prove or disprove the guṇas, the ātman, or bhakti metaphysics.

## Option A — Careful attention / environment citation (habits lane only)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The Cost of Interrupted Work: More Speed and Stress |
| Authors | Gloria Mark, Daniela Gudith, Ulrich Klocke |
| Year | 2008 |
| Venue | CHI 2008 (ACM Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems) |
| DOI / URL | https://doi.org/10.1145/1357054.1357072 |
| Finding (narrow) | In a controlled email-task experiment, interrupted work was associated with higher stress, frustration, time pressure, and effort, even when people compensated by working faster. |
| Limitation | Lab office-task context; not a study of families, chanting, or Vedic ontology. Does **not** measure or validate sattva/rajas/tamas. Individual differences appeared; results are not a universal law for every household. |
| Application to C2-W4 | Supports a *practical* reason to reduce multi-source interruption before family chanting/reading (Clarity Before Chanting / five-minute reset). Use only as environment-design wisdom—never as “science proves the modes.” |
| Facilitator sentence | “Research on interruptions can motivate quieter setups; our teaching that modes bind the embodied self rests on Bhagavad-gītā 14.5, not on CHI papers.” |

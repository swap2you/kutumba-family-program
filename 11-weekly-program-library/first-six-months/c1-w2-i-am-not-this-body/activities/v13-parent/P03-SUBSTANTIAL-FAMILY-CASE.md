## Case (fictional)

A middle-school child comes home upset after a relative joked about their height at a gathering. A sibling laughs and repeats the joke at dinner. One parent says “toughen up.” Another parent freezes and says nothing.

## Facilitator answer direction (teacher-only)

- Mistaken: body jokes are harmless; silence is enough; toughness fixes hurt.  
- Principle: body changes; conscious person continues; speech must honor dignity.  
- Response: stop the repeat; affirm the child; privately coach sibling; prepare warm relative redirect.  
- Action: speech pledge + nightly memory-line cue.  
- Do not say: toughen up; spiritual people ignore feelings; announce stats.

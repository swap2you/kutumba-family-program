# C1-W2 · O02 · Life-Stage Evidence Timeline

**Print:** US Letter · one per student · Grades 4–5  
**Mechanic:** Timeline / sequence  
**Note:** Timeline is pedagogy about change and continuity — **not** soul-proof.

---

## Instructions

Place three stages on the timeline. Under each, write one **body change**. Under the arrow, write what **continues**.

```
CHILDHOOD  ----->  YOUTH  ----->  ELDER
   |                  |              |
 change:           change:        change:
 ________          ________       ________

What continues across all three?
________________________________________________________________
```

## Prompts

1. Name two body changes everyone can recognize without shame.  
2. Why is this timeline helpful for teaching BG 2.13?  
3. Why is this timeline **not** proof of the soul?

## Teacher key (direction)

- Changes: height, teeth, hair, strength, wrinkles (keep respectful).  
- Continues: conscious “I” / person who can choose kind speech and love Kṛṣṇa (age-appropriate).  
- Not proof: ontology rests on śāstra (BG 2.13+), not drawings.

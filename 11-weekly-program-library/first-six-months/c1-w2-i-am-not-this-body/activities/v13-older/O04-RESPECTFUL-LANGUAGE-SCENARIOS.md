**Print:** US Letter · one scenario per group · Grades 4–5

## Scenario A — Sibling appearance tease at snack

Mistaken line often heard: “Just a joke.”

Write:
1. Mistaken conclusion  
2. Principle from BG 2.13 (one sentence)  
3. Compassionate response  
4. Better family action  
5. One sentence **not** to say

## Teacher key (direction)

| Scenario | Mistaken | Principle | Response | Action | Do not say |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Jokes are harmless | Dignity; body changes; person continues | Stop; affirm; repair | Speech pledge | Toughen up / just a joke |
| B | Body project = self | Care ≠ identity | Affirm health; separate “I care” from “I am only” | Keep routine + memory line/service | Fitness is māyā / body doesn’t matter |
| C | Public stats OK | Privacy + dignity | Warm redirect; protect child | Agree redirect line | Announce stats / angry lecture in front of child |

Source for principle: https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/13/

# C1-W2 · O03 · Identity Statement Sort (Self vs Body Labels)

**Print:** US Letter · cut cards · Grades 4–5  
**Mechanic:** Card sort (distinct content from W1 Is/Is-Not)

---

## Mats

1. **SELF / CONTINUES**  
2. **BODY / CHANGES**  
3. **CARE DUTY** (body care that remains good)

## How to run

1. Sort S/B/C cards first.  
2. Hold X cards for whole-group discussion.  
3. Say limits aloud.

## Teacher key

As table. X cards are misconceptions to block, not sorted as true.

# C1-W2 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** I Am Not This Body  
**Primary:** BG 2.13 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/13/  
**Memory phrase:** My body changes; I continue as the conscious self.

---

## Objective

Children show body-change vs continuing “I,” practice kind body-speech, and take home a Care / Self cue card.

## Core activity — Changing Garments Demo (5–8 min)

1. Teacher wraps a soft scarf on a stuffed animal, then changes it for another scarf.
2. Ask: “Did the friend inside disappear when the scarf changed?” Children: No.
3. Show child → youth → elder picture cards: bodies change; person continues.
4. Practice kind line together: “We care for bodies. We don’t tease bodies.”
5. Three echoes of the memory phrase.

## Story boundary

Paraphrase only from the younger teacher guide (Arjuna’s grief context → BG 2.13 stages; garment support). No invented deity dialogue. No graphic violence. No body-contempt language. No claiming photos prove the soul.

## Movement — Grow and Freeze

1. Children walk slowly.
2. **Grow!** = stretch tall.
3. **Freeze!** = whisper memory phrase.
4. **Kind hands!** = hand on heart / friendly wave.
5. Three rounds; on round three point to kind-speech card (not tease card).

## Craft — Care / Self cue card (step-by-step)

1. Fold card stock.
2. Left outside: small body outline labeled **Care**.
3. Right outside: heart or “I” labeled **Self**.
4. Inside: write memory phrase (helper may write).
5. Back: draw one care act (sleep, water, brush) or one kind speech bubble.
6. Practice: touch Care → Self while saying the phrase.
7. Take home; place near mirror or toothbrush.

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` if present; else color the cue card.

## Backup (low prep)

Pass soft object. Catch → say “change,” “continue,” or “kind.” Close with memory phrase.

## Materials

Line art (optional), crayons, card stock, scarf, stuffed animal, stage picture cards, soft toss object.

## Parent note

Home practice: once daily — notice one body change or one kind body-speech; say the memory phrase. Photos/psychology help teaching; they do not prove the soul. Snack/water only at Saturday program; no ranking.

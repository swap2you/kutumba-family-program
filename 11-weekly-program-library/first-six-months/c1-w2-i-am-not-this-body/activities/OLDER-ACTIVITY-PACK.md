## Section A — Text observation (BG 2.13)

**IAST:** dehino ’smin yathā dehe kaumāraṁ yauvanaṁ jarā / tathā dehāntara-prāptir dhīras tatra na muhyati

**KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Just as the embodied self passes through childhood, youth, and old age in one body, the sober person is not bewildered when the self passes to another body.

*(KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not BBT translation.)*

**Source:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/13/

1. List the three body stages named in the teaching meaning.
2. Circle key ideas: embodied self / not bewildered / laboratory proof / appearance ranking. Cross out what does **not** belong.
3. Name two respectful ways to **care** for the body this week.
4. Why can family photos help teaching but **not prove** the soul?
5. Why is “ignore the body — we’re spiritual” **not** this week’s conclusion?
6. Rewrite “It’s just a joke” into a repair sentence after appearance teasing.

## Section A — expected directions

1. **Stages:** Childhood, youth, old age (kaumāraṁ, yauvanaṁ, jarā).
2. **Circle:** embodied self, not bewildered. **Cross out:** laboratory proof, appearance ranking.
3. **Care examples:** sleep, hygiene, food, medicine, exercise as service readiness — not identity.
4. **Photos:** Pedagogy for continuity of “I” across stages; do not prove ātman. Doctrine rests on śāstra.
5. **Not body neglect:** Care for the vehicle; refuse harsh dualism (“body is trash”).
6. **Repair sentence examples:** “I’m sorry I teased your looks. You are a person deserving respect.”

## Section B — scenario directions

### Sibling appearance tease
- Mistaken: “Just a joke.”
- Principle: Dignity; body changes, self continues; speech should be kind.
- Response: Stop gently; affirm both; repair.
- Action: Seven-day no appearance-joke pledge.
- Do not say: “Toughen up”; spiritual threats.

### Fitness-as-self
- Mistaken: “Body project *is* the self.”
- Principle: Care ≠ identity collapse into metrics.
- Response: Affirm health; add non-body practice (memory line / service).
- Do not say: “Fitness is māyā — stop exercising.”

### Public weight question
- Mistaken: “Public body talk is fine.”
- Principle: Privacy + respect; body not public property.
- Response: Warm subject change; protect child.
- Action: Parents pre-agree redirect line.
- Do not say: Announce stats; angry public lecture using child as prop.

## Section C — diagram

Accept coherent chain. Kind-speech cell circled with short echo. Reframe private trauma disclosures privately.

## Section D — forks

**R** = care, dignity, repair, continuity of self. **M** = mockery, proof claims, body contempt, “just a joke” without repair.

## Section E–F

Any sincere speech pledge + care act. Reflection must touch both blocked extremes.

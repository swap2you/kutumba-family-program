# C1-W2 · Y02 · Same-Person / Changing-Body Matching

**Print:** US Letter · cut cards · K–2  
**Mechanic:** Matching  
**Memory:** My body changes; I continue as the conscious self.

---

## Teaching purpose

Match pairs showing the **same person** with a **changed body** (shoes too small; taller; lost tooth) without shame language.

## Card pairs (print)

| Pair | Card A | Card B | Match idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baby shoes | Bigger shoes | Same child; feet grew |
| 2 | Short sleeve last year | Longer arms this year | Same child; body grew |
| 3 | First tooth smile | Missing tooth smile | Same child; body changed |
| 4 | Small bicycle | Bigger bicycle seat | Same rider; body grew |

## How to run

1. Mix eight cards face up.
2. Children find matching pairs.
3. For each match: “Body changed. Person continues. Kind words.”
4. Block: “Funny-looking” jokes — redirect to Care words.

## Materials

Printed cards; tray.

## Teacher key

Pairs as in table above. All matches affirm continuity + kindness.

## Age boundary

No body-shame prompts. No public weighing talk.

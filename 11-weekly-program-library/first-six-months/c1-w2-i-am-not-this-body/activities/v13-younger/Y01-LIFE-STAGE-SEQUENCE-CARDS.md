# C1-W2 · Y01 · Life-Stage Picture Sequence Cards

**Print:** US Letter · cut on borders · K–2  
**Mechanic:** Sequence / timeline (distinct from W1 Four Corners)  
**Primary bridge:** BG 2.13 stages — childhood, youth, old age  
**URL:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/13/

---

## Teaching purpose

Children order respectful drawings of **child → youth → elder** and say: bodies change; the “I” continues. Drawings are pedagogy — **not** proof of the soul.

## How to run

1. Place three cards face down; children help order them left to right.
2. Teacher: “What changed?” (height, hair, face lines — keep gentle).
3. Teacher: “Who is still the same person who can love and choose kind words?”
4. Echo memory phrase once.

## Card content (print these labels)

| Card | Large label | Short line |
|---|---|---|
| Y01-A | CHILD | A smaller body. Growing. |
| Y01-B | YOUTH | A growing body. Changing. |
| Y01-C | ELDER | An older body. Still a person. |

Child can place cards in order and echo that the person continues.

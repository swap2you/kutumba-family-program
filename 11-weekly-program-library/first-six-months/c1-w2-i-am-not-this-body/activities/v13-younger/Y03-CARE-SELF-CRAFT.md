# C1-W2 · Y03 · Care / Self Cue Craft Page

**Print:** US Letter · one per child · K–2  
**Mechanic:** Craft / manipulative  
**Visual:** `visuals/v13/care-self-craft.png`

---

## Teaching purpose

Children make a take-home cue that separates **Care** (body duties) from **Self** (continuing “I”) without teaching body neglect.

## Page content

- Left panel: body outline labeled **Care** — color; draw sleep / water / brush.
- Right panel: heart or “I” labeled **Self** — color.
- Bottom strip: **My body changes; I continue as the conscious self.**
- Back/optional line: one kind speech bubble (“You’re a friend”).

## Steps

1. Color Care and Self panels.
2. Helper writes memory phrase if needed.
3. Practice: touch Care → touch Self while saying the phrase.
4. Take home; place near mirror or toothbrush.

## Materials

Printed craft page; crayons; optional card-stock backing.

## Boundaries

Do not say “body is trash.” Care remains good.

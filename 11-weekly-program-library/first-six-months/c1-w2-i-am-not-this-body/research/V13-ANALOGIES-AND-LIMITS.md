# V13 Analogies and Limits — C1-W2

## 1. Clothes / garments
- Mapping: clothes change; wearer continues.
- Helps: body stages vs continuing self.
- Breaks down: bodies are not fashion props; deserve care; not chosen like outfits for mockery.

## 2. Driver and vehicle
- Mapping: driver cares for car; is not the car.
- Helps: care without identity fusion.
- Breaks down: living bodies are not machines; no body-contempt.

## 3. Baby photo vs today
- Mapping: same person across change.
- Helps: continuity intuition.
- Breaks down: photos do not prove the soul; śāstra teaches ātman.

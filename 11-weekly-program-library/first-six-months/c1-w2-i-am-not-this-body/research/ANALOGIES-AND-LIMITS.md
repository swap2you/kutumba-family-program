# C1-W2 Analogies and Limits

| Analogy | Teaching value | Source status | Failure point / limit | Younger use | Older/adult use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Changing garments | Clothes change; the wearer continues. Helps children grasp body vs self. | pedagogy / śāstra-aligned | Clothes are chosen; bodies are not fashion accessories. Do not teach contempt for the body. | younger object lesson | adult discussion |
| Life-stage photos | Same person across childhood, youth, elder photos — continuity of the 'I'. | pedagogy / śāstra-aligned | Photos are not ontology proof; memory and personality also change in expression. | younger object lesson | adult discussion |
| Driver and vehicle | The operator uses the vehicle; the operator is not the metal. | pedagogy / śāstra-aligned | Avoid harsh dualism ('body is trash'). Care for the vehicle for service. | younger object lesson | adult discussion |

## Rule

Label every analogy as pedagogy. Never present analogy as if it were a śāstra quotation. Facilitators must speak the limit in the same breath as the image.

## Inline requirement

The controlling facilitator guide already inlines these analogies — do not teach from a "see research" stub.

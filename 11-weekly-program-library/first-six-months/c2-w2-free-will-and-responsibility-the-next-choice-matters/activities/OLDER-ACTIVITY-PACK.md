## Section A — Text observation (BG 18.63)

**IAST:** iti te jñānam ākhyātaṁ guhyād guhyataraṁ mayā / vimṛśyaitad aśeṣeṇa yathecchasi tathā kuru

**KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** The Lord has explained confidential knowledge; now deliberate fully and then act as you choose — responsibility remains with the living being.

*(Labeled KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not labeled as BBT translation.)*

**Source:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/63/

1. In your own words, what does this verse ask a person to do **before** acting?
2. Circle three key words in the teaching meaning: deliberate / choose / responsibility / machine / reincarnation. (Cross out the word that does **not** belong to this week’s main point.)
3. Name two things you might **not** fully control in a hard moment.
4. Name one thing you **can** still choose in that same moment.
5. Why is “do whatever you feel” **not** the meaning of BG 18.63?
6. Why is “I had no choice” an incomplete sentence after hearing this verse?

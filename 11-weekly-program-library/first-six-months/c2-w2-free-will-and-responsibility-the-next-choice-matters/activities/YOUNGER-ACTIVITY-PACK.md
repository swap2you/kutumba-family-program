# C2-W2 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** Free Will and Responsibility: The Next Choice Matters  
**Primary:** BG 18.63 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/63/  
**Memory phrase:** The next choice matters.

---

## Objective

Children show red–yellow–green order, echo the memory phrase, and take home a Pause–Remember–Choose cue card.

## Core activity — Traffic-Light Choice Fork

**Time:** 5–8 minutes  

1. Teacher holds a shared toy and two puppets. Puppet A feels grabby; Puppet B freezes.
2. Show **red** card: “Pause — bodies freeze.” Children freeze with Puppet B.
3. Show **yellow** card: “Remember Kṛṣṇa and kind hands.” Children whisper: *The next choice matters.*
4. Show **green** card: Offer two endings on picture cards — (1) snatch and shout, (2) ask / take turns / ask adult for help.
5. Children point to the responsible green ending; practice once with the real toy in a gentle take-turns demo.
6. End with three echoes of the memory phrase.

## Story boundary

Paraphrase only from the Gītā arc used in the younger teacher guide (seeking guidance → deliberate → choose; later clarity to follow instruction). No invented deity dialogue. No graphic violence.

## Movement — Freeze and Remember

1. Children walk slowly in a circle.
2. Teacher claps once = freeze (pause).
3. Teacher claps twice = hand on heart + memory phrase.
4. Two rounds, then sit.

## Craft — Pause–Remember–Choose cue card (step-by-step)

1. Fold card stock into a standing mini-folder.
2. Draw or stick three circles: red (top), yellow (middle), green (bottom).
3. Color each circle.
4. Inside, write or sticker: **The next choice matters.**
5. On the back, draw one green choice (share, soft voice, ask for help).
6. Practice touching red → yellow → green while saying Pause / Remember / Choose.
7. Take home; place near the family’s chosen trigger (toy shelf, homework table, door).

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` on US Letter if present. If missing, children color their cue card only.

## Backup (low prep)

Pass a soft object around the circle. Each child names one kind next choice (“share,” “sorry,” “ask”). Close with the memory phrase.

## Materials

Printed line art (optional), crayons, card stock, red/yellow/green cards, soft toss object, two puppets or stuffed animals, shared toy/block.

## Parent note (glue inside cue card or say at handoff)

Home practice: once daily on your chosen trigger — Pause, Remember, Choose. Snack/water only at the Saturday program; no weekly meal. We do not rank children.

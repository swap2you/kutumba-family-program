## Section A — expected directions

1. **Before acting:** Deliberate / think carefully / consider the knowledge fully (accept close paraphrases).
2. **Circle:** deliberate, choose, responsibility. **Cross out:** reincarnation (next week) and/or machine if they marked machine as a “key teaching-meaning word” — machine is a BG 18.61 image, not the three focal words of 18.63’s teaching meaning. Prefer: cross out **reincarnation** as the clear out-of-scope distractor.
3. **Not fully control (examples):** other person’s words, past events, body adrenaline, traffic, the game invitation already sent, weather, sibling’s first reaction.
4. **Can still choose (examples):** pause, ask for help, kind tone, take turns, start homework for ten minutes, apologize/repair, put the remote down.
5. **Not “do whatever you feel”:** The verse comes after confidential knowledge and requires full deliberation; choice is responsible, not license. BG 18.73 shows alignment with the Lord’s instruction after clarity.
6. **“I had no choice” incomplete:** Limits are real, but BG 18.63 affirms deliberation and action as you choose — responsibility remains with the living being.

## Section B — scenario directions

### Sibling anger
- Mistaken: “Anger made me do it / I had no choice.”
- Pause: freeze hands/voice; breath.
- Remember: Kṛṣṇa, memory phrase, family kind-hands rule.
- Choose: ask / take turns / get adult help.
- Do not say: “You’re evil,” public shame, spiritual threats.

### Homework vs game
- Mistaken: “Easy feeling = right,” or “schedules own me so effort is pointless.”
- Pause: notice urge.
- Remember: duty + verse meaning.
- Choose: minimum focused work block, then timed break.
- Do not say: grade-based ranking in circle; “bad devotee.”

### Repair after a snap
- Mistaken: “Stress erased my agency” or “one failure means quit.”
- Pause: stop escalation.
- Remember: next choice matters; guidance/repair is responsible (BG 2.7 pattern of seeking what is best).
- Choose: brief apology + restored tone + clear next step (without forcing the other person to comfort you).
- Do not say: forced public apology theater; using the child as a prop.

## Section C — diagram

Accept any coherent low-risk chain. **Choice** cell must be circled and contain the short echo. If a student writes a high-risk private disclosure, thank them privately and help reframe to a low-risk example; do not broadcast.

## Section D — forks

Mark **R** when the ending includes pause, deliberation, repair, or duty with kindness. Mark **M** for fatalism (“no choice”) or license (“whatever I feel”).

## Section E–F

Any sincere trigger + next responsible act. Reflection should mention both blocked extremes (license and no-agency).

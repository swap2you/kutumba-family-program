**Week:** Free Will and Responsibility: The Next Choice Matters  
**Primary:** BG 18.63 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/63/  
**Session length:** ~40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)  
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10  
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

---

## Objective

Students observe BG 18.63, explain that deliberation and choice go together with responsibility, map a trigger chain, and contribute one project sentence answering: **What is my next responsible choice?**

## Exact memory phrase

> After giving knowledge, Kṛṣṇa tells Arjuna to deliberate fully and then act as he chooses.

**Short echo:** The next choice matters.

## Teacher background

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** The Lord has explained confidential knowledge; now deliberate fully and then act as you choose — responsibility remains with the living being. (Label as KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not as BBT translation.)
- **Supporting frames (one sentence each):** BG 18.61 machine-of-nature limits; BG 3.34 sense attachment/aversion; BG 2.7 seeking guidance; BG 18.73 willing alignment after clarity.
- **Block:** “Choice = do whatever I feel” and “I have no agency.”
- **Scope:** Do not teach reincarnation ontology (C2-W3) tonight.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

1. Welcome; feet on floor.
2. Hook: “Which sentence is more true — ‘I had no choice’ or ‘I can do whatever I feel’?” Let two volunteers answer; hold both as incomplete.
3. State essential question: What is my next responsible choice?

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation (BG 18.63)

1. Display verse; read IAST aloud together once; teacher reads Devanāgarī slowly or plays authorized audio if available.
2. Students underline three idea-words in the teaching meaning: **deliberate**, **choose**, **responsibility**.
3. Observation prompts (write short answers):
   - What has already been given? (knowledge explained)
   - What must Arjuna do before acting? (deliberate fully)
   - Who remains responsible for action? (the living being / Arjuna as representative learner)
4. Add one-sentence bridges:
   - BG 2.7 — seeking guidance is a responsible move when confused.
   - BG 18.73 — clarity can lead to acting according to the Lord’s instruction.
5. Paraphrase only; no invented dialogue.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A of the older activity pack:
1. Restate KUTUMBA teaching meaning in their own words (one sentence).
2. Name two things a person might **not** control in a hard moment.
3. Name one thing a person **can** still choose.
4. Why is “do whatever you feel” not the meaning of this verse?
5. Why is “I had no choice” incomplete?

Teacher circulates; use `OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md` for expected directions (not for grading ranks).

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

Each group gets one fictional card:

**Card A — Sibling anger:** Urge to shout/snatch. Mistaken line: “Anger made me do it.”  
**Card B — Homework vs game:** Urge to skip work. Mistaken line: “Easy feeling = right choice.”  
**Card C — Repair after a snap:** Adult or older child spoke harshly; now what?

For each card, group writes:
1. What is not fully controlled?
2. What is the Pause–Remember–Choose path?
3. What should we **not** say (shame, ranking, public exposure)?

Share one insight per group (60 seconds).

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Responsible Fork

1. Teacher reads a short fork aloud (two endings).
2. Teams hold up **R** (responsible next step) or **M** (mistaken conclusion).
3. Three rounds, then students explain one “R” choice using the words deliberate or responsibility.
4. Keep energy respectful; no mocking wrong holds.

### 33–37 min — Diagram task

On the choice-map sheet, students fill one low-risk example (lost pencil, long line, noisy sibling — not forced trauma disclosure):

`Event → Body sensation → Thought → Urge → Choice → Consequence`

Circle the **Choice** box and write the memory short echo inside it.

### 37–40 min — Project contribution + reflection + handoff

1. Complete project half-sheet: “This week my next responsible choice at _________ is _________.”
2. Reflection (one sentence): How does BG 18.63 challenge both license and fatalism?
3. Cleanup; bring sheets to reunification.
4. Parent handoff line: Home practice is Pause–Remember–Choose once daily on the family trigger.

## Extension (if group finishes early)

Compare BG 3.34 (sense attachment/aversion) with the traffic-light pause: feelings arise; we need not obey every urge. Still no reincarnation teaching.

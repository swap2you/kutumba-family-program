## Objective

Children experience that feelings can be strong while the **next choice** still matters. They practice a simple **red–yellow–green** pause and take home one cue card for Pause–Remember–Choose.

## Exact memory phrase

> The next choice matters.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** The Lord has explained confidential knowledge; now deliberate fully and then act as you choose — responsibility remains with the living being.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** Kṛṣṇa teaches Arjuna: think carefully, then choose what is right. We do not control everything, but we can choose our next kind step.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**  
  - “I can do whatever I feel.”  
  - “I had no choice.”
- **Do not:** invent Kṛṣṇa–Arjuna dialogue; use scary battle talk; force children to confess home fights; rank children.

## Materials

- Red, yellow, and green cardstock circles (one set per small group)
- Soft puppet or two stuffed animals
- Toy or block for the “shared toy” demo
- Card stock for take-home cue cards
- Crayons / markers
- Printed `visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` if available (US Letter)
- Soft toss object for backup game
- Tape or mini-stands so traffic-light cards can stand

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; calm corner with one chair for quiet reset. Keep aisles clear for movement game.

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome by name; feet on floor; hands in lap.
2. Echo memory phrase three times, clap rhythm: *The next / choice / matters.*
3. Show red card: “Pause.” Yellow: “Remember Kṛṣṇa and kind rules.” Green: “Choose a good next step.”
4. Ask: “Did anyone practice a pause at home?” Accept nods; no forced stories.

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Long ago, Arjuna felt confused about what he should do. He asked Kṛṣṇa to teach him what was best. That asking was already a good choice.  
> Kṛṣṇa taught him many important things. Then Kṛṣṇa told him to think carefully about what he heard, and then to choose how to act.  
> Later, Arjuna’s confusion was gone, and he was ready to follow Kṛṣṇa’s instruction.  
> We also get confused. We do not control every feeling. But after we pause and remember, we can choose the next kind step. The next choice matters.

**Story boundaries:** Paraphrase from BG 2.7, 18.63, and 18.73 themes only. No invented quotes. No gore. No blaming a child as “the bad Arjuna.”

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. When you feel mad or grabby, what is the **red** step?
2. Whom can we remember on the **yellow** step? (Kṛṣṇa; also family kind rules)
3. What is one **green** choice with a brother, sister, or friend?

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Traffic-Light Choice (steps)

1. Spread children in a safe open space.
2. Teacher calls **Red** — everyone freezes (pause).
3. Teacher calls **Yellow** — everyone places hand on heart and whispers memory phrase.
4. Teacher calls **Green** — everyone takes three gentle steps toward the kindness mat / circle center and sits.
5. Play three rounds. On round three, add a choice: show two picture cards (kind share vs harsh snatch). On green, children point to the kind card.
6. End seated; echo memory phrase once.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Pause–Remember–Choose cue card (steps)

1. Give each child a folded card stock “traffic light.”
2. Color top circle red, middle yellow, bottom green (model once).
3. Inside the card, teacher or helper writes: **The next choice matters.**
4. On the back, child draws one green choice (share, soft voice, ask for help).
5. Practice once: hold card, touch red → yellow → green while saying Pause / Remember / Choose.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet. If short on time, skip coloring and keep the cue card as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind words** — “Try your green voice.”
- **Toy rests** — “Toy takes a nap on the shelf.”
- **Quiet reset** — one minute in calm corner with red card; rejoin on yellow whisper.

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Crayons in bin; cards stacked; soft toys away.
2. Each child holds cue card.
3. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:  
   > Memory phrase: “The next choice matters.” Home cue: once daily on your family trigger — Pause, Remember, Choose.
4. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Running | “Red light bodies.” | Freeze, then walk |
| Snatching | “Yellow — remember kind hands.” | Hands to lap |
| Loud shout | “Green voice, please.” | Soft repeat |
| Shutdown | “Quiet reset is okay.” | Calm corner 60 sec |

## Backup low-prep (if craft fails or energy crashes)

1. Sit in a circle with soft toss object.
2. Child catches, says one kind next choice (“share,” “sorry,” “ask”), tosses to next.
3. End with three echoes of the memory phrase.
4. Optional: freeze-and-remember — freeze on clap, say phrase on second clap.

## What success looks like

- Child can show red / yellow / green in order.
- Child can echo: **The next choice matters.**
- Child can name one green action.

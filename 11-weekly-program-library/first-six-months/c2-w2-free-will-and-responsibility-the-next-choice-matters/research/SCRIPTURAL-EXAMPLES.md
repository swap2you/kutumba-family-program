**Week:** Free Will and Responsibility: The Next Choice Matters

## Supporting anchors

| Reference | URL | Teaching paraphrase | Classroom use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BG 18.61 | https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/61/ | The Supreme Lord is situated in the heart and directs the wanderings of living beings seated as on a machine of material energy. | Shows limits on absolute control of circumstance. | Must be held with 18.63 so learners do not conclude zero agency. |
| BG 18.62 | https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/62/ | Surrender unto Him; by His grace one attains peace and the eternal abode. | Links responsible choice to seeking the Lord’s shelter. | Surrender is not fatalism that excuses harmful speech. |
| BG 3.33 | https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/33/ | Even one in knowledge acts according to nature; beings follow their nature. | Names conditioning as real pressure. | Nature does not erase the next responsible choice. |
| BG 3.34 | https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/34/ | Attachment and aversion to sense objects sit in the senses; one should not come under their control. | Supports Pause–Remember–Choose when urges rise. | Do not shame children for having feelings. |
| BG 5.14 | https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/5/14/ | The embodied living being does not create actions, nor induce people to act, nor create fruits of action — these are enacted by the modes. | Prevents “I control every result” pride. | Pair with responsibility language so “nothing matters” does not take root. |
| BG 5.15 | https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/5/15/ | The Lord does not take anyone’s sinful or pious activities; ignorance covers knowledge and bewilders the living beings. | Keeps facilitators from playing God with private karma talk. | No public karma diagnoses. |
| BG 2.7 | https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/7/ | Arjuna, confused about duty, asks Kṛṣṇa to tell him for certain what is best and offers himself as a disciple. | Seeking guidance is itself a responsible choice. | Paraphrase only; no invented dialogue. |
| BG 18.73 | https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/73/ | Arjuna says illusion is gone, memory regained by Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, and he is prepared to act according to the Lord’s instruction. | Shows deliberation can end in willing alignment. | No pressure for dramatic life announcements in the circle. |

## Classroom sequence

Open BG 18.63 URL → state KUTUMBA teaching meaning in full → add 2.7 and 18.73 as the seek → deliberate → align arc → use 18.61 and 3.34 only as limit/urge frames → keep reincarnation for C2-W3.

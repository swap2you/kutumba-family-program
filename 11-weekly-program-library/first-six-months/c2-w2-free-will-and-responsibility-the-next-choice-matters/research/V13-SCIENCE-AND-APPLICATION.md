## Option B — explicit N/A

**N/A — no empirical claim is needed for this week's doctrinal conclusion.**

### Reason

Free will and responsibility as taught from BG 18.63 are doctrinal conclusions from śāstra: after confidential knowledge is explained, the living being is told to deliberate fully and then act as he chooses, so responsibility remains with the living being. Empirical psychology, neuroscience, or habit science cannot prove or disprove ātman agency, Supersoul direction, or the Gītā’s account of minute independence. Therefore this week does not cite a study as evidence for the siddhānta.

### Allowed pedagogical borrowing (non-proof)

Classroom language for **Pause–Remember–Choose** may borrow ordinary habit-design ideas (a cue, a short delay, a planned response) to help families practice. That borrowing is **implementation scaffolding only**. It must never be presented as scientific proof that the soul chooses, that karma is measurable, or that BG 18.63 has been experimentally verified.

### Facilitator line if asked

> Science may help us design a pause habit. It does not prove the soul’s agency. Tonight’s conclusion rests on Bhagavad-gītā 18.63.

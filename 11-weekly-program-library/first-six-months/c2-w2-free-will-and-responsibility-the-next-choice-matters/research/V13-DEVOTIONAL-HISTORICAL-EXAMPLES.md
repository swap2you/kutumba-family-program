## Traceable example 1 — Arjuna’s disciple request (BG 2.7)

- **Source:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/7/
- **Paraphrase:** Facing duty confusion, Arjuna asks Kṛṣṇa to tell him for certain what is best and offers himself as a disciple.
- **Teaching use:** Responsible agency includes seeking guidance when bewildered — not performing confident independence while confused.
- **Limitation:** Paraphrase only; no invented emotional speeches; do not force participants to disclose private crises.

## Traceable example 2 — Deliberation offered, then willing action (BG 18.63 → 18.73)

- **Sources:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/63/ · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/73/
- **Paraphrase:** After confidential knowledge is explained, Arjuna is told to deliberate fully and then act as he chooses; later he states that illusion is gone and he is prepared to act according to the Lord’s instruction.
- **Teaching use:** Freedom and responsibility meet; the “next choice” can be alignment after hearing.
- **Limitation:** Do not stage pressure for families to announce major vows tonight; keep home practice small.

## Traceable example 3 — Little independence theme in the published purport of BG 18.63

- **Source:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/63/ (published purport theme; do not paste full purport into repo handouts)
- **Paraphrase for teachers:** The Gītā’s “as you like, you may act” language is taught as indicating that the Lord does not remove the living entity’s little independence; deliberation precedes acceptance of instruction, and surrender to the Supersoul’s direction is presented as the living being’s best interest.
- **Teaching use:** Supports agency-with-responsibility without making the jīva the Supreme controller.
- **Limitation:** Point learners to VedaBase for personal study; no fabricated lecture anecdotes; no claim that KUTUMBA owns BBT purport text.

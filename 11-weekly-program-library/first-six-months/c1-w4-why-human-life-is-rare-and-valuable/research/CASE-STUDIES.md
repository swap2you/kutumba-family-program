# C1-W4 Case Studies

### Constructed case 1 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** Screens eat the family's only quiet evening block every night.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Entertainment first is always fine.
- **Relevant principle / source:** ŚB 11.9.29 — protect opportunity for inquiry and practice. — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/11/9/29/
- **Compassionate response:** No shame spiral. Name one protected 10-minute block as 'Must.'
- **Family action:** Move one optional screen slot after the spiritual cue.
- **What not to say:** You're wasting your human life forever. Terrify the kids.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

### Constructed case 2 — fictional / anonymized

- **Situation:** A parent uses death scare to make a child chant.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Fear creates lasting urgency.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Compassion and opportunity — not terror. BG 2.40 supports steady endeavor without fear-panic. — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/40/
- **Compassionate response:** Stop fear tactics. Replace with invitation and short joyful practice.
- **Family action:** Rewrite the family ask: 'We get to practice' not 'or else.'
- **What not to say:** If you don't chant you'll be punished / die badly.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one sentence + one action; Older — name the mistaken conclusion explicitly; Adults — connect to home cue.

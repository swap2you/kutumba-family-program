# C1-W4 Analogies and Limits

| Analogy | Teaching value | Source status | Failure point / limit | Younger use | Older/adult use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rare ticket | A hard-to-get ticket is meant to be used, not forgotten in a drawer. | pedagogy / śāstra-aligned | Not a scare tactic; opportunity language, not threat language. | younger object lesson | adult discussion |
| Crossroads sign | Choice of path matters when the road splits. | pedagogy / śāstra-aligned | No species contempt; no mocking other forms of life. | younger object lesson | adult discussion |
| Seed season | Plant while the season is open. | pedagogy / śāstra-aligned | No guaranteed harvest timing; avoid pressuring children with panic. | younger object lesson | adult discussion |

## Rule

Label every analogy as pedagogy. Never present analogy as if it were a śāstra quotation. Facilitators must speak the limit in the same breath as the image.

## Inline requirement

The controlling facilitator guide already inlines these analogies — do not teach from a "see research" stub.

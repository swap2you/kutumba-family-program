# C1-W4 Older Teacher Guide (Grades 4–5)

## Objective

Students explain: **Human life gives a rare opportunity for deliberate self-realization — protect inquiry and practice time.** using SB 11.9.29.

## Essential question

What deserves protected family time?

## Text-observation task (10 min)

Open https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/11/9/29/.  
Students write:
1. Who is speaking / what is the setting (if known)?  
2. One phrase that means "this continues" or "this is temporary/rare/eternal" depending on week.  
3. One sentence paraphrase in their own words.  
4. One thing this verse does **not** say.

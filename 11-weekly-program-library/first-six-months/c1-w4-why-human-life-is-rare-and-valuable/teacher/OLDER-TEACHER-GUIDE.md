# C1-W4 Older Teacher Guide (Grades 4–5)

**Week:** Why Human Life Is Rare and Valuable  
**Primary:** ŚB 11.9.29 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/11/9/29/  
**Session length:** ~40 minutes (parallel track ~2:30–3:10)  
**Parents:** Remain onsite; reunite at 3:10  
**Status:** Internal founding-cohort teaching material — human/temple review EXTERNAL_OPEN

---

## Objective

Students observe ŚB 11.9.29, protect inquiry and practice time without fear tactics, correctly source Nārada–Mṛgāri to **CC Madhya 24.229–282**, complete a Must/Should/Optional time-jar, and answer: **What deserves protected family time?**

## Exact memory phrase

> Human life is a rare chance to ask who I am and serve Kṛṣṇa.

**Short echo:** Rare chance — protect practice time.

## Teacher background

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** After many births one obtains the rare human form — temporary yet able to give the highest purpose. A sober person should quickly endeavor for the ultimate good while this body lasts. (KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not BBT translation.)
- **Supporting frames:** BG 2.40 — little progress not lost (helps five-minute families); CC Madhya 24.229–282 — mercy and authorized instruction transform harmful conduct.
- **Hard source lock:** Mṛgāri is **not** SB 6.x and **not** SB 4.8. Useful Madhya anchors if needed: intro 24.229, name 24.242, bow 24.255–256, careful steps 24.270–272, conclusion 24.278–282 — paraphrase only; no gore.
- **Block:** Fear/death-pressure motivation; entertainment-first as default; busy = successful human life; species contempt.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

1. Welcome; feet on floor.
2. Hook: “Does urgency have to mean fear?” Collect yes/no with one reason each; hold both for the verse.
3. Essential question: What deserves protected family time?

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation (ŚB 11.9.29)

1. Display verse; read IAST aloud together once; Devanāgarī slowly or authorized audio.
2. Students underline: **rare / temporary / highest purpose / endeavor**.
3. Observation prompts (write short answers):
   - What is rare, and what can it give?
   - What should a sober person do while the body lasts?
   - What classroom language do we refuse (terror / death pressure)?
4. Bridges:
   - BG 2.40 — a little sincere endeavor is not lost.
   - Source drill: write **CC Madhya 24.229–282** as the only Mṛgāri source; mark SB 6.x / SB 4.8 as wrong.
5. Paraphrase Madhya mercy arc in one gentle sentence if used — no invented dialogue, no gore.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A:
1. Restate teaching meaning in opportunity language (one sentence).
2. Rewrite a fear-based ask into an invitation ask.
3. Circle rare / purpose / endeavor; cross out terror ranking.
4. Select correct Mṛgāri source.
5. Explain how BG 2.40 helps a five-minute family.
6. Name one Must slot to protect this week.

Circulate with answer key for directions, not ranks.

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

**Card A — Screens eat the only quiet evening block.** Mistaken: Entertainment first is always fine.  
**Card B — Parent uses death scare to force chanting.** Mistaken: Fear creates lasting urgency.  
**Card C — Calendar full; no inquiry time.** Mistaken: Busy equals successful human life.

Each group writes mistaken conclusion / principle / compassionate response / better action / what not to say. Share 60 seconds.

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Source Check + Priority Sort

1. **Round 1 — Source Check:** Teacher reads citations for Mṛgāri. Teams hold **M24** or **WRONG**.
2. **Round 2 — Priority Sort:** Quietly sort activity slips into Must / Should / Optional.
3. One student explains one Must using “rare chance” language (not scare language).
4. Keep energy respectful; no mocking wrong holds.

### 33–37 min — Diagram task

Fill Must / Should / Optional jars. Move one Optional below a Must spiritual cue. Write the short echo inside Must. Low-risk schedule examples only — no forced disclosure of home screen battles.

### 37–40 min — Project contribution + reflection + handoff

1. Project: “This week we protect _______________ before Optional screens.”
2. Reflection: How does ŚB 11.9.29 invite urgency without panic?
3. Cleanup; reunification sheets ready.
4. Parent handoff: Must slot first; invitation language; Mṛgāri = Madhya 24 if discussed.

## Extension (if group finishes early)

Map Madhya 24 mercy arc in three words only: meet → teach → careful life. Still no gore; still no SB mis-citation.

# C1-W4 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** Why Human Life Is Rare and Valuable  
**Primary:** ŚB 11.9.29 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/11/9/29/  
**Memory phrase:** Human life is a rare chance to ask who I am and serve Kṛṣṇa.

---

## Objective

Children treat human life as a rare chance (not a scare), put practice in Must before Optional, and take home a Must-ticket card.

## Core activity — Rare Ticket + Two Jars (5–8 min)

1. Show Must jar and Optional jar.
2. Hold rare ticket; place a “ask & serve” slip in Must.
3. Place a screen/toy slip in Optional.
4. Children help: “Which jar first?” → Must.
5. Three echoes of memory phrase. Chance words only — no scare faces.

## Story boundary

Paraphrase ŚB 11.9.29 and gentle Nārada–Mṛgāri from **CC Madhya 24.229–282 only**. No invented dialogue. No gore. No SB 6.x / SB 4.8 for Mṛgāri. No death pressure.

## Movement — Ticket Walk

1. Walk with imaginary tickets.
2. **Must!** = freeze + phrase + step to Must jar.
3. **Optional!** = soft sit three counts, then stand.
4. **Kind chance!** = hand on heart.
5. Three rounds; choose practice-first picture on round three.

## Craft — Must-ticket card (step-by-step)

1. Cut or fold ticket-shaped card.
2. Front: “RARE TICKET” + simple path/lamp drawing.
3. Inside: memory phrase or *Rare chance — ask & serve.*
4. Back: **Must** box (hearing/phrase) and **Optional** box (toy/screen).
5. Star or sticker in Must.
6. Practice: “Must first, then Optional.”
7. Take home near the family’s evening screen spot.

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` if present; else color ticket.

## Backup (low prep)

Soft toss: “chance,” “ask,” “serve,” “Must.” Close with phrase. Optional: sort picture slips into jars.

## Materials

Ticket card stock, two jars/boxes, picture slips, crayons, soft toss object, line art optional.

## Parent note

Home: one Must spiritual slot before Optional screens. Opportunity language — never death scare. If Mṛgāri is mentioned: CC Madhya 24 only. Snack/water only at program; no ranking.

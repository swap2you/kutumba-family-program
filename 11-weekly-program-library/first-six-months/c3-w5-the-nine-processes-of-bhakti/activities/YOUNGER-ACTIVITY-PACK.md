# C3-W5 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** The Nine Processes of Bhakti
**Primary:** ŚB 7.5.23–24 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/7/5/23-24/
**Memory phrase:** Nine ways to love Viṣṇu.

---

## Objective

Children recognize nine picture processes, walk one door, and say nine ways to love Viṣṇu — offered to Him.

## Core activity — One-Door Walk

**Time:** 5–8 minutes

1. Nine cards around room.
2. Children walk to teacher-chosen one door; act a simple gesture.
3. Say phrase.
4. Return to circle.

## Story boundary

No scare surrender talk. No invented dialogues. No ranking.

## Craft — Addressed heart (step-by-step)

1. Paper heart.
2. Write/ helper: To Kṛṣṇa/Viṣṇu.
3. Draw one process picture.
4. Phrase on back.

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` on US Letter if present. If missing, children color their craft only.

## Backup (low prep)

Toss and name one process picture.

## Materials

- Nine picture cards
- Door mats
- Paper hearts
- Crayons
- Soft toss
- Calm corner
- Phrase stickers

## Parent note (say at handoff)

Home practice: Continue the family's core practice and add one underused process once.. Snack/water only at the Saturday program; no weekly meal. We do not rank children.

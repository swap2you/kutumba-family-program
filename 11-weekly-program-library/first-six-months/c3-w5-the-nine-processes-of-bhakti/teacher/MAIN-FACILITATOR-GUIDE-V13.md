## Two-minute summary

**ŚB 7.5.23–24** lists nine processes of bhakti and concludes that when such devotion is offered to Viṣṇu (*iti puṁsārpitā*), it is topmost learning. Lab: **Nine-Process Bhakti Stations**. Home: continue core practice and add one underused process once. Block requiring mastery of all nine at once and generic virtue without Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa as object.

## Essential question

How do the nine processes map family life — with Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa as the object, not generic virtue?

## Memory line

Hearing, chanting, remembering, serving, worshiping, praying, serving as a servant, friendship and full surrender are processes of pure devotional service when offered to Viṣṇu.

## Exact memory phrase (children echo)

Nine ways to love Viṣṇu.

**KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Hearing, chanting, remembering, serving the Lord's feet, worship, prayer, servitude, friendship, and full surrender — nine processes of bhakti to Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa; when such bhakti is offered to Viṣṇu (iti puṁsārpitā), Prahlāda considers it the topmost learning.

## Core explanation (facilitator must say)

Prahlāda lists nine processes of pure bhakti centered on Viṣṇu. Verse 24’s iti puṁsārpitā teaches that when this bhakti is offered to Viṣṇu, it is the highest learning. KUTUMBA families continue a core practice and gently add one underused process — never generic virtue with the Lord removed, and never panic mastery of all nine at once.

**Conclusion line:** Nine ways to love Viṣṇu — offered to Him, not as generic self-help.

**Common errors to block:**
1. Require mastering all nine at once.
2. Generic virtue without Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa as object.

## Analogies / examples with limits (teach inline)

### 1) Nine doors, one home
- **Say:** A home may have nine doors; you can enter through one and still be inside. Processes interconnect; panic-mastery of every door tonight is unnecessary.
- **Limit:** Pedagogy; still name the object of the home — Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa.
- **Younger:** Nine picture doors; walk through one.
- **Older/adult:** Which door is underused at home?
### 2) Letter without address
- **Say:** Virtue without Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa as object is like a kind letter with no address — good paper, wrong delivery for bhakti as Prahlāda defines it.
- **Limit:** Do not insult ordinary kindness; clarify bhakti’s object.
- **Younger:** Put a stamp/address to Kṛṣṇa on a paper heart.
- **Older/adult:** Rewrite a generic virtue as offered-to-Viṣṇu.
### 3) Toolbox again
- **Say:** Nine processes are a toolbox for lifelong devotion, not a one-night exam.
- **Limit:** No ranking toolkits between families.
- **Younger:** Pick one tool sticker.
- **Older/adult:** Core tool + underused tool plan.

## Three constructed household cases (week-specific)

### Constructed case 1 — All-nine panic
- **Situation:** A fictional parent prints a checklist and declares the family “fails” unless all nine are done daily starting tomorrow.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Master all nine at once.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Stations + one underused add; tradition allows focus.
- **Compassionate response:** Keep core practice; add one process once; release panic.
- **Family action:** Home minimum card.
- **What not to say:** Public failure ranking.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — one door. Older — triage worksheet.
### Constructed case 2 — Kindness without object
- **Situation:** A teen says bhakti is “just being a good person” with no Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa reference.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Generic virtue = nine processes.
- **Relevant principle / source:** ŚB 7.5.23–24 viṣṇoḥ / puṁsārpitā viṣṇau.
- **Compassionate response:** Affirm kindness; restore the Lord as object.
- **Family action:** Rewrite one practice as offered to Kṛṣṇa/Viṣṇu.
- **What not to say:** Mocking kindness. Ranking holiness.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — address the letter. Older — object audit.
### Constructed case 3 — Surrender scare
- **Situation:** Hearing ātma-nivedanam, a parent fears immediate sannyāsa pressure on a spouse.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Surrender process = abrupt family abandonment tonight.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Age-appropriate meaning: offering life to Viṣṇu’s service within responsible household scope for this cohort class.
- **Compassionate response:** Defer extreme applications; keep household bhakti practical.
- **Family action:** Choose a gentle process (hearing/service) if fear is high.
- **What not to say:** Pushing vows. Mocking fear.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — skip scare. Older — define classroom scope.

## Objective

Students observe both verses, list nine processes, explain iti puṁsārpitā, and choose core + underused process.

## Exact memory phrase

> Hearing, chanting, remembering, serving, worshiping, praying, serving as a servant, friendship and full surrender are processes of pure devotional service when offered to Viṣṇu.

**Short echo:** Nine ways to love Viṣṇu.

## Teacher background

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning:** Hearing, chanting, remembering, serving the Lord's feet, worship, prayer, servitude, friendship, and full surrender — nine processes of bhakti to Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa; when such bhakti is offered to Viṣṇu (iti puṁsārpitā), Prahlāda considers it the topmost learning. (Label as KUTUMBA teaching meaning — not as BBT translation.)
- **Supporting frames (one sentence each):** BG 9.14 overlap; object clarity; anti-panic pacing.
- **Block:** Require mastering all nine at once.; Generic virtue without Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa as object.
- **Scope:** No sannyāsa pressure; no ranking portfolios.

## Minute-by-minute plan

### 0–5 min — Settle and hook

Checklist panic vs offered bhakti — which matches Prahlāda?

### 5–14 min — Primary text observation

Read 23 list; read 24 iti puṁsārpitā; underline viṣṇoḥ / viṣṇau.

### 14–22 min — Worksheet questions

Students complete Section A of the older activity pack. Teacher circulates; use `OLDER-ANSWER-KEY.md` for expected directions (not for grading ranks).

### 22–28 min — Scenario cards (small groups)

A All-nine panic B Generic virtue C Surrender scare

Share one insight per group (60 seconds).

### 28–33 min — Puzzle / game: Object or Generic

O vs G claims.

### 33–37 min — Diagram / map task

Nine boxes; star core; circle underused.

### 37–40 min — Project sentence + handoff

Core+underused to parent.

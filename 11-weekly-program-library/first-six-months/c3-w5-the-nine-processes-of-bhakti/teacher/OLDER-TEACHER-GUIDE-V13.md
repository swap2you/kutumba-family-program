**KUTUMBA teaching meaning (not a BBT translation):**  
Hearing, chanting, remembering, serving the Lord's feet, worship, prayer, servitude, friendship, and full surrender — nine processes of bhakti; when such bhakti is offered to Viṣṇu, that is considered the topmost learning.

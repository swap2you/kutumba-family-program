## Objective

Children recognize nine picture processes, walk one door, and say nine ways to love Viṣṇu — offered to Him.

## Exact memory phrase

> Nine ways to love Viṣṇu.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** Hearing, chanting, remembering, serving the Lord's feet, worship, prayer, servitude, friendship, and full surrender — nine processes of bhakti to Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa; when such bhakti is offered to Viṣṇu (iti puṁsārpitā), Prahlāda considers it the topmost learning.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** There are nine ways to love Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa. We keep our main practice and try one more way with love.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**
  - “We must do all nine today.”
  - “Being nice is enough with no Kṛṣṇa.”
- **Do not:** invent deity dialogue; force confession; rank children; use scare tactics; claim temple certification stickers.

## Materials

- Nine picture cards
- Door mats
- Paper hearts
- Crayons
- Soft toss
- Calm corner
- Phrase stickers

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; calm corner with one chair for quiet reset. Keep aisles clear for movement.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome.
2. Echo: *Nine ways / to love / Viṣṇu.*
3. Show two pictures; say “to Kṛṣṇa.”
4. Say: “Not all nine today.”

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Prahlāda said the best learning is loving Viṣṇu in nine ways — hearing, chanting, remembering, serving, worshiping, praying, being a servant, being a friend, and giving ourselves to Him.
> We do not have to do every door today. We keep our main practice and try one more door with love for Kṛṣṇa.

**Story boundaries:** No scare surrender talk. No invented dialogues. No ranking.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. How many ways? (Nine)
2. To whom? (Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa)
3. All nine today? (No)

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: One-Door Walk (steps)

1. Nine cards around room.
2. Children walk to teacher-chosen one door; act a simple gesture.
3. Say phrase.
4. Return to circle.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Addressed heart (steps)

1. Paper heart.
2. Write/ helper: To Kṛṣṇa/Viṣṇu.
3. Draw one process picture.
4. Phrase on back.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet if available. If short on time, keep the craft as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind hands** — “Hands help; toys rest.”
- **Loud shout** — “Soft voice, please.”
- **Quiet reset** — “Calm corner for sixty seconds.”

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Materials away; craft in hand.
2. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:
   > Memory phrase: “Nine ways to love Viṣṇu..” Home cue: Continue the family's core practice and add one underused process once.
3. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

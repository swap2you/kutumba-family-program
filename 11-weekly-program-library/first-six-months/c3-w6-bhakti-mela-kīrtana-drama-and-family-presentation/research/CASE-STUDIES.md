# C3-W6 Case Studies

**Week:** Bhakti Mela: Kīrtana, Drama and Family Presentation
**Primary principle source:** Cycle 3 review (BG 5.29 · BG 7.7 · BG 4.34 · CC Antya 20.12 · ŚB 7.5.23–24) — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/7/5/23-24/
**Note:** All cases are fictional / anonymized teaching constructs for the founding cohort. Not reports about real cohort families.

---

### Constructed case 1 — Talent-show ranking (fictional)

- **Situation:** Adults whisper scores after family presentations; a shy family wants to leave.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Mela = ranking.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Offer it to Viṣṇu; no ranking.
- **Compassionate response:** Stop score talk; thank offerings; invite shy family to a tiny share or silent offering card.
- **Family action:** Baseline card without comparison.
- **What not to say:** Public critique. Comparing children.
- **Age adaptation:** Younger — offering plate. Older — rewrite rank talk.
### Constructed case 2 — Doctrine dump into off week (fictional)

- **Situation:** A facilitator plans to “finally teach everything else” during the protected off week instead of baseline.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Off week = new curriculum dump.
- **Relevant principle / source:** Protected off week / thirty-day baseline — no new doctrine dump.
- **Compassionate response:** Return to smallest daily practice plan.
- **Family action:** Write baseline only on the card.
- **What not to say:** Shaming facilitators mid-mela.
- **Age adaptation:** All — baseline star.
### Constructed case 3 — Generic holy-name drift at mela (fictional)

- **Situation:** Kīrtana station signs say only “Holy Name Corner” with no śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam.
- **Tempting mistaken conclusion:** Generic-only label is fine at review.
- **Relevant principle / source:** CC Antya 20.12 language rule carries into mela.
- **Compassionate response:** Rename station kindly; echo victory line.
- **Family action:** Phrase practice once.
- **What not to say:** Humiliation.
- **Age adaptation:** All ages echo full refrain once.

## Objective

Children celebrate soft śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam, make an offering-plate craft, and pick a tiny daily baseline star — without ranking.

## Exact memory phrase

> Offer it to Viṣṇu.

## Teacher background (2 minutes to review)

- **KUTUMBA teaching meaning (adult wording):** Bhakti Mela integrates Cycle 3 — celebrate hearing, śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana, and family presentation without ranking.
- **Child-facing paraphrase:** We offer our singing and learning to Viṣṇu. We practice a tiny bit every day. We do not rank friends.
- **Block misconceptions (child words):**
  - “Who was best?”
  - “Skip practice after the party.”
- **Do not:** invent deity dialogue; force confession; rank children; use scare tactics; claim temple certification stickers.

## Materials

- Paper plates
- Five pearl stickers
- Soft kīrtana guide
- Star stickers
- Crayons
- Calm corner
- Phrase poster

## Room setup

Circle on the floor; materials table behind you; calm corner with one chair for quiet reset. Keep aisles clear for movement.

---

## Minute-by-minute executable plan

### 0–5 min — Opening and recall

1. Welcome.
2. Echo: *Offer / it / to Viṣṇu.*
3. Soft clap.
4. Say: “We don’t rank friends.”

### 5–13 min — Story script (paraphrase only; ~8 minutes)

**Say exactly this spine (do not invent deity dialogue):**

> Tonight is a happy offering festival. We remember Kṛṣṇa owns and befriends, nothing is above Kṛṣṇa, we ask-serve-verify, we sing śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam, and we love Viṣṇu in many ways.
> We put our learning on an offering plate for Viṣṇu. Then we light a tiny daily practice for thirty days. We do not give prizes for who is best.

**Story boundaries:** Review themes only. No invented dialogues. No ranking games.

### 13–16 min — Three wonder questions

1. Do we rank? (No)
2. Who do we offer to? (Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa)
3. Tiny daily practice? (Yes)

Accept one- or two-word answers. Affirm effort.

### 16–24 min — Movement game: Offering Plate Parade (steps)

1. Hold paper plates.
2. Walk gently to “offer” mat; say phrase.
3. Soft echo of a short mantra line.
4. Sit; place a star sticker for baseline.

### 24–32 min — Craft: Offering plate + baseline star (steps)

1. Decorate paper plate.
2. Add five pearl stickers (review cues).
3. Helper writes: **Offer it to Viṣṇu.**
4. Star sticker: tiny daily practice picture.

Full printable steps also live in `activities/YOUNGER-ACTIVITY-PACK.md` (use together; this guide remains executable if the pack is missing).

### 32–35 min — Printable / coloring task

If time remains, color the younger line-art sheet if available. If short on time, keep the craft as the take-home.

### 35–38 min — Behavior redirects and quiet reset

Use calm, specific redirects:
- **Feet on floor** — “Feet visit the mat.”
- **Kind hands** — “Hands help; toys rest.”
- **Loud shout** — “Soft voice, please.”
- **Quiet reset** — “Calm corner for sixty seconds.”

### 38–40 min — Cleanup and parent handoff

1. Materials away; craft in hand.
2. At reunification, tell parent in one sentence:
   > Memory phrase: “Offer it to Viṣṇu..” Home cue: Begin the thirty-day baseline with the smallest daily practice.
3. Do not report private behavior struggles in front of other families.

## Behavior redirects (quick reference)

| Signal | Teacher line | Child action |
|---|---|---|
| Running | Walking feet. | Walk to mat |
| Snatching | Kind hands. | Hands to lap |
| Interrupt shout | Soft voice. | Whisper retry |
| Shutdown | Quiet reset is okay. | Calm corner 60 sec |

# C3-W6 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

**Theme:** Bhakti Mela: Kīrtana, Drama and Family Presentation
**Primary:** Cycle 3 review (BG 5.29 · BG 7.7 · BG 4.34 · CC Antya 20.12 · ŚB 7.5.23–24) — https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/7/5/23-24/
**Memory phrase:** Offer it to Viṣṇu.

---

## Objective

Children celebrate soft śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam, make an offering-plate craft, and pick a tiny daily baseline star — without ranking.

## Core activity — Offering Plate Parade

**Time:** 5–8 minutes

1. Hold paper plates.
2. Walk gently to “offer” mat; say phrase.
3. Soft echo of a short mantra line.
4. Sit; place a star sticker for baseline.

## Story boundary

Review themes only. No invented dialogues. No ranking games.

## Craft — Offering plate + baseline star (step-by-step)

1. Decorate paper plate.
2. Add five pearl stickers (review cues).
3. Helper writes: **Offer it to Viṣṇu.**
4. Star sticker: tiny daily practice picture.

## Printable

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` on US Letter if present. If missing, children color their craft only.

## Backup (low prep)

Toss and say “Offer it to Viṣṇu.”

## Materials

- Paper plates
- Five pearl stickers
- Soft kīrtana guide
- Star stickers
- Crayons
- Calm corner
- Phrase poster

## Parent note (say at handoff)

Home practice: Begin the thirty-day baseline with the smallest daily practice.. Snack/water only at the Saturday program; no weekly meal. We do not rank children.

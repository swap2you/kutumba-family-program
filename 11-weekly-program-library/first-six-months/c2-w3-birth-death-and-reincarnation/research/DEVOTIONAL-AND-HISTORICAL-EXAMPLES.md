# C2-W3 Devotional and Historical Examples

## Example 1 — Chapter 2 battlefield teaching context (traceable)

- **Source range:** Bhagavad-gītā Chapter 2, especially BG 2.13–2.27 cluster with primary BG 2.22  
- **URLs:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/22/ · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/  
- **Classroom paraphrase:** Kṛṣṇa instructs Arjuna that the embodied self continues while bodies change, so bewilderment based only on bodily endings is misplaced.  
- **Teaching use:** Model calm ontological clarity under pressure — without graphic war detail for children and without invented dialogue.  
- **Limitation:** Our living room is not Kurukṣetra; we borrow teaching intent, not battle atmosphere.

## Example 2 — Remembrance emphasis (traceable)

- **Source range:** BG 8.5–8.6  
- **URLs:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/5/ · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/8/6/  
- **Classroom paraphrase:** The consciousness remembered at life’s end shapes direction; remembering the Lord at the end points toward the Lord. Therefore families practice short, calm remembrance now.  
- **Teaching use:** Ground the Bedtime Remembrance Routine without claiming we can read anyone’s posthumous destination.  
- **Limitation:** No diagnosis of deceased relatives; no fear-based “last-thought” scare talks for children.

## Research decision — examples not selected this week

Extended narratives that require graphic death detail, ghost folklore, or speculative past-life identity claims are **not** used. Prefer primary verse + the two sourced emphases above + constructed family cases.

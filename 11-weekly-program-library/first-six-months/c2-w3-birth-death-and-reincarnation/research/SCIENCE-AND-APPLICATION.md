# C2-W3 Science and Application

## Decision: Option B — N/A for doctrinal proof

**N/A — no empirical claim is needed for this week's doctrinal conclusion.**

### Reason

Transmigration of the embodied self (BG 2.22) is a **śāstra conclusion**. Laboratory methods measure bodies and behavior; they do not establish ātman, rebirth, or posthumous destination. KUTUMBA therefore does not cite science as proof of reincarnation this week.

### Pedagogy note (not proof)

Developmental and clinical literature on how adults talk with children about death may inform *tone* (calm, honest, age-appropriate, non-coercive). Such literature may help facilitators avoid scare pedagogy. It still **does not prove** transmigration and must never be framed as metaphysical evidence.

### Application boundary

- Use calm-speech pedagogy only.
- Do not say “research proves we reincarnate.”
- Do not say “research disproves we reincarnate.”
- Doctrine and hope rest on śāstra; science stays in the speech-habits lane if mentioned at all.

# C2-W3 Younger Activity Pack (K–2)

## Objective

Show and say: **Birth and death are changes of dress for the eternal self** — calmly, without fear.

## 1. Core circle activity — Doll clothes change (5–8 min)

1. Teacher demonstrates once with the week object: doll (or paper doll) + two outfits/paper shirts.
2. Say: “Clothes change. Friend stays.” Children echo.
3. Every child gets one turn to help hold a shirt or say the echo — or partner turn if the group is large.
4. End with memory phrase echo (two rounds max).
5. Hard stop: no “dying,” no funerals, no animal-body guessing games.

## 2. Story boundary

Use the story in `teacher/YOUNGER-TEACHER-GUIDE.md`.  
Paraphrase only. No invented deity dialogue. No graphic violence. No scare tactics. No ghost stories.

## 3. Movement — Soft Clothes Walk — C2-W3

Walk gently with a soft scarf → pause on “Change!” → fold scarf to heart → finish memory phrase → sit.

## 4. Craft / object (actual)

**Clothes-card:**  
1. Fold card stock in half.  
2. Outside: draw a simple shirt or glue a tiny fabric scrap.  
3. Inside: write or sticker — *Birth and death are changes of dress for the eternal self.*  
4. Optional: draw a small moon for “bedtime remembrance.”

Take-home: use the card before the one-minute bedtime practice.

## 5. Memory card

- Front: simple shirt icon for C2-W3  
- Back: Birth and death are changes of dress for the eternal self.

## 6. Printable coloring

Color `../visuals/V12/line-art-younger.svg` (US Letter). Prompt: color a calm scene that shows clothes changing and a peaceful heart — no graves, no ghosts.

## 7. Take-home family cue

Ask at home: How do we speak calmly about birth and death? (one soft sentence each)  
Plus: one-minute bedtime remembrance on three nights.

## Backup low-prep

Pass soft scarf; each child says one kind word; echo memory phrase.

## Materials

Printed line art, crayons, card stock, soft scarf, doll or paper doll, two outfits/paper shirts, tape, optional fabric scrap.

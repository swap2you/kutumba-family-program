**Primary:** BG 2.22 — https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/22/

**Devanāgarī:** वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि । तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णान्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही ॥ २२ ॥

**IAST:** `vāsāṁsi jīrṇāni yathā vihāya navāni gṛhṇāti naro ’parāṇi / tathā śarīrāṇi vihāya jīrṇāny anyāni saṁyāti navāni dehī`

**KUTUMBA teaching meaning (not a BBT translation):**  
As a person puts on new garments, giving up old ones, the embodied self similarly accepts new bodies, giving up old ones — taught calmly, without fear tactics for children.
